# 1. 摘要

We present WiscKey, a persistent LSM-tree-based key-value store with a performance-oriented data layout that separates keys from values to minimize I/O amplification. The design of WiscKey is highly SSD optimized, leveraging both the sequential and random performance characteristics of the device. We demonstrate the advantages of WiscKey with both microbenchmarks and YCSB workloads. Microbenchmark results show that WiscKey is 2.5×-111× faster than LevelDB for loading a database and 1.6×-14× faster for random lookups. WiscKey is faster than both LevelDB and RocksDB in all six YCSB workloads.

我们提出 WiscKey, 一种基于 LSM-tree 的持久化键值存储. 它采用面向性能的数据布局, 将键与值分开存储, 以尽量降低 I/O 放大. WiscKey 针对 SSD 进行了充分优化, 同时利用设备的顺序访问和随机访问特性. 我们通过微基准测试和 YCSB 工作负载展示其优势. 微基准测试表明, WiscKey 加载数据库的速度是 LevelDB 的 2.5-111 倍, 随机查找的速度是其 1.6-14 倍. 在全部六种 YCSB 工作负载中, WiscKey 均快于 LevelDB 和 RocksDB.

# 2. 引言

Persistent key-value stores play a critical role in a variety of modern data-intensive applications, including web indexing [16, 48], e-commerce [24], data deduplication [7, 22], photo stores [12], cloud data [32], social networking [9, 25, 51], online gaming [23], messaging [1, 29], software repository [2] and advertising [20]. By enabling efficient insertions, point lookups, and range queries, key-value stores serve as the foundation for this growing group of important applications.

持久化键值存储在多种现代数据密集型应用中发挥着关键作用, 包括网页索引 [16, 48], 电子商务 [24], 数据去重 [7, 22], 照片存储 [12], 云端数据 [32], 社交网络 [9, 25, 51], 在线游戏 [23], 消息服务 [1, 29], 软件仓库 [2] 和广告系统 [20]. 键值存储能够高效地完成插入, 点查和范围查询, 因而成为这些重要应用的基础, 其应用范围也在不断扩大.

For write-intensive workloads, key-value stores based on Log-Structured Merge-Trees (LSM-trees) [43] have become the state of the art. Various distributed and local stores built on LSM-trees are widely deployed in large-scale production environments, such as BigTable [16] and LevelDB [48] at Google, Cassandra [33], HBase [29] and RocksDB [25] at Facebook, PNUTS [20] at `Yahoo!`, and Riak [4] at Basho. The main advantage of LSM-trees over other indexing structures (such as B-trees) is that they maintain sequential access patterns for writes. Small updates on B-trees may involve many random writes, and are hence not efficient on either solid-state storage devices or hard-disk drives.

对于写密集型负载, 基于日志结构合并树 (Log-Structured Merge-Tree, LSM-tree) [43] 的键值存储已成为先进的实现方案. 各种基于 LSM-tree 的分布式和本地存储系统广泛部署于大规模生产环境, 例如 Google 的 BigTable [16] 和 LevelDB [48], Facebook 的 Cassandra [33], HBase [29] 和 RocksDB [25], Yahoo! 的 PNUTS [20], 以及 Basho 的 Riak [4]. 相比 B-tree 等索引结构, LSM-tree 的主要优势在于能够保持顺序写入. B-tree 中的小规模更新可能引发大量随机写, 无论在固态存储设备还是机械硬盘上, 效率都不高.

To deliver high write performance, LSM-trees batch key-value pairs and write them sequentially. Subsequently, to enable efficient lookups (for both individual keys as well as range queries), LSM-trees continuously read, sort, and write key-value pairs in the background, thus maintaining keys and values in sorted order. As a result, the same data is read and written multiple times throughout its lifetime; as we show later (§2), this I/O amplification in typical LSM-trees can reach a factor of 50x or higher [39, 54].

为获得较高的写入性能, LSM-tree 将键值对攒成批次后顺序写入. 随后, 为了高效完成单键查找和范围查询, 它会在后台持续读取, 排序并重写键值对, 使键和值保持有序. 因此, 同一份数据在其生命周期内会被反复读写. 后文将说明, 典型 LSM-tree 的这种 I/O 放大可达 50 倍甚至更高 [39, 54] (原文第 2 节).

The success of LSM-based technology is tied closely to its usage upon classic hard-disk drives (HDDs). In HDDs, random I/Os are over 100× slower than sequential ones [43]; thus, performing additional sequential reads and writes to continually sort keys and enable efficient lookups represents an excellent trade-off.

基于 LSM 的技术之所以成功, 与其运行在传统机械硬盘 (HDD) 上密切相关. HDD 的随机 I/O 比顺序 I/O 慢 100 倍以上 [43]. 因而, 通过额外的顺序读写持续对键排序, 以换取高效查找, 是一种很划算的取舍.

However, the storage landscape is quickly changing, and modern solid-state storage devices (SSDs) are supplanting HDDs in many important use cases. As compared to HDDs, SSDs are fundamentally different in their performance and reliability characteristics; when considering key-value storage system design, we believe the following three differences are of paramount importance. First, the difference between random and sequential performance is not nearly as large as with HDDs; thus, an LSM-tree that performs a large number of sequential I/Os to reduce later random I/Os may be wasting bandwidth needlessly. Second, SSDs have a large degree of internal parallelism; an LSM built atop an SSD must be carefully designed to harness said parallelism [53]. Third, SSDs can wear out through repeated writes [34, 40]; the high write amplification in LSM-trees can significantly reduce device lifetime. As we will show in the paper (§4), the combination of these factors greatly impacts LSM-tree performance on SSDs, reducing throughput by 90% and increasing write load by a factor over 10. While replacing an HDD with an SSD underneath an LSM-tree does improve performance, with current LSM-tree technology, the SSD's true potential goes largely unrealized.

然而, 存储硬件正在迅速变化, 固态存储设备 (SSD) 正在许多重要场景中取代 HDD. 两者的性能和可靠性特征有本质差异. 在设计键值存储系统时, 我们认为以下三点尤其重要. 第一, SSD 随机访问与顺序访问的性能差距远小于 HDD. 因此, LSM-tree 为减少后续随机 I/O 而执行大量顺序 I/O, 可能是在无谓地浪费带宽. 第二, SSD 内部具有很高的并行度, 运行在 SSD 上的 LSM 实现需要精心设计, 才能利用这种并行能力 [53]. 第三, 反复写入会磨损 SSD [34, 40], LSM-tree 的高写放大可能显著缩短设备寿命. 后文的实验将表明, 这些因素共同严重影响了 LSM-tree 在 SSD 上的性能, 使吞吐量降低 90%, 写入量增至 10 倍以上 (原文第 4 节). 虽然将 LSM-tree 底层的 HDD 换成 SSD 确实能提高性能, 但现有 LSM-tree 技术仍未充分发挥 SSD 的潜力.

In this paper, we present WiscKey, an SSD-conscious persistent key-value store derived from the popular LSM-tree implementation, LevelDB. The central idea behind WiscKey is the separation of keys and values [42]; only keys are kept sorted in the LSM-tree, while values are stored separately in a log. In other words, we decouple key sorting and garbage collection in WiscKey while LevelDB bundles them together. This simple technique can significantly reduce write amplification by avoiding the unnecessary movement of values while sorting. Furthermore, the size of the LSM-tree is noticeably decreased, leading to fewer device reads and better caching during lookups. WiscKey retains the benefits of LSM-tree technology, including excellent insert and lookup performance, but without excessive I/O amplification.

本文提出 WiscKey, 一种面向 SSD 设计的持久化键值存储, 基于广泛使用的 LSM-tree 实现 LevelDB 开发. WiscKey 的核心思想是键值分离 [42]: LSM-tree 只对键排序, 值则单独保存在日志中. 换言之, WiscKey 将键排序与垃圾回收解耦, 而 LevelDB 将两者绑定在一起. 这一简单方法避免了排序时不必要的值搬移, 可以显著降低写放大. 同时, LSM-tree 的体积大幅缩小, 查找所需的设备读取更少, 缓存效果也更好. WiscKey 保留了 LSM-tree 插入和查找性能出色的优势, 同时避免了过高的 I/O 放大.

Separating keys from values introduces a number of challenges and optimization opportunities. First, range query (scan) performance may be affected because values are not stored in sorted order anymore. WiscKey solves this challenge by using the abundant internal parallelism of SSD devices. Second, WiscKey needs garbage collection to reclaim the free space used by invalid values. WiscKey proposes an online and lightweight garbage collector which only involves sequential I/Os and impacts the foreground workload minimally. Third, separating keys and values makes crash consistency challenging; WiscKey leverages an interesting property in modern file systems, that appends never result in garbage data on a crash. WiscKey optimizes performance while providing the same consistency guarantees as found in modern LSM-based systems.

键值分离也带来了一系列挑战和优化机会. 首先, 值不再按键排序存储, 可能影响范围查询 (扫描) 的性能. WiscKey 利用 SSD 内部丰富的并行能力解决这一问题. 其次, WiscKey 需要通过垃圾回收释放失效值占用的空间. 为此, 我们设计了轻量级在线垃圾回收器, 其数据搬移只涉及顺序 I/O, 对前台负载的影响很小. 第三, 键值分离增加了崩溃一致性的实现难度. WiscKey 利用了现代文件系统的一项特性: 追加写入在崩溃后不会产生垃圾数据. 因此, 它能在优化性能的同时, 提供与现代 LSM 系统相同的一致性保证.

We compare the performance of WiscKey with LevelDB [48] and RocksDB [25], two popular LSM-tree key-value stores. For most workloads, WiscKey performs significantly better. With LevelDB's own microbenchmark, WiscKey is 2.5×-111× faster than LevelDB for loading a database, depending on the size of the key-value pairs; for random lookups, WiscKey is 1.6×-14× faster than LevelDB. WiscKey's performance is not always better than standard LSM-trees; if small values are written in random order, and a large dataset is range-queried sequentially, WiscKey performs worse than LevelDB. However, this workload does not reflect real-world use cases (which primarily use shorter range queries) and can be improved by log reorganization. Under YCSB macrobenchmarks [21] that reflect real-world use cases, WiscKey is faster than both LevelDB and RocksDB in all six YCSB workloads, and follows a trend similar to the load and random lookup microbenchmarks.

我们将 WiscKey 与两种常见的 LSM-tree 键值存储 LevelDB [48] 和 RocksDB [25] 进行了比较. 在多数负载下, WiscKey 的性能显著更好. 使用 LevelDB 自带的微基准测试时, 随键值对大小不同, WiscKey 加载数据库的速度是 LevelDB 的 2.5-111 倍; 随机查找速度是其 1.6-14 倍. 但 WiscKey 并非总优于标准 LSM-tree: 如果先按随机顺序写入较小的值, 再顺序扫描一个很大的数据范围, 它会慢于 LevelDB. 不过, 这种负载不能代表主要使用短范围查询的实际场景, 而且可以通过重组日志来改善. 在反映实际使用场景的 YCSB 综合基准测试 [21] 中, WiscKey 在全部六种负载下均快于 LevelDB 和 RocksDB, 整体趋势与加载和随机查找微基准测试相似.

The rest of the paper is organized as follows. We first describe the background and motivation in Section 2. Section 3 explains the design of WiscKey, and Section 4 analyzes its performance. We briefly describe related work in Section 5, and conclude in Section 6.

# 3. 背景与动机

In this section, we first describe the concept of a Log-Structured Merge-tree (LSM-tree). Then, we explain the design of LevelDB, a popular key-value store based on LSM-tree technology. We investigate read and write amplification in LevelDB. Finally, we describe the characteristics of modern storage hardware.

本节先介绍日志结构合并树 (LSM-tree) 的概念, 再说明基于这一技术的常用键值存储 LevelDB 的设计, 并分析它的读放大与写放大. 最后, 我们讨论现代存储硬件的特征.

## 3.1 日志结构合并树

An LSM-tree is a persistent structure that provides efficient indexing for a key-value store with a high rate of inserts and deletes [43]. It defers and batches data writes into large chunks to use the high sequential bandwidth of hard drives. Since random writes are nearly two orders of magnitude slower than sequential writes on hard drives, LSM-trees provide better write performance than traditional B-trees, which require random accesses.

LSM-tree 是一种持久化数据结构, 能为插入和删除频繁的键值存储提供高效索引 [43]. 它将写入延后并汇聚成大块, 以利用机械硬盘较高的顺序带宽. 由于机械硬盘的随机写比顺序写慢近两个数量级, LSM-tree 的写入性能优于需要随机访问的传统 B-tree.

An LSM-tree consists of a number of components of exponentially increasing sizes, C0 to Ck, as shown in Figure 1. The C0 component is a memory-resident update-in-place sorted tree, while the other components C1 to Ck are disk-resident append-only B-trees.

如图 1 所示, LSM-tree 由 C0 到 Ck 等多个组成部分构成, 它们的容量呈指数增长. C0 是驻留内存, 支持原地更新的有序树; C1 到 Ck 则是驻留磁盘, 仅追加写入的 B-tree.

During an insert in an LSM-tree, the inserted key-value pair is appended to an on-disk sequential log file, so as to enable recovery in case of a crash. Then, the key-value pair is added to the in-memory C0, which is sorted by keys; C0 allows efficient lookups and scans on recently inserted key-value pairs. Once C0 reaches its size limit, it will be merged with the on-disk C1 in an approach similar to merge sort; this process is known as compaction. The newly merged tree will be written to disk sequentially, replacing the old version of C1. Compaction (`i.e.`, merge sorting) also happens for on-disk components, when each Ci reaches its size limit. Note that compactions are only performed between adjacent levels (Ci and Ci+1), and they can be executed asynchronously in the background.

插入键值对时, LSM-tree 先将其追加到磁盘上的顺序日志文件, 以便崩溃后恢复, 再将其加入按键排序的内存结构 C0. C0 能高效查找和扫描最近插入的键值对. 当 C0 达到容量上限时, 系统采用类似归并排序的方法, 将它与磁盘上的 C1 合并, 这个过程称为 compaction. 合并后的新树顺序写入磁盘, 替换旧的 C1. 磁盘上的 Ci 达到容量上限后, 也会触发 compaction, 即归并排序. compaction 只发生在相邻层 Ci 和 Ci+1 之间, 可以在后台异步执行.

To serve a lookup operation, LSM-trees may need to search multiple components. Note that C0 contains the freshest data, followed by C1, and so on. Therefore, to retrieve a key-value pair, the LSM-tree searches components starting from C0 in a cascading fashion until it locates the desired data in the smallest component Ci. Compared with B-trees, LSM-trees may need multiple reads for a point lookup. Hence, LSM-trees are most useful when inserts are more common than lookups [43].

处理一次查找时, LSM-tree 可能需要搜索多个组成部分. C0 包含最新数据, 其次是 C1, 依此类推. 因此, 查找键值对时, 系统从 C0 开始逐层搜索, 直到在包含目标数据的最小组成部分 Ci 中找到它. 相比 B-tree, LSM-tree 的一次点查可能需要多次读取. 因而, LSM-tree 最适合插入比查找更频繁的场景 [43].

## 3.2 LevelDB

LevelDB is a widely used key-value store based on LSM-trees that is inspired by BigTable [16, 48]. LevelDB supports range queries, snapshots, and other features that are useful in modern applications. In this section, we briefly describe the core design of LevelDB.

LevelDB 是一种广泛使用的 LSM-tree 键值存储, 设计受到 BigTable 的启发 [16, 48]. 它支持范围查询, 快照等适用于现代应用的功能. 本节简要介绍 LevelDB 的核心设计.

The overall architecture of LevelDB is shown in Figure 1. The main data structures in LevelDB are an on-disk log file, two in-memory sorted skiplists (memtable and immutable memtable), and seven levels (L0 to L6) of on-disk Sorted String Table (SSTable) files. LevelDB initially stores inserted key-value pairs in a log file and the in-memory memtable. Once the memtable is full, LevelDB switches to a new memtable and log file to handle further inserts from the user. In the background, the previous memtable is converted into an immutable memtable, and a compaction thread then flushes it to the disk, generating a new SSTable file (about 2 MB usually) at level 0 (L0); the previous log file is discarded.

图 1 展示了 LevelDB 的整体架构. 主要数据结构包括磁盘日志文件, 两个内存有序跳表 (memtable 和 immutable memtable), 以及磁盘上从 L0 到 L6 共七层有序字符串表 (Sorted String Table, SSTable) 文件. LevelDB 最初将插入的键值对写入日志文件和内存 memtable. memtable 写满后, 它切换到新的 memtable 和日志文件, 继续处理用户插入. 原有 memtable 在后台转为 immutable memtable, 再由 compaction 线程刷入磁盘, 在 L0 生成一个通常约为 2 MB 的 SSTable 文件; 原有日志随后被丢弃.

The size of all files in each level is limited, and increases by a factor of ten with the level number. For example, the size limit of all files at L1 is 10 MB, while the limit of L2 is 100 MB. To maintain the size limit, once the total size of a level Li exceeds its limit, the compaction thread will choose one file from Li, merge sort with all the overlapped files of Li+1, and generate new Li+1 SSTable files. The compaction thread continues until all levels are within their size limits. Also, during compaction, LevelDB ensures that all files in a particular level, except L0, do not overlap in their key-ranges; keys in files of L0 can overlap with each other since they are directly flushed from memtable.

每层文件的总大小都有上限, 且每加深一层, 上限增至前一层的 10 倍. 例如, L1 的总容量上限为 10 MB, L2 为 100 MB. 当 Li 的总大小超过上限时, compaction 线程从 Li 选择一个文件, 将它与 Li+1 中所有键范围重叠的文件归并排序, 生成新的 Li+1 SSTable 文件. 这一过程持续到所有层都满足容量限制. 此外, LevelDB 在 compaction 时保证除 L0 外, 同一层各文件的键范围互不重叠. L0 文件直接由 memtable 刷盘生成, 因而键范围可能重叠.

To serve a lookup operation, LevelDB searches the memtable first, immutable memtable next, and then files L0 to L6 in order. The number of file searches required to locate a random key is bounded by the maximum number of levels, since keys do not overlap between files within a single level, except in L0. Since files in L0 can contain overlapping keys, a lookup may search multiple files at L0. To avoid a large lookup latency, LevelDB slows down the foreground write traffic if the number of files at L0 is bigger than eight, in order to wait for the compaction thread to compact some files from L0 to L1.

查找时, LevelDB 先搜索 memtable, 再搜索 immutable memtable, 然后依次搜索 L0 到 L6 的文件. 除 L0 外, 同一层文件的键范围互不重叠, 因而定位一个随机键所需搜索的文件数量受到层数限制. L0 文件可能包含重叠的键, 一次查找可能需要检查该层多个文件. 为避免查找延迟过高, 当 L0 文件数超过 8 个时, LevelDB 会放慢前台写入, 等待 compaction 线程将部分文件从 L0 合并到 L1.

## 3.3 写放大与读放大

Write and read amplification are major problems in LSM-trees such as LevelDB. Write (read) amplification is defined as the ratio between the amount of data written to (read from) the underlying storage device and the amount of data requested by the user. In this section, we analyze the write and read amplification in LevelDB.

对于 LevelDB 这类 LSM-tree, 写放大和读放大是主要问题. 写放大 (读放大) 定义为实际写入底层存储设备 (从设备读取) 的数据量与用户请求数据量之比. 本节分析 LevelDB 的写放大和读放大.

To achieve mostly-sequential disk access, LevelDB writes more data than necessary (although still sequentially), `i.e.`, LevelDB has high write amplification. Since the size limit of Li is 10 times that of Li-1, when merging a file from Li-1 to Li during compaction, LevelDB may read up to 10 files from Li in the worst case, and write back these files to Li after sorting. Therefore, the write amplification of moving a file across two levels can be up to 10. For a large dataset, since any newly generated table file can eventually migrate from L0 to L6 through a series of compaction steps, write amplification can be over 50 (10 for each gap between L1 to L6).

为使磁盘访问尽量保持顺序, LevelDB 会写入超出实际需要的数据量, 即使这些写入仍是顺序的, 也会造成较高的写放大. Li 的容量上限是 Li-1 的 10 倍, 因而 compaction 将一个文件从 Li-1 合并到 Li 时, 最坏需要读取 Li 中的 10 个文件, 排序后再写回 Li. 因此, 文件跨越相邻两层的写放大可达 10. 对于大数据集, 新生成的表文件最终可能经过一系列 compaction, 从 L0 迁移到 L6, 写放大可超过 50 (L1 到 L6 之间每次跨层为 10).

Read amplification has been a major problem for LSM-trees due to trade-offs made in the design. There are two sources of read amplification in LevelDB. First, to lookup a key-value pair, LevelDB may need to check multiple levels. In the worst case, LevelDB needs to check eight files in L0, and one file for each of the remaining six levels: a total of 14 files. Second, to find a key-value pair within a SSTable file, LevelDB needs to read multiple metadata blocks within the file. Specifically, the amount of data actually read is given by (index block + bloom-filter blocks + data block). For example, to lookup a 1-KB key-value pair, LevelDB needs to read a 16-KB index block, a 4-KB bloom-filter block, and a 4-KB data block; in total, 24 KB. Therefore, considering the 14 SSTable files in the worst case, the read amplification of LevelDB is 24 × 14 = 336. Smaller key-value pairs will lead to an even higher read amplification.

LSM-tree 的设计取舍也使读放大成为一个主要问题. LevelDB 的读放大来自两个方面. 首先, 查找一个键值对可能需要检查多层文件. 最坏情况下, 需要检查 L0 中的 8 个文件, 以及其余六层各 1 个文件, 共 14 个. 其次, 在一个 SSTable 文件内定位键值对, 还需读取多个元数据块. 实际读取量等于索引块, Bloom filter 块和数据块之和. 例如, 查找一个 1 KB 的键值对, 需要读取 16 KB 的索引块, 4 KB 的 Bloom filter 块和 4 KB 的数据块, 合计 24 KB. 若最坏情况下需检查 14 个 SSTable 文件, 读放大就是 24 × 14 = 336. 键值对越小, 读放大还会更高.

To measure the amount of amplification seen in practice with LevelDB, we perform the following experiment. We first load a database with 1-KB key-value pairs, and then lookup 100,000 entries from the database; we use two different database sizes for the initial load, and choose keys randomly from a uniform distribution. Figure 2 shows write amplification during the load phase and read amplification during the lookup phase. For a 1-GB database, write amplification is 3.1, while for a 100-GB database, write amplification increases to 14. Read amplification follows the same trend: 8.2 for the 1-GB database and 327 for the 100-GB database. The reason write amplification increases with database size is straightforward. With more data inserted into a database, the key-value pairs will more likely travel further along the levels; in other words, LevelDB will write data many times when compacting from low levels to high levels. However, write amplification does not reach the worst-case predicted previously, since the average number of files merged between levels is usually smaller than the worst case of 10. Read amplification also increases with the dataset size, since for a small database, all the index blocks and bloom filters in SSTable files can be cached in memory. However, for a large database, each lookup may touch a different SSTable file, paying the cost of reading index blocks and bloom filters each time.

为测量 LevelDB 实际的放大程度, 我们先将 1 KB 的键值对加载到数据库, 再随机查找 100,000 条记录. 实验采用两种初始数据库大小, 查询键服从均匀分布. 图 2 给出了加载阶段的写放大和查找阶段的读放大. 数据库为 1 GB 时, 写放大为 3.1; 增至 100 GB 时, 写放大升至 14. 读放大也呈现相同趋势, 分别为 8.2 和 327. 写放大随数据库增大而上升的原因很直接: 数据越多, 键值对就越可能迁移到更深的层次, 在从低层向高层 compaction 的过程中被多次写入. 不过, 实际写放大没有达到前述最坏值, 因为跨层合并的平均文件数通常小于最坏情况下的 10 个. 读放大也随数据集增大而上升: 小数据库的 SSTable 索引块和 Bloom filter 可以全部缓存在内存中; 大数据库的每次查找却可能访问不同的 SSTable, 每次都要付出读取这些元数据的代价.

It should be noted that the high write and read amplifications are a justified tradeoff for hard drives. As an example, for a given hard drive with a 10-ms seek latency and a 100-MB/s throughput, the approximate time required to access a random 1K of data is 10 ms, while that for the next sequential block is about 10 µs - the ratio between random and sequential latency is 1000:1. Hence, compared to alternative data structures such as B-Trees that require random write accesses, a sequential-write-only scheme with write amplification less than 1000 will be faster on a hard drive [43, 49]. On the other hand, the read amplification for LSM-trees is still comparable to B-Trees. For example, considering a B-Tree with a height of five and a block size of 4 KB, a random lookup for a 1-KB key-value pair would require accessing six blocks, resulting in a read amplification of 24.

需要指出, 对于机械硬盘, 较高的写放大和读放大是合理的取舍. 例如, 对于寻道延迟 10 ms, 吞吐量 100 MB/s 的硬盘, 随机访问 1 KB 数据约需 10 ms, 而访问紧接着的顺序块约需 10 µs, 两者的延迟比为 1000:1. 因此, 相比 B-tree 等需要随机写入的数据结构, 只要写放大小于 1000, 纯顺序写方案在机械硬盘上仍然更快 [43, 49]. 另一方面, LSM-tree 的读放大仍与 B-tree 相当. 例如, 对于高度为 5, 块大小为 4 KB 的 B-tree, 随机查找一个 1 KB 键值对需要访问 6 个块, 读放大为 24.

## 3.4 高速存储硬件

Many modern servers adopt SSD devices to achieve high performance. Similar to hard drives, random writes are considered harmful also in SSDs [10, 31, 34, 40] due to their unique erase-write cycle and expensive garbage collection. Although initial random-write performance for SSD devices is good, the performance can significantly drop after the reserved blocks are utilized. The LSM-tree characteristic of avoiding random writes is hence a natural fit for SSDs; many SSD-optimized key-value stores are based on LSM-trees [25, 50, 53, 54].

许多现代服务器采用 SSD 来提高性能. 与机械硬盘类似, 随机写也会损害 SSD 的性能 [10, 31, 34, 40], 原因是其特有的擦除再写入机制, 以及代价较高的垃圾回收. SSD 初始的随机写性能虽然不错, 但预留块耗尽后, 性能可能明显下降. 因此, LSM-tree 避免随机写的特点很适合 SSD, 许多针对 SSD 优化的键值存储都采用了 LSM-tree [25, 50, 53, 54].

However, unlike hard-drives, the relative performance of random reads (compared to sequential reads) is significantly better on SSDs; furthermore, when random reads are issued concurrently in an SSD, the aggregate throughput can match sequential throughput for some workloads [17]. As an example, Figure 3 shows the sequential and random read performance of a 500-GB Samsung 840 EVO SSD, for various request sizes. For random reads by a single thread, the throughput increases with the request size, reaching half the sequential throughput for 256 KB. With concurrent random reads by 32 threads, the aggregate throughput matches sequential throughput when the size is larger than 16 KB. For more high-end SSDs, the gap between concurrent random reads and sequential reads is much smaller [3, 39].

但与机械硬盘不同, SSD 的随机读性能与顺序读更为接近. 而且, 并发发起随机读时, 部分负载下的总吞吐量可以达到顺序读水平 [17]. 图 3 以 500 GB 的 Samsung 840 EVO SSD 为例, 展示了不同请求大小下的顺序读与随机读性能. 单线程随机读的吞吐量随请求增大而提高, 请求为 256 KB 时达到顺序读的一半. 使用 32 个线程并发随机读时, 请求大于 16 KB 后, 总吞吐量即可达到顺序读水平. 在更高端的 SSD 上, 并发随机读与顺序读之间的差距还要小得多 [3, 39].

As we showed in this section, LSM-trees have a high write and read amplification, which is acceptable for hard drives. Using LSM-trees on a high-performance SSD may waste a large percentage of device bandwidth with excessive writing and reading. In this paper, our goal is to improve the performance of LSM-trees on SSD devices to efficiently exploit device bandwidth.

本节表明, LSM-tree 的读写放大较高, 但对机械硬盘而言可以接受. 如果直接用于高性能 SSD, 过多的读写却可能浪费设备的大量带宽. 本文的目标是提高 LSM-tree 在 SSD 上的性能, 更有效地利用设备带宽.

# 4. WiscKey

The previous section explained how LSM-trees maintain sequential I/O access by increasing I/O amplification. While this trade-off between sequential I/O access and I/O amplification is justified for traditional hard disks, they are not optimal for modern hardware utilizing SSDs. In this section, we present the design of WiscKey, a key-value store that minimizes I/O amplification on SSDs.

上一节说明了 LSM-tree 如何通过增加 I/O 放大来保持顺序访问. 对传统机械硬盘来说, 这种取舍合理, 但对采用 SSD 的现代硬件并非最优. 本节介绍 WiscKey 的设计, 它是一种旨在尽量降低 SSD 上 I/O 放大的键值存储.

To realize an SSD-optimized key-value store, WiscKey includes four critical ideas. First, WiscKey separates keys from values, keeping only keys in the LSM-tree and the values in a separate log file. Second, to deal with unsorted values (which necessitate random access during range queries), WiscKey uses the parallel random-read characteristic of SSD devices. Third, WiscKey utilizes unique crash-consistency and garbage-collection techniques to efficiently manage the value log. Finally, WiscKey optimizes performance by removing the LSM-tree log without sacrificing consistency, thus reducing system-call overhead from small writes.

为实现针对 SSD 优化的键值存储, WiscKey 采用了四个关键思路. 第一, 将键和值分离, LSM-tree 中只保留键, 值单独写入日志文件. 第二, 利用 SSD 的并行随机读能力, 应对值不再有序而导致的范围查询随机访问. 第三, 采用专门的崩溃一致性和垃圾回收机制, 高效管理值日志. 最后, 在不牺牲一致性的前提下去掉 LSM-tree 日志, 减少小写入带来的系统调用开销.

## 4.1 设计目标

WiscKey is a single-machine persistent key-value store, derived from LevelDB. It can be deployed as the storage engine for a relational database (`e.g.`, MySQL) or a distributed key-value store (`e.g.`, MongoDB). It provides the same API as LevelDB, including Put(key, value), Get(key), Delete(key) and Scan(start, end). The design of WiscKey follows these main goals.

WiscKey 是基于 LevelDB 开发的单机持久化键值存储, 可作为关系数据库 (如 MySQL) 或分布式键值存储 (如 MongoDB) 的存储引擎. 它提供与 LevelDB 相同的 API, 包括 `Put(key, value)`, `Get(key)`, `Delete(key)` 和 `Scan(start, end)`. 设计主要遵循以下目标.

Low write amplification. Write amplification introduces extra unnecessary writes. Even though SSD devices have higher bandwidth compared to hard drives, large write amplification can consume most of the write bandwidth (over 90% is not uncommon) and decrease the SSD's lifetime due to limited erase cycles. Therefore, it is important to minimize write amplification, so as to improve workload performance and SSD lifetime.

**低写放大.** 写放大会产生不必要的额外写入. 虽然 SSD 的带宽高于机械硬盘, 但严重的写放大仍可能消耗大部分写带宽, 超过 90% 并不少见. 由于擦除次数有限, 这也会缩短 SSD 寿命. 因此, 应尽量降低写放大, 以改善负载性能并延长设备寿命.

Low read amplification. Large read amplification causes two problems. First, the throughput of lookups is significantly reduced by issuing multiple reads for each lookup. Second, the large amount of data loaded into memory decreases the efficiency of the cache. WiscKey targets a small read amplification to speedup lookups.

**低读放大.** 严重的读放大有两个问题: 一次查找触发多次读取, 显著降低查询吞吐量; 大量数据进入内存, 降低缓存效率. WiscKey 希望以较低的读放大加快查找.

SSD optimized. WiscKey is optimized for SSD devices by matching its I/O patterns with the performance characteristics of SSD devices. Specifically, sequential writes and parallel random reads are effectively utilized so that applications can fully utilize the device's bandwidth.

**针对 SSD 优化.** WiscKey 使 I/O 模式与 SSD 的性能特征相匹配, 具体通过顺序写和并行随机读, 帮助应用充分利用设备带宽.

Feature-rich API. WiscKey aims to support modern features that have made LSM-trees popular, such as range queries and snapshots. Range queries allow scanning a contiguous sequence of key-value pairs. Snapshots allow capturing the state of the database at a particular time and then performing lookups on the state.

**功能丰富的 API.** WiscKey 希望支持范围查询和快照等促成 LSM-tree 广泛应用的功能. 范围查询可以扫描连续的一段键值对; 快照可以保存数据库某一时刻的状态, 并在该状态上执行查找.

Realistic key-value sizes. Keys are usually small in modern workloads (`e.g.`, 16 B) [7, 8, 11, 22, 35], though value sizes can vary widely (`e.g.`, 100 B to larger than 4 KB) [6, 11, 22, 28, 32, 49]. WiscKey aims to provide high performance for this realistic set of key-value sizes.

**符合实际负载的键值大小.** 现代负载中的键通常较小, 例如 16 B [7, 8, 11, 22, 35], 而值的大小差异很大, 从 100 B 到超过 4 KB 不等 [6, 11, 22, 28, 32, 49]. WiscKey 希望在这些实际常见的键值大小下都能提供较高性能.

## 4.2 键值分离

The major performance cost of LSM-trees is the compaction process, which constantly sorts SSTable files. During compaction, multiple files are read into memory, sorted, and written back, which could significantly affect the performance of foreground workloads. However, sorting is required for efficient retrieval; with sorting, range queries (`i.e.`, scan) will result mostly in sequential access to multiple files, while point queries would require accessing at most one file at each level.

LSM-tree 的主要性能开销来自不断对 SSTable 文件排序的 compaction 过程. 多个文件被读入内存, 排序后重新写回, 可能严重影响前台负载. 但高效检索又离不开排序: 有序存储使范围查询 (扫描) 访问多个文件时主要采用顺序读取, 点查则至多需要访问每层的一个文件.

WiscKey is motivated by a simple revelation. Compaction only needs to sort keys, while values can be managed separately [42]. Since keys are usually smaller than values, compacting only keys could significantly reduce the amount of data needed during the sorting. In WiscKey, only the location of the value is stored in the LSM-tree with the key, while the actual values are stored elsewhere in an SSD-friendly fashion. With this design, for a database with a given size, the size of the LSM-tree of WiscKey is much smaller than that of LevelDB. The smaller LSM-tree can remarkably reduce the write amplification for modern workloads that have a moderately large value size. For example, assuming a 16-B key, a 1-KB value, and a write amplification of 10 for keys (in the LSM-tree) and 1 for values, the effective write amplification of WiscKey is only (10 × 16 + 1024) / (16 + 1024) = 1.14. In addition to improving the write performance of applications, the reduced write amplification also improves an SSD's lifetime by requiring fewer erase cycles.

WiscKey 源于一个简单的观察: compaction 只需要对键排序, 值可以单独管理 [42]. 键通常比值小, 因而仅对键执行 compaction, 可以显著减少排序过程处理的数据量. WiscKey 在 LSM-tree 中随键保存的只是值的位置, 实际的值以适合 SSD 的方式存放在其他地方. 对于相同大小的数据库, 这一设计使 WiscKey 的 LSM-tree 远小于 LevelDB 的树. 当值较大时, 较小的树可以显著降低现代负载的写放大. 例如, 假设键为 16 B, 值为 1 KB, 树中键的写放大为 10, 值的写放大为 1, 则 WiscKey 的实际写放大只有 (10 × 16 + 1024) / (16 + 1024) = 1.14. 降低写放大不仅能改善应用的写入性能, 还可减少擦除次数, 延长 SSD 寿命.

WiscKey's smaller read amplification improves lookup performance. During lookup, WiscKey first searches the LSM-tree for the key and the value's location; once found, another read is issued to retrieve the value. Readers might assume that WiscKey will be slower than LevelDB for lookups, due to its extra I/O to retrieve the value. However, since the LSM-tree of WiscKey is much smaller than LevelDB (for the same database size), a lookup may search fewer levels of table files in the LSM-tree and a significant portion of the LSM-tree can be easily cached in memory. Hence, each lookup only requires a single random read (for retrieving the value) and thus achieves a lookup performance better than LevelDB. For example, assuming 16-B keys and 1-KB values, if the size of the entire key-value dataset is 100 GB, then the size of the LSM-tree is only around 2 GB (assuming a 12-B cost for a value's location and size), which can be easily cached in modern servers which have over 100-GB of memory.

WiscKey 较低的读放大也改善了查找性能. 查询时, 它先在 LSM-tree 中找到键及值的位置, 再读取实际的值. 由于多了一次取值 I/O, 读者可能以为它会比 LevelDB 更慢. 但对于同样大小的数据库, WiscKey 的树小得多, 查询可能只需搜索更少的层, 而且很大一部分树可以轻松缓存在内存中. 因而, 每次查询可能只需一次用于取值的随机读, 性能反而优于 LevelDB. 例如, 假设键为 16 B, 值为 1 KB, 整个数据集为 100 GB, 则 LSM-tree 只有约 2 GB (假设记录值的位置和大小需要 12 B), 在拥有超过 100 GB 内存的现代服务器上很容易缓存下来.

WiscKey's architecture is shown in Figure 4. Keys are stored in an LSM-tree while values are stored in a separate value-log file, the vLog. The artificial value stored along with the key in the LSM-tree is the address of the actual value in the vLog.

图 4 展示了 WiscKey 的架构. 键保存在 LSM-tree 中, 值保存在单独的值日志文件 vLog 中. 在 LSM-tree 内与键配对的占位值, 实际上是该键对应的值在 vLog 中的地址.

When the user inserts a key-value pair in WiscKey, the value is first appended to the vLog, and the key is then inserted into the LSM tree along with the value's address (`<vLog-offset, value-size>`). Deleting a key simply deletes it from the LSM tree, without touching the vLog. All valid values in the vLog have corresponding keys in the LSM-tree; the other values in the vLog are invalid and will be garbage collected later (§ 3.3.2).

用户插入键值对时, WiscKey 先将值追加到 vLog, 再将键及值的地址 `<vLog-offset, value-size>` 插入 LSM-tree. 删除键时只需从树中删除, 不必操作 vLog. vLog 中所有有效值都在 LSM-tree 中有对应的键, 其余值则已失效, 留待后续垃圾回收 (原文第 3.3.2 节).

When the user queries for a key, the key is first searched in the LSM-tree, and if found, the corresponding value's address is retrieved. Then, WiscKey reads the value from the vLog. Note that this process is applied to both point queries and range queries.

用户查询一个键时, WiscKey 先搜索 LSM-tree, 找到后取出对应值的地址, 再从 vLog 读取值. 点查和范围查询都使用这一过程.

Although the idea behind key-value separation is simple, it leads to many challenges and optimization opportunities described in the following subsections.

键值分离的思路虽然简单, 却带来了许多挑战和优化机会, 后续小节将逐一说明.

## 4.3 设计挑战

The separation of keys and values makes range queries require random I/O. Furthermore, the separation makes both garbage collection and crash consistency challenging. We now explain how we solve these challenges.

键值分离使范围查询需要随机 I/O, 也给垃圾回收和崩溃一致性带来挑战. 下面说明我们如何解决这些问题.

### 4.3.1 并行范围查询

Range queries are an important feature of modern key-value stores, allowing users to scan a range of key-value pairs. Relational databases [26], local file systems [30, 46, 50], and even distributed file systems [37] use key-value stores as their storage engines, and range queries are a core API requested in these environments.

范围查询是现代键值存储的重要功能, 允许用户扫描一段键值对. 关系数据库 [26], 本地文件系统 [30, 46, 50], 乃至分布式文件系统 [37] 都使用键值存储作为存储引擎, 范围查询是这些场景要求提供的核心 API.

For range queries, LevelDB provides the user with an iterator-based interface with Seek(key), Next(), Prev(), Key() and Value() operations. To scan a range of key-value pairs, users can first Seek() to the starting key, then call Next() or Prev() to search keys one by one. To retrieve the key or the value of the current iterator position, users call Key() or Value(), respectively.

LevelDB 通过迭代器提供范围查询接口, 包括 `Seek(key)`, `Next()`, `Prev()`, `Key()` 和 `Value()` 操作. 用户先用 `Seek()` 定位起始键, 再调用 `Next()` 或 `Prev()` 逐个遍历. 要获取迭代器当前位置的键或值, 分别调用 `Key()` 或 `Value()`.

In LevelDB, since keys and values are stored together and sorted, a range query can sequentially read key-value pairs from SSTable files. However, since keys and values are stored separately in WiscKey, range queries require random reads, and are hence not efficient. As we see in Figure 3, the random read performance of a single thread on SSD cannot match the sequential read performance. However, parallel random reads with a fairly large request size can fully utilize the device's internal parallelism, getting performance similar to sequential reads.

在 LevelDB 中, 键和值一起按序存储, 范围查询可以从 SSTable 文件顺序读取键值对. WiscKey 将两者分开存储, 范围查询需要随机读, 效率因而受限. 如图 3 所示, SSD 的单线程随机读性能赶不上顺序读. 不过, 当请求足够大时, 并行随机读能够充分利用设备内部并行度, 获得接近顺序读的性能.

To make range queries efficient, WiscKey leverages the parallel I/O characteristic of SSD devices to prefetch values from the vLog during range queries. The underlying idea is that, with SSDs, only keys require special attention for efficient retrieval. So long as keys are retrieved efficiently, range queries can use parallel random reads for efficiently retrieving values.

为提高范围查询效率, WiscKey 利用 SSD 的并行 I/O 特性, 在查询过程中预取 vLog 中的值. 核心思路是: 使用 SSD 时, 只需特别关注键的检索效率. 只要能高效获取键, 范围查询就可以通过并行随机读高效地取回对应的值.

The prefetching framework can easily fit with the current range query interface. In the current interface, if the user requests a range query, an iterator is returned to the user. For each Next() or Prev() requested on the iterator, WiscKey tracks the access pattern of the range query. Once a contiguous sequence of key-value pairs is requested, WiscKey starts reading a number of following keys from the LSM-tree sequentially. The corresponding value addresses retrieved from the LSM-tree are inserted into a queue; multiple threads will fetch these addresses from the vLog concurrently in the background.

这一预取机制很容易融入现有的范围查询接口. 用户发起范围查询后会得到一个迭代器. 每次调用 `Next()` 或 `Prev()` 时, WiscKey 都会跟踪访问模式. 一旦发现用户连续访问一段键值对, 它就开始从 LSM-tree 顺序读取后续若干个键, 并将取得的值地址加入队列; 多个后台线程随后并发读取 vLog 中对应位置的数据.

### 4.3.2 垃圾回收

Key-value stores based on standard LSM-trees do not immediately reclaim free space when a key-value pair is deleted or overwritten. Rather, during compaction, if data relating to a deleted or overwritten key-value pair is found, the data is discarded and space is reclaimed. In WiscKey, only invalid keys are reclaimed by the LSM-tree compaction. Since WiscKey does not compact values, it needs a special garbage collector to reclaim free space in the vLog.

标准 LSM-tree 键值存储在键值对被删除或覆盖时, 不会立即释放空间. 它会在 compaction 中丢弃与这些键值对有关的旧数据, 从而回收空间. WiscKey 的 LSM-tree compaction 只回收失效的键, 不处理值, 因而需要专门的垃圾回收器释放 vLog 中的空间.

Since we only store the values in the vLog file (§ 3.2), a naive way to reclaim free space from the vLog is to first scan the LSM-tree to get all the valid value addresses; then, all the values in the vLog without any valid reference from the LSM-tree can be viewed as invalid and reclaimed. However, this method is too heavyweight and is only usable for offline garbage collection.

按照原文第 3.2 节的设计, vLog 只保存值. 一种直接的回收方法是先扫描 LSM-tree, 获取所有有效值的地址, 再将 vLog 中没有有效引用的值视为失效数据并回收. 但这种方法开销过大, 只适用于离线垃圾回收.

WiscKey targets a lightweight and online garbage collector. To make this possible, we introduce a small change to WiscKey's basic data layout: while storing values in the vLog, we also store the corresponding key along with the value. The new data layout is shown in Figure 5: the tuple (key size, value size, key, value) is stored in the vLog.

WiscKey 需要轻量级在线垃圾回收. 为此, 我们对基本数据布局做了一处小改动: 将值写入 vLog 时, 一并保存对应的键. 如图 5 所示, vLog 存储的内容变成了 `(key size, value size, key, value)` 元组.

WiscKey's garbage collection aims to keep valid values (that do not correspond to deleted keys) in a contiguous range of the vLog, as shown in Figure 5. One end of this range, the head, always corresponds to the end of the vLog where new values will be appended. The other end of this range, known as the tail, is where garbage collection starts freeing space whenever it is triggered. Only the part of the vLog between the head and the tail contains valid values and will be searched during lookups.

如图 5 所示, WiscKey 的垃圾回收使有效值 (其对应的键尚未删除) 位于 vLog 的一段连续区间内. 区间的一端称为 head, 始终对应 vLog 的末尾, 新值从这里追加. 另一端称为 tail, 垃圾回收每次都从这里开始释放空间. 只有 head 和 tail 之间可能包含有效值, 查找也只会访问这一区间.

![WiscKey 图 5: 支持垃圾回收的 WiscKey 新数据布局](/assets/images/posts/wisckey/figure-05.webp)

</div>

<span class="paper-caption paper-lang-en" lang="en">Figure 5: WiscKey New Data Layout for Garbage Collection. This figure shows the new data layout of WiscKey to support an efficient garbage collection. A head and tail pointer are maintained in memory and stored persistently in the LSM-tree. Only the garbage collection thread changes the tail, while all writes to the vLog are append to the head.</span>

<span class="paper-caption paper-lang-zh" lang="zh-CN">图 5: 支持垃圾回收的 WiscKey 新数据布局. 系统在内存中维护 head 和 tail 指针, 并将其持久化到 LSM-tree. 只有垃圾回收线程修改 tail, 所有 vLog 写入都追加到 head.</span>

During garbage collection, WiscKey first reads a chunk of key-value pairs (`e.g.`, several MBs) from the tail of the vLog, then finds which of those values are valid (not yet overwritten or deleted) by querying the LSM-tree. WiscKey then appends valid values back to the head of the vLog. Finally, it frees the space occupied previously by the chunk, and updates the tail accordingly.

垃圾回收时, WiscKey 先从 vLog 的 tail 读取一块键值对, 例如几 MB, 再查询 LSM-tree, 判断其中哪些值仍有效, 即尚未被覆盖或删除. 然后将有效值重新追加到 head, 最后释放原数据块占用的空间, 并相应更新 tail.

To avoid losing any data if a crash happens during garbage collection, WiscKey has to make sure that the newly appended valid values and the new tail are persistent on the device before actually freeing space. WiscKey achieves this using the following steps. After appending the valid values to the vLog, the garbage collection calls a fsync() on the vLog. Then, it adds these new value's addresses and current tail to the LSM-tree in a synchronous manner; the tail is stored in the LSM-tree as `<"tail", tail-vLog-offset>`. Finally, the free space in the vLog is reclaimed.

为防止垃圾回收过程中崩溃导致数据丢失, WiscKey 必须在真正释放空间之前, 确保新追加的有效值和新的 tail 已持久化. 具体步骤是: 将有效值追加到 vLog 后, 对 vLog 调用 `fsync()`; 然后以同步方式将这些值的新地址和当前 tail 写入 LSM-tree, 其中 tail 保存为 `<"tail", tail-vLog-offset>`; 最后回收 vLog 中的旧空间.

WiscKey can be configured to initiate and continue garbage collection periodically or until a particular threshold is reached. The garbage collection can also run in offline mode for maintenance. Garbage collection can be triggered rarely for workloads with few deletes and for environments with overprovisioned storage space.

WiscKey 可以配置为定期启动并持续执行垃圾回收, 或在达到某个阈值后执行. 维护时也可以离线运行垃圾回收. 对于删除较少的负载或存储空间预留充足的环境, 可以降低回收频率.

### 4.3.3 崩溃一致性

On a system crash, LSM-tree implementations usually guarantee atomicity of inserted key-value pairs and in-order recovery of inserted pairs. Since WiscKey's architecture stores values separately from the LSM-tree, obtaining the same crash guarantees can appear complicated. However, WiscKey provides the same crash guarantees by using an interesting property of modern file systems (such as ext4, btrfs, and xfs). Consider a file that contains the sequence of bytes `<b1 b2 b3 ... bn>`, and the user appends the sequence `<bn+1 bn+2 bn+3 ... bn+m>` to it. If a crash happens, after file-system recovery in modern file systems, the file will be observed to contain the sequence of bytes `<b1 b2 b3 ... bn bn+1 bn+2 bn+3 ... bn+x>`, ∃ x < m, `i.e.`, only some prefix of the appended bytes will be added to the end of the file during file-system recovery [45]. It is not possible for random bytes or a non-prefix subset of the appended bytes to be added to the file. Since values are appended sequentially to the end of the vLog file in WiscKey, the aforementioned property conveniently translates as follows: if a value X in the vLog is lost in a crash, all future values (inserted after X) are lost too.

系统崩溃后, LSM-tree 实现通常保证插入键值对的原子性, 并按插入顺序恢复. WiscKey 将值与树分开存储, 要提供相同保证似乎更复杂. 不过, 它利用了 ext4, btrfs 和 xfs 等现代文件系统的一项特性. 假设文件原有字节序列为 `<b1 b2 b3 ... bn>`, 用户追加 `<bn+1 bn+2 bn+3 ... bn+m>`. 发生崩溃并完成文件系统恢复后, 文件中的字节序列为 `<b1 b2 b3 ... bn bn+1 bn+2 bn+3 ... bn+x>`, 其中存在 x < m. 也就是说, 恢复后追加到文件末尾的, 只能是新写入字节序列的某个前缀 [45], 不会出现随机字节或非前缀的子集. WiscKey 将值依次追加到 vLog 末尾, 因而这一特性意味着: 如果值 X 在崩溃中丢失, 那么在 X 之后插入的所有值也都会丢失.

When the user queries a key-value pair, if WiscKey cannot find the key in the LSM-tree because the key had been lost during a system crash, WiscKey behaves exactly like traditional LSM-trees: even if the value had been written in vLog before the crash, it will be garbage collected later. If the key could be found in the LSM tree, however, an additional step is required to maintain consistency. In this case, WiscKey first verifies whether the value address retrieved from the LSM-tree falls within the current valid range of the vLog, and then whether the value found corresponds to the queried key. If the verifications fail, WiscKey assumes that the value was lost during a system crash, deletes the key from the LSM-tree, and informs the user that the key was not found. Since each value added to the vLog has a header including the corresponding key, verifying whether the key and the value match is straightforward; if necessary, a magic number or checksum can be easily added to the header.

用户查询键值对时, 如果键在崩溃中丢失, 导致 WiscKey 无法在树中找到它, 系统的行为与传统 LSM-tree 相同: 即使值在崩溃前已写入 vLog, 后续也会被垃圾回收. 如果树中仍能找到键, 则还需额外检查以保持一致性. WiscKey 先确认值地址位于 vLog 当前有效区间内, 再确认读到的值确实对应查询的键. 检查失败时, 系统认为值已在崩溃中丢失, 从树中删除该键, 并向用户返回未找到. vLog 中每个值的头部都包含对应的键, 因此很容易核对键值是否匹配; 必要时还可以在头部加入魔数或校验和.

LSM-tree implementations also guarantee the user durability of key value pairs after a system crash if the user specifically requests synchronous inserts. WiscKey implements synchronous inserts by flushing the vLog before performing a synchronous insert into its LSM-tree.

如果用户明确要求同步插入, LSM-tree 实现还会保证键值对在系统崩溃后的持久性. WiscKey 实现同步插入的方式是先将 vLog 刷入持久存储, 再向 LSM-tree 执行同步插入.

## 4.4 优化

Separating keys from values in WiscKey provides an opportunity to rethink how the value log is updated and the necessity of the LSM-tree log. We now describe how these opportunities can lead to improved performance.

键值分离让我们有机会重新考虑值日志的更新方式, 以及是否还需要 LSM-tree 日志. 下面介绍这些优化如何提高性能.

### 4.4.1 值日志写缓冲

For each Put(), WiscKey needs to append the value to the vLog by using a write() system call. However, for an insert-intensive workload, issuing a large number of small writes to a file system can introduce a noticeable overhead, especially on a fast storage device [15, 44]. Figure 6 shows the total time to sequentially write a 10-GB file in ext4 (Linux 3.14). For small writes, the overhead of each system call aggregates significantly, leading to a long run time. With large writes (larger than 4 KB), the device throughput is fully utilized.

每次执行 `Put()` 时, WiscKey 都需要通过 `write()` 系统调用将值追加到 vLog. 然而, 对于插入密集型负载, 向文件系统发起大量小写入会产生明显开销, 在高速存储设备上尤其如此 [15, 44]. 图 6 展示了在 ext4 (Linux 3.14) 上顺序写入 10 GB 文件所需的总时间. 写入较小时, 单次系统调用的开销不断累积, 导致运行时间较长. 当单次写入大于 4 KB 时, 设备吞吐能力才得以充分发挥.

</div>

<span class="paper-caption paper-lang-en" lang="en">Figure 6: Impact of Write Unit Size. This figure shows the total time to write a 10-GB file to an ext4 file system on an SSD device, followed by a fsync() at the end. We vary the size of each write() system call.</span>

<span class="paper-caption paper-lang-zh" lang="zh-CN">图 6: 单次写入大小的影响. 图中统计向 SSD 上的 ext4 文件系统写入 10 GB 文件, 并在末尾执行一次 fsync() 的总时间. 横轴为每次 write() 系统调用的写入大小.</span>

To reduce overhead, WiscKey buffers values in a userspace buffer, and flushes the buffer only when the buffer size exceeds a threshold or when the user requests a synchronous insertion. Thus, WiscKey only issues large writes and reduces the number of write() system calls. For a lookup, WiscKey first searches the vLog buffer, and if not found there, actually reads from the vLog. Obviously, this mechanism might result in some data (that is buffered) to be lost during a crash; the crash-consistency guarantee obtained is similar to LevelDB.

为减少开销, WiscKey 先将值放入用户态缓冲区, 只有缓冲区大小超过阈值, 或用户要求同步插入时, 才将其刷出. 这样既能进行大块写入, 又能减少 `write()` 系统调用次数. 查找时先检查 vLog 缓冲区, 没有找到才读取 vLog 文件. 显然, 崩溃可能导致部分缓冲数据丢失, 这一机制提供的崩溃一致性保证与 LevelDB 类似.

### 4.4.2 优化 LSM-tree 日志

As shown in Figure 1, a log file is usually used in LSM-trees. The LSM-tree tracks inserted key-value pairs in the log file so that, if the user requests synchronous inserts and there is a crash, the log can be scanned after reboot and the inserted key-value pairs recovered.

如图 1 所示, LSM-tree 通常使用日志文件记录插入的键值对. 如果用户要求同步插入, 后续又发生崩溃, 系统可以在重启后扫描日志, 恢复这些键值对.

In WiscKey, the LSM-tree is only used for keys and value addresses. Moreover, the vLog also records inserted keys to support garbage collection as described in the previous section. Hence, writes to the LSM-tree log file can be avoided without affecting correctness.

在 WiscKey 中, LSM-tree 只保存键和值的地址. 而且, 如前节所述, vLog 为支持垃圾回收, 也记录了插入的键. 因而, 不再写入 LSM-tree 日志也不会影响正确性.

If a crash happens before the keys are persistent in the LSM-tree, they can be recovered by scanning the vLog. However, a naive algorithm would require scanning the entire vLog for recovery. So as to require scanning only a small portion of the vLog, WiscKey records the head of the vLog periodically in the LSM-tree, as a key-value pair `<"head", head-vLog-offset>`. When a database is opened, WiscKey starts the vLog scan from the most recent head position stored in the LSM-tree, and continues scanning until the end of the vLog. Since the head is stored in the LSM-tree, and the LSM-tree inherently guarantees that keys inserted into the LSM-tree will be recovered in the inserted order, this optimization is crash consistent. Therefore, removing the LSM-tree log of WiscKey is a safe optimization, and improves performance especially when there are many small insertions.

如果键尚未在 LSM-tree 中持久化就发生崩溃, 可以通过扫描 vLog 恢复. 直接扫描整个 vLog 代价很高. 为使恢复只需扫描其中一小段, WiscKey 定期把 vLog 的 head 以 `<"head", head-vLog-offset>` 键值对的形式写入 LSM-tree. 打开数据库时, 从树中记录的最新 head 位置开始扫描, 直到 vLog 末尾. head 保存在 LSM-tree 中, 而树本身保证按插入顺序恢复键, 因而这一优化满足崩溃一致性要求. 因此, WiscKey 可以省去 LSM-tree 日志 (即用于崩溃恢复的预写日志 WAL), 在保证崩溃一致性的同时提升性能, 尤其适用于需要插入大量小记录的场景.

## 4.5 实现

WiscKey is based on LevelDB 1.18. WiscKey creates a vLog when creating a new database, and manages the keys and value addresses in the LSM-tree. The vLog is internally accessed by multiple components with different access patterns. For example, a lookup is served by randomly reading the vLog, while the garbage collector sequentially reads from the tail and appends to the head of the vLog file. We use posix_fadvise() to predeclare access patterns for the vLog under different situations.

WiscKey 基于 LevelDB 1.18 实现. 创建数据库时会同时创建 vLog, 键和值地址则由 LSM-tree 管理. 内部多个组件以不同模式访问 vLog: 查找通过随机读取获取值, 垃圾回收器则从 tail 顺序读取, 向 head 追加写入. 我们使用 `posix_fadvise()` 预先告知文件系统不同情况下的访问模式.

For range queries, WiscKey maintains a background thread pool with 32 threads. These threads sleep on a thread-safe queue, waiting for new value addresses to arrive. When prefetching is triggered, WiscKey inserts a fixed number of value addresses to the worker queue, and then wakes up all the sleeping threads. These threads will start reading values in parallel, caching them in the buffer cache automatically.

为处理范围查询, WiscKey 维护一个包含 32 个线程的后台线程池. 线程在一个线程安全队列上休眠, 等待新的值地址到来. 预取触发后, WiscKey 将固定数量的值地址加入工作队列, 唤醒所有休眠线程. 各线程并行读取值, 数据随之自动进入系统缓冲缓存.

To efficiently garbage collect the free space of the vLog, we use the hole-punching functionality of modern file systems (fallocate()). Punching a hole in a file can free the physical space allocated, and allows WiscKey to elastically use the storage space. The maximal file size on modern file systems is big enough for WiscKey to run a long time without wrapping back to the beginning of the file; for example, the maximal file size is 64 TB on ext4, 8 EB on xfs and 16 EB on btrfs. The vLog can be trivially adapted into a circular log if necessary.

为高效回收 vLog 空间, 我们使用现代文件系统的打洞功能 `fallocate()`. 对文件打洞可以释放已分配的物理空间, 让 WiscKey 按需占用存储容量. 现代文件系统支持的最大文件足够大, WiscKey 可以长时间运行而不必绕回文件开头. 例如, ext4 的最大文件为 64 TB, xfs 为 8 EB, btrfs 为 16 EB. 如果需要, 也很容易把 vLog 改为循环日志.

本节通过实验结果, 展示 WiscKey 各项设计选择带来的收益.

## 5.1 微基准测试

We use db_bench (the default microbenchmarks in LevelDB) to evaluate LevelDB and WiscKey. We always use a key size of 16 B, but perform experiments for different value sizes. We disable data compression for easier understanding and analysis of performance.

我们使用 LevelDB 默认的微基准测试工具 `db_bench` 评估 LevelDB 和 WiscKey. 键大小固定为 16 B, 值大小随实验变化. 为便于理解和分析性能, 实验关闭了数据压缩.

### 5.1.1 加载性能

We now describe the results for the sequential-load and random-load microbenchmarks. The former benchmark constructs a 100-GB database by inserting keys in a sequential order, while the latter inserts keys in a uniformly distributed random order. Note that the sequential-load benchmark does not cause compaction in either LevelDB or WiscKey, while the random-load does.

下面介绍顺序加载和随机加载微基准测试的结果. 前者按键的顺序插入, 构建一个 100 GB 数据库; 后者按均匀分布的随机顺序插入. 顺序加载在 LevelDB 和 WiscKey 中均不会触发 compaction, 随机加载则会.

Figure 7 shows the sequential-load throughput of LevelDB and WiscKey for a wide range of value sizes: the throughput of both stores increases with the value size. But, even for the largest value size considered (256 KB), LevelDB's throughput is far from the device bandwidth. To analyze this further, Figure 8 shows the distribution of the time spent in different components during each run of the benchmark, for LevelDB; time is spent in three major parts: writing to the log file, inserting to the memtable, and waiting for the memtable to be flushed to the device. For small key-value pairs, writing to the log file accounts for the most significant percentage of the total time, for the reasons explained in Figure 6. For larger pairs, log writing and the memtable sorting are more efficient, while memtable flushes are the bottleneck. Unlike LevelDB, WiscKey reaches the full device bandwidth for value sizes more than 4 KB. Since it does not write to the LSM-tree log and buffers appends to the vLog, it is 3× faster even for small values.

WiscKey 则在值大于 4 KB 时就能充分利用设备带宽. 由于它不写 LSM-tree 日志, 且缓冲了 vLog 追加写入, 即使值很小, 速度也达到 LevelDB 的 3 倍.

### 5.1.2 查询性能

We now compare the random lookup (point query) and range query performance of LevelDB and WiscKey. Figure 11 presents the random lookup results of 100,000 operations on a 100-GB random-loaded database. Even though a random lookup in WiscKey needs to check both the LSM-tree and the vLog, the throughput of WiscKey is still much better than LevelDB: for 1-KB value size, WiscKey's throughput is 12× of that of LevelDB. For large value sizes, the throughput of WiscKey is only limited by the random read throughput of the device, as shown in Figure 3. LevelDB has low throughput because of the high read amplification mentioned in Section 2.3. WiscKey performs significantly better because the read amplification is lower due to a smaller LSM-tree. Another reason for WiscKey's better performance is that the compaction process in WiscKey is less intense, thus avoiding many background reads and writes.

下面比较两者的随机查找 (点查) 和范围查询性能. 图 11 展示了对随机加载的 100 GB 数据库执行 100,000 次随机查找的结果. 虽然 WiscKey 需要同时访问 LSM-tree 和 vLog, 吞吐量仍明显高于 LevelDB. 值为 1 KB 时, WiscKey 的吞吐量是 LevelDB 的 12 倍. 值较大时, WiscKey 的吞吐量仅受图 3 所示的设备随机读能力限制. LevelDB 的性能较低, 原因是原文第 2.3 节讨论的高读放大. WiscKey 的树更小, 读放大更低, 因而性能显著更好. 此外, 它的 compaction 也更轻量, 减少了许多后台读写.

### 5.1.3 垃圾回收

We now investigate WiscKey's performance while garbage collection is performed in the background. The performance can potentially vary depending on the percentage of free space found during garbage collection, since this affects the amount of data written and the amount of space freed by the garbage collection thread. We use random-load (the workload that is most affected by garbage collection) as the foreground workload, and study its performance for various percentages of free space. Our experiment specifically involves three steps: we first create a database using random-load, then delete the required percentage of key-value pairs, and finally, we run the random-load workload and measure its throughput while garbage collection happens in the background. We use a key-value size of 4 KB and vary the percentage of free space from 25% to 100%.

下面考察后台垃圾回收期间的性能. 回收过程发现的可释放空间比例不同, 所需写入的数据量和最终释放的空间也不同, 性能可能随之变化. 我们选用受垃圾回收影响最大的随机加载作为前台负载, 考察不同空闲空间比例下的表现. 实验分三步: 先随机加载数据库, 再删除指定比例的键值对, 最后在后台执行垃圾回收的同时运行随机加载并测量吞吐量. 键值对大小为 4 KB, 可释放空间比例从 25% 变化到 100%.

Figure 13 shows the results: if 100% of data read by the garbage collector is invalid, the throughput is only 10% lower. Throughput is only marginally lower because garbage collection reads from the tail of the vLog and writes only valid key-value pairs to the head; if the data read is entirely invalid, no key-value pair needs to be written. For other percentages of free space, throughput drops about 35% since the garbage collection thread performs additional writes. Note that, in all cases, while garbage collection is happening, WiscKey is at least 70× faster than LevelDB.

图 13 表明, 当回收器读到的数据全部失效时, 吞吐量仅下降 10%. 原因是回收器从 tail 读取, 只将仍有效的键值对写回 head; 如果全部失效, 就不必写回任何键值对. 在其他可释放空间比例下, 回收线程需要额外写入, 吞吐量约下降 35%. 但在所有测试情况中, 即使正在执行垃圾回收, WiscKey 的速度仍至少是 LevelDB 的 70 倍.

### 5.1.4 崩溃一致性

Separating keys from values necessitates additional mechanisms to maintain crash consistency. We verify the crash consistency mechanisms of WiscKey by using the ALICE tool [45]; the tool chooses and simulates a comprehensive set of system crashes that have a high probability of exposing inconsistency. We use a test case which invokes a few asynchronous and synchronous Put() calls. When configured to run tests for ext4, xfs, and btrfs, ALICE checks more than 3000 selectively-chosen system crashes, and does not report any consistency vulnerability introduced by WiscKey.

键值分离需要额外机制来保持崩溃一致性. 我们使用 ALICE 工具 [45] 验证 WiscKey 的相关机制. 该工具选择并模拟一组覆盖广泛, 容易暴露不一致问题的系统崩溃情况. 测试用例调用若干次异步和同步 `Put()`. 针对 ext4, xfs 和 btrfs 配置测试后, ALICE 检查了 3000 多种有针对性选择的崩溃情况, 未报告由 WiscKey 引入的一致性漏洞.

The new consistency mechanism also affects WiscKey's recovery time after a crash, and we design an experiment to measure the worst-case recovery time of WiscKey and LevelDB. LevelDB's recovery time is proportional to the size of its log file after the crash; the log file exists at its maximum size just before the memtable is written to disk. WiscKey, during recovery, first retrieves the head pointer from the LSM-tree, and then scans the vLog file from the head pointer till the end of the file. Since the updated head pointer is persisted on disk when the memtable is written, WiscKey's worst-case recovery time also corresponds to a crash happening just before then. We measured the worst-case recovery time induced by the situation described so far; for 1-KB values, LevelDB takes 0.7 seconds to recover the database after the crash, while WiscKey takes 2.6 seconds. Note that WiscKey can be configured to persist the head pointer more frequently if necessary.

新的一致性机制也影响崩溃恢复时间. 我们设计实验, 测量 WiscKey 和 LevelDB 最坏情况下的恢复耗时. LevelDB 的恢复时间与崩溃时日志文件大小成正比, 日志在 memtable 即将刷盘前达到最大. WiscKey 恢复时先从 LSM-tree 读取 head, 再从该位置扫描 vLog 直到末尾. 更新后的 head 随 memtable 刷盘而持久化, 因而其最坏情况同样发生在即将刷盘时崩溃. 实测在值为 1 KB 时, LevelDB 需 0.7 秒恢复数据库, WiscKey 需 2.6 秒. 必要时, 可以配置 WiscKey 更频繁地持久化 head.

### 5.1.5 空间放大

When evaluating a key-value store, most previous work focused only on read and write amplification. However, space amplification is important for flash devices because of their expensive price-per-GB compared with hard drives. Space amplification is the ratio of the actual size of the database on disk to the logical size of the the database [5]. For example, if a 1-KB key-value pair takes 4 KB of space on disk, then the space amplification is 4. Compression decreases space amplification while extra data (garbage, fragmentation, or metadata) increases space amplification. Compression is disabled to make the discussion simple.

以往评估键值存储的工作大多只关注读写放大. 但闪存单位容量的价格高于机械硬盘, 空间放大也很重要. 空间放大定义为数据库实际磁盘占用与逻辑大小之比 [5]. 例如, 1 KB 的键值对占用 4 KB 磁盘空间, 空间放大就是 4. 压缩可以降低空间放大, 垃圾数据, 碎片和元数据等额外内容则会增加它. 为简化讨论, 此处关闭压缩.

For a sequential-load workload, the space amplification can be near one, given that the extra metadata in LSM-trees is minimal. For a random-load or overwrite workload, space amplification is usually more than one when invalid pairs are not garbage collected fast enough.

顺序加载时, LSM-tree 的额外元数据很少, 空间放大可以接近 1. 随机加载或覆盖写入时, 如果失效键值对未能及时回收, 空间放大通常会大于 1.

Figure 14 shows the database size of LevelDB and WiscKey after randomly loading a 100-GB dataset (the same workload as Figure 9). The space overhead of LevelDB arises due to invalid key-value pairs that are not garbage collected when the workload is finished. The space overhead of WiscKey includes the invalid key-value pairs and the extra metadata (pointers in the LSM-tree and the tuple in the vLog as shown in Figure 5). After garbage collection, the database size of WiscKey is close to the logical database size when the extra metadata is small compared to the value size.

图 14 展示了随机加载 100 GB 数据集后两者的数据库大小, 负载与图 9 相同. LevelDB 的额外空间来自负载结束时尚未回收的失效键值对. WiscKey 的额外空间包括失效键值对, 以及树中的指针和图 5 所示 vLog 元组等元数据. 如果元数据相对于值很小, 垃圾回收后 WiscKey 的实际大小就接近逻辑大小.

No key-value store can minimize read amplification, write amplification, and space amplification at the same time. Tradeoffs among these three factors are balanced differently in various systems. In LevelDB, the sorting and garbage collection are coupled together. LevelDB trades higher write amplification for lower space amplification; however, the workload performance can be significantly affected. WiscKey consumes more space to minimize I/O amplification when the workload is running; because sorting and garbage collection are decoupled in WiscKey, garbage collection can be done later, thus minimizing its impact on foreground performance.

键值存储无法同时将读放大, 写放大和空间放大都降至最低, 不同系统对三者采取了不同取舍. LevelDB 将排序与垃圾回收绑定, 以更高的写放大换取更低的空间放大, 却可能严重影响负载性能. WiscKey 在负载运行期间多占用一些空间, 以尽量降低 I/O 放大. 由于排序与垃圾回收已经解耦, 可以将回收延后, 从而尽量减轻对前台性能的影响.

## 5.2 YCSB 基准测试

The YCSB benchmark [21] provides a framework and a standard set of six workloads for evaluating the performance of key-value stores. We use YCSB to compare LevelDB, RocksDB [25], and WiscKey, on a 100-GB database. In addition to measuring the usual-case performance of WiscKey, we also run WiscKey with garbage collection always happening in the background so as to measure its worst-case performance. RocksDB [25] is a SSD-optimized version of LevelDB with many optimizations, including multiple memtables and background threads for compaction. We use RocksDB with the default configuration parameters. We evaluated the key-value stores with two different value sizes, 1 KB and 16 KB (data compression is disabled).

YCSB 基准测试 [21] 提供了评估键值存储的框架和六种标准负载. 我们在 100 GB 数据库上比较 LevelDB, RocksDB [25] 和 WiscKey. 除了 WiscKey 的常规性能, 还让后台垃圾回收始终运行, 测量其最坏情况下的表现. RocksDB 是针对 SSD 优化的 LevelDB 版本, 包含多个 memtable 和多个后台 compaction 线程等优化. 测试使用 RocksDB 默认配置, 值大小分别为 1 KB 和 16 KB, 关闭数据压缩.

WiscKey performs significantly better than LevelDB and RocksDB, as shown in Figure 15. For example, during load, for 1-KB values, WiscKey performs at least 50× faster than the other databases in the usual case, and at least 45× faster in the worst case (with garbage collection); with 16-KB values, WiscKey performs 104× better, even under the worst case.

如图 15 所示, WiscKey 明显优于 LevelDB 和 RocksDB. 例如, 加载 1 KB 值时, WiscKey 常规情况下的速度至少是其他数据库的 50 倍; 最坏情况下, 即同时运行垃圾回收时, 也至少为 45 倍. 值为 16 KB 时, 即使在最坏情况下, 速度也达到 104 倍.

For reads, the Zipf distribution used in most workloads allows popular items to be cached and retrieved without incurring disk access, thus reducing WiscKey's advantage over LevelDB and RocksDB. Hence, WiscKey's relative performance (compared to the LevelDB and RocksDB) is better in Workload-A (50% reads) than in Workload-B (95% reads) and Workload-C (100% reads). However, RocksDB and LevelDB still do not match WiscKey's performance in any of these workloads.

对于读取, 多数负载采用 Zipf 分布, 热门记录可以缓存在内存中, 无需访问磁盘即可返回, 因而缩小了 WiscKey 相对 LevelDB 和 RocksDB 的优势. 所以, WiscKey 在读取占 50% 的负载 A 中, 相对优势大于读取占 95% 的负载 B 和纯读取的负载 C. 不过, 在这些负载中, RocksDB 和 LevelDB 仍都未达到 WiscKey 的性能.

The worst-case performance of WiscKey (with garbage collection switched on always, even for read-only workloads) is better than LevelDB and RocksDB. However, the impact of garbage collection on performance is markedly different for 1-KB and 16-KB values. Garbage collection repeatedly selects and cleans a 4-MB chunk of the vLog; with small values, the chunk will include many key-value pairs, and thus garbage collection spends more time accessing the LSM-tree to verify the validity of each pair. For large values, garbage collection spends less time on the verification, and hence aggressively writes out the cleaned chunk, affecting foreground throughput more. Note that, if necessary, garbage collection can be throttled to reduce its foreground impact.

WiscKey 最坏情况下的性能仍优于 LevelDB 和 RocksDB, 即使对只读负载也始终开启垃圾回收. 但回收对 1 KB 和 16 KB 值的影响明显不同. 回收器反复选择并清理 vLog 中 4 MB 的数据块. 值较小时, 一块包含很多键值对, 回收器要花更多时间访问 LSM-tree 来验证有效性. 值较大时, 验证所需时间较少, 回收器便更频繁地写出清理后的数据块, 对前台吞吐量的影响也更大. 必要时, 可以限制垃圾回收速率以减轻这种影响.

Unlike the microbenchmark considered previously, Workload-E has multiple small range queries, with each query retrieving between 1 and 100 key-value pairs. Since the workload involves multiple range queries, accessing the first key in each range resolves to a random lookup - a situation favorable for WiscKey. Hence, WiscKey performs better than RocksDB and LevelDB even for 1-KB values.

与前面的微基准测试不同, 负载 E 包含多个短范围查询, 每次读取 1-100 个键值对. 每个范围中的第一个键都需要一次随机查找, 这对 WiscKey 有利. 因而, 即使值为 1 KB, WiscKey 也优于 RocksDB 和 LevelDB.

# 6. 相关工作

Various key-value stores based on hash tables have been proposed for SSD devices. FAWN [8] keeps key-value pairs in a append-only log on the SSD, and uses an in-memory hash table index for fast lookups. FlashStore [22] and SkimpyStash [23] follow the same design, but optimize the in-memory hash table; FlashStore uses cuckoo hashing and compact key signatures, while SkimpyStash moves a part of the table to the SSD using linear chaining. BufferHash [7] uses multiple in-memory hash tables, with bloom filters to choose which hash table to use for a lookup. SILT [35] is highly optimized for memory, and uses a combination of log-structure, hash-table, and sorted-table layouts. WiscKey shares the log-structure data layout with these key-value stores. However, these stores use hash tables for indexing, and thus do not support modern features that have been built atop LSM-tree stores, such as range queries or snapshots. WiscKey instead targets a feature-rich key-value store which can be used in various situations.

已有多种面向 SSD, 基于哈希表的键值存储. FAWN [8] 将键值对保存在 SSD 上只追加写入的日志中, 通过内存哈希表索引快速查找. FlashStore [22] 和 SkimpyStash [23] 沿用这一设计并优化内存哈希表: 前者采用布谷鸟哈希和紧凑的键签名, 后者通过线性链将部分哈希表移到 SSD. BufferHash [7] 使用多个内存哈希表, 通过 Bloom filter 选择应查询的表. SILT [35] 对内存占用做了深入优化, 混合使用日志结构, 哈希表和有序表布局. WiscKey 与这些系统一样使用日志结构布局, 但它们以哈希表建立索引, 不支持范围查询和快照等构建在 LSM-tree 存储之上的现代功能. WiscKey 则希望提供功能丰富, 适用于多种场景的键值存储.

Much work has gone into optimizing the original LSM-tree key-value store [43]. bLSM [49] presents a new merge scheduler to bound write latency, thus maintaining a steady write throughput, and also uses bloom filters to improve performance. VT-tree [50] avoids sorting any previously sorted key-value pairs during compaction, by using a layer of indirection. WiscKey instead directly separates values from keys, significantly reducing write amplification regardless of the key distribution in the workload. LOCS [53] exposes internal flash channels to the LSM-tree key-value store, which can exploit the abundant parallelism for a more efficient compaction. Atlas [32] is a distributed key-value store based on ARM processors and erasure coding, and stores keys and values on different hard drives. WiscKey is a standalone key-value store, where the separation between keys and values is highly optimized for SSD devices to achieve significant performance gains. LSM-trie [54] uses a trie structure to organize keys, and proposes a more efficient compaction based on the trie; however, this design sacrifices LSM-tree features such as efficient support for range queries. RocksDB, described previously, still exhibits high write amplification due to its design being fundamentally similar to LevelDB; RocksDB's optimizations are orthogonal to WiscKey's design.

许多工作致力于优化原始 LSM-tree 键值存储 [43]. bLSM [49] 提出新的合并调度器来限制写延迟, 保持稳定的写吞吐量, 并使用 Bloom filter 改善性能. VT-tree [50] 通过一层间接寻址, 避免在 compaction 中重新排序已有序的键值对. WiscKey 则直接分离键和值, 不论负载的键分布如何, 都能显著降低写放大. LOCS [53] 将闪存内部通道暴露给 LSM-tree 键值存储, 利用丰富的并行能力提高 compaction 效率. Atlas [32] 是基于 ARM 处理器和纠删码的分布式键值存储, 将键和值放在不同的机械硬盘上. WiscKey 是单机键值存储, 键值分离专门针对 SSD 优化, 以取得显著性能收益. LSM-trie [54] 用 trie 结构组织键, 并据此提高 compaction 效率, 但牺牲了高效范围查询等 LSM-tree 功能. 前文介绍的 RocksDB 在根本设计上仍与 LevelDB 相似, 写放大依然较高; 它的优化与 WiscKey 的设计可以相互补充.

Walnut [18] is a hybrid object store which stores small objects in a LSM-tree and writes large objects directly to the file system. IndexFS [47] stores its metadata in a LSM-tree with the column-style schema to speed up the throughput of insertion. Purity [19] also separates its index from data tuples by only sorting the index and storing tuples in time order. All three systems use similar techniques as WiscKey. However, we solve this problem in a more generic and complete manner, and optimize both load and lookup performance for SSD devices across a wide range of workloads.

Walnut [18] 是混合对象存储, 小对象保存在 LSM-tree 中, 大对象直接写入文件系统. IndexFS [47] 在 LSM-tree 中采用列式模式保存元数据, 提高插入吞吐量. Purity [19] 也将索引与数据元组分离, 只对索引排序, 元组按时间顺序存储. 这三个系统都采用了与 WiscKey 类似的技术. 不过, 我们以更通用, 更完整的方式解决这一问题, 面向 SSD 和广泛的工作负载, 同时优化加载与查找性能.

Key-value stores based on other data structures have also been proposed. TokuDB [13, 14] is based on fractal-tree indexes, which buffer updates in internal nodes; the keys are not sorted, and a large index has to be maintained in memory for good performance. ForestDB [6] uses a HB+-trie to efficiently index long keys, improving the performance and reducing the space overhead of internal nodes. NVMKV [39] is a FTL-aware key-value store which uses native FTL capabilities, such as sparse addressing, and transactional supports. Vector interfaces that group multiple requests into a single operation are also proposed for key-value stores [52]. Since these key-value stores are based on different data structures, they each have different trade-offs relating to performance; instead, WiscKey proposes improving the widely used LSM-tree structure.

也有研究采用其他数据结构构建键值存储. TokuDB [13, 14] 基于分形树索引, 在内部节点缓冲更新; 键不排序, 为获得较好性能, 需要在内存中保留较大的索引. ForestDB [6] 使用 HB+-trie 高效索引长键, 提高性能并减少内部节点的空间开销. NVMKV [39] 能感知闪存转换层 (FTL), 利用其稀疏寻址和事务支持等原生能力. 还有工作提出向量接口, 将多个请求组合为一次操作 [52]. 这些系统的数据结构不同, 因而各有性能取舍; WiscKey 选择改进的是已经广泛应用的 LSM-tree.

Many proposed techniques seek to overcome the scalability bottlenecks of in-memory key-value stores, such as Mastree [38], MemC3 [27], Memcache [41], MICA [36] and cLSM [28]. These techniques may be adapted for WiscKey to further improve its performance.

许多技术尝试解决内存键值存储的扩展性瓶颈, 例如 Mastree [38], MemC3 [27], Memcache [41], MICA [36] 和 cLSM [28]. 这些技术也可能用于 WiscKey, 进一步提高其性能.

# 7. 结论

Key-value stores have become a fundamental building block in data-intensive applications. In this paper, we propose WiscKey, a novel LSM-tree-based key-value store that separates keys and values to minimize write and read amplification. The data layout and I/O patterns of WiscKey are highly optimized for SSD devices. Our results show that WiscKey can significantly improve performance for most workloads. Our hope is that key-value separation and various optimization techniques in WiscKey will inspire the future generation of high-performance key-value stores.

键值存储已成为数据密集型应用的基础组件. 本文提出 WiscKey, 一种通过键值分离尽量降低读写放大的新型 LSM-tree 键值存储. 它的数据布局和 I/O 模式都针对 SSD 进行了充分优化. 实验表明, WiscKey 能显著提高多数负载的性能. 我们希望其中的键值分离思路和各项优化, 能为下一代高性能键值存储提供启发.
