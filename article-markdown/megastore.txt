# 译 - Megastore: Providing Scalable, Highly Available Storage for Interactive Services

<div class="paper-shared">

2006 年的 Bigtable 论文展示了 Google 如何扩展结构化数据存储, 但 Bigtable 的事务范围限于单行. 2011 年的 Megastore 论文介绍了建立在 Bigtable 之上的方案: 按实体组组织数据, 在组内提供可串行化的 ACID 事务, 并用 Paxos 跨数据中心同步复制写入. 相比 Bigtable, 它还提供强类型模式, 二级索引和队列等更丰富的能力, 以支撑交互式应用. 以下是对 Megastore 原论文的翻译.

原论文: [Megastore: Providing Scalable, Highly Available Storage for Interactive Services](https://www.cidrdb.org/cidr2011/Papers/CIDR11_Paper32.pdf). Jason Baker 等, CIDR 2011, pp. 223-234. 原文采用 [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/) 许可; 本文保留作者与会议署名.

# 0. 摘要

Megastore is a storage system developed to meet the requirements of today's interactive online services. Megastore blends the scalability of a NoSQL datastore with the convenience of a traditional RDBMS in a novel way, and provides both strong consistency guarantees and high availability. We provide fully serializable ACID semantics within fine-grained partitions of data. This partitioning allows us to synchronously replicate each write across a wide area network with reasonable latency and support seamless failover between datacenters. This paper describes Megastore's semantics and replication algorithm. It also describes our experience supporting a wide range of Google production services built with Megastore.

Megastore 是为交互式在线服务设计的存储系统. 它既能像 NoSQL 存储一样扩展, 又具备传统关系数据库便于开发应用的特点, 同时保证强一致性和高可用性. **Megastore 在细粒度分区内提供完全可串行化的 ACID 事务.** 按分区组织数据后, 系统可以跨广域网同步复制每次写入, 延迟仍在交互式应用可接受的范围内; 数据中心故障时, 也能平滑切换到其他数据中心. 本文介绍 Megastore 的语义和复制算法, 以及它在多种 Google 生产服务中的应用经验.

<div class="paper-shared">

关键词: 大型数据库, 分布式事务, Bigtable, Paxos.

</div>

# 1. 介绍

Interactive online services are forcing the storage community to meet new demands as desktop applications migrate to the cloud. Services like email, collaborative documents, and social networking have been growing exponentially and are testing the limits of existing infrastructure. Meeting these services' storage demands is challenging due to a number of conflicting requirements.

随着桌面应用迁往云端, 交互式在线服务也对存储系统提出了新要求. 电子邮件, 协作文档和社交网络等服务快速增长, 现有基础设施逐渐难以承受. 这些服务对存储系统有多方面的要求, 而这些要求往往相互牵制.

First, the Internet brings a huge audience of potential users, so the applications must be highly scalable. A service can be built rapidly using MySQL<sup>[10]</sup> as its datastore, but scaling the service to millions of users requires a complete redesign of its storage infrastructure. Second, services must compete for users. This requires rapid development of features and fast time-to-market. Third, the service must be responsive; hence, the storage system must have low latency. Fourth, the service should provide the user with a consistent view of the data - the result of an update should be visible immediately and durably. Seeing edits to a cloud-hosted spreadsheet vanish, however briefly, is a poor user experience. Finally, users have come to expect Internet services to be up 24/7, so the service must be highly available. The service must be resilient to many kinds of faults ranging from the failure of individual disks, machines, or routers all the way up to large-scale outages affecting entire datacenters.

首先, 互联网服务可能面对大量用户, 因此必须具备扩展能力. 使用 MySQL<sup>[10]</sup> 可以快速搭建服务, 但服务要扩展到数百万用户时, 存储架构往往需要彻底重做. 其次, 服务要吸引用户, 就必须快速开发新功能并尽早上线. 第三, 服务需要及时响应, 存储系统的延迟因此不能太高. 第四, 用户看到的数据必须始终一致: 更新后应立即可见, 而且不能丢失. 用户刚在云端表格里做的修改, 哪怕只是短暂地看不到, 也很难让人接受. 最后, 用户希望互联网服务全天候运行, 因此系统必须能应对从磁盘, 机器或路由器故障, 到整个数据中心中断的各种情况.

These requirements are in conflict. Relational databases provide a rich set of features for easily building applications, but they are difficult to scale to hundreds of millions of users. NoSQL datastores such as Google's Bigtable<sup>[15]</sup>, Apache Hadoop's HBase<sup>[1]</sup>, or Facebook's Cassandra<sup>[6]</sup> are highly scalable, but their limited API and loose consistency models complicate application development. Replicating data across distant datacenters while providing low latency is challenging, as is guaranteeing a consistent view of replicated data, especially during faults.

这些要求难以兼顾. 关系数据库功能丰富, 便于开发应用, 却很难支撑数亿用户. Google Bigtable<sup>[15]</sup>, Apache Hadoop HBase<sup>[1]</sup> 和 Facebook Cassandra<sup>[6]</sup> 等 NoSQL 存储更容易扩展, 但 API 功能有限, 一致性保证也较弱, 应用开发因此更复杂. 跨越相距很远的数据中心复制数据, 还要保持低延迟和一致的数据视图, 尤其在故障期间, 并不容易.

Megastore is a storage system developed to meet the storage requirements of today's interactive online services. It is novel in that it blends the scalability of a NoSQL datastore with the convenience of a traditional RDBMS. It uses synchronous replication to achieve high availability and a consistent view of the data. In brief, it provides fully serializable ACID semantics over distant replicas with low enough latencies to support interactive applications.

Megastore 正是为满足交互式在线服务的这些需求而设计. 它既保留 NoSQL 存储的扩展能力, 又像传统 RDBMS 一样方便开发应用. 系统通过同步复制保持高可用性和一致的数据视图; 即使副本分布在不同地区, 仍能以交互式应用可接受的延迟提供完全可串行化的 ACID 事务.

We accomplish this by taking a middle ground in the RDBMS vs. NoSQL design space: we partition the datastore and replicate each partition separately, providing full ACID semantics within partitions, but only limited consistency guarantees across them. We provide traditional database features, such as secondary indexes, but only those features that can scale within user-tolerable latency limits, and only with the semantics that our partitioning scheme can support. We contend that the data for most Internet services can be suitably partitioned (for example, by user) to make this approach viable, and that a small, but not spartan, set of features can substantially ease the burden of developing cloud applications.

我们采取介于 RDBMS 与 NoSQL 之间的做法: 将数据分区, 分别复制, **在分区内提供完整的 ACID 语义, 在分区间只提供有限的一致性保证.** 系统保留二级索引等传统数据库功能, 但只提供符合分区方案语义, 且扩展后仍能保持可接受延迟的功能. 我们认为, 多数互联网服务都能找到合适的数据分区方式 (例如按用户划分). 这样一套精简而实用的功能, 足以减轻云应用开发的负担.

Contrary to conventional wisdom<sup>[24, 28]</sup>, we were able to use Paxos<sup>[27]</sup> to build a highly available system that provides reasonable latencies for interactive applications while synchronously replicating writes across geographically distributed datacenters. While many systems use Paxos solely for locking, master election, or replication of metadata and configurations, we believe that Megastore is the largest system deployed that uses Paxos to replicate primary user data across datacenters on every write.

虽然一些传统观点认为这很难实现<sup>[24, 28]</sup>, 我们仍用 Paxos<sup>[27]</sup> 构建了高可用系统: 每次写入都跨地域数据中心同步复制, 延迟却仍在交互式应用可接受的范围内. 许多系统只用 Paxos 处理加锁, 主节点选举, 元数据或配置复制. 据我们所知, Megastore 是当时规模最大的此类系统, 每次写入都会用 Paxos 跨数据中心复制主要用户数据.

Megastore has been widely deployed within Google for several years<sup>[20]</sup>. It handles more than three billion write and 20 billion read transactions daily and stores nearly a petabyte of primary data across many global datacenters.

截至论文撰写时, Megastore 已在 Google 内部广泛使用多年<sup>[20]</sup>. **它每天处理超过 30 亿次写事务和 200 亿次读事务, 在全球多个数据中心保存近 1 PB 核心数据.**

The key contributions of this paper are:

**本文的主要贡献是:**

1. the design of a data model and storage system that allows rapid development of interactive applications where high availability and scalability are built-in from the start;
2. an implementation of the Paxos replication and consensus algorithm optimized for low-latency operation across geographically distributed datacenters to provide high availability for the system;
3. a report on our experience with a large-scale deployment of Megastore at Google.

<!-- language-pair -->

1. 设计一种便于快速开发交互式应用的数据模型和存储系统, 从一开始就具备高可用性与扩展能力;
2. 实现 Paxos 复制与共识算法, 针对跨地域数据中心的低延迟运行加以优化, 提高系统可用性;
3. 总结 Megastore 在 Google 大规模部署的经验.

The paper is organized as follows. Section 2 describes how Megastore provides availability and scalability using partitioning and also justifies the sufficiency of our design for many interactive Internet applications. Section 3 provides an overview of Megastore's data model and features. Section 4 explains the replication algorithms in detail and gives some measurements on how they perform in practice. Section 5 summarizes our experience developing the system. We review related work in Section 6. Section 7 concludes.

下文安排如下: 第 2 节说明 Megastore 如何通过分区提高可用性与扩展能力, 并解释这种设计为何适用于许多交互式互联网应用; 第 3 节概述数据模型和功能; 第 4 节详述复制算法及其实际性能; 第 5 节总结开发经验; 第 6 节回顾相关工作; 第 7 节给出结论.

# 2. 迈向高可用与可扩展

In contrast to our need for a storage platform that is global, reliable, and arbitrarily large in scale, our hardware building blocks are geographically confined, failure-prone, and suffer limited capacity. We must bind these components into a unified ensemble offering greater throughput and reliability.

我们需要一套覆盖全球, 可靠且能持续扩展的存储平台, 但组成平台的机器各有容量上限, 也可能发生故障. 我们必须把这些分散的机器组织起来, 才能获得更高的吞吐量和可靠性.

To do so, we have taken a two-pronged approach. For availability, we implemented a synchronous, fault-tolerant log replicator optimized for long-distance links. For scale, we partitioned data into a vast space of small databases, each with its own replicated log stored in a per-replica NoSQL datastore.

我们从两方面解决这个问题. 为提高可用性, 实现了适合远距离链路的同步容错日志复制器. 为扩展规模, 将数据划分为大量小型数据库; 每个数据库都有独立的复制日志, 存放在各副本的 NoSQL 存储中.

## 2.1 复制

Replicating data across hosts within a single datacenter improves availability by overcoming host-specific failures, but with diminishing returns. We still must confront the networks that connect them to the outside world and the infrastructure that powers, cools, and houses them. Economically constructed sites risk some level of facility-wide outages<sup>[25]</sup> and are vulnerable to regional disasters. For cloud storage to meet availability demands, service providers must replicate data over a wide geographic area.

在一个数据中心内把数据复制到多台主机, 能抵御单机故障, 但副本增加到一定程度后, 收益会递减. 对外网络, 供电, 制冷和机房设施仍可能整体失效. 按合理成本建设的数据中心仍可能发生全站中断<sup>[25]</sup>, 也可能遭遇区域性灾害. 云存储要满足可用性要求, 就必须跨地域复制数据.

### 2.1.1 方案选择

We evaluated common strategies for wide-area replication:

我们评估了三种常见的广域复制方案:

- **Asynchronous Master/Slave.** A master node replicates write-ahead log entries to at least one slave. Log appends are acknowledged at the master in parallel with transmission to slaves. The master can support fast ACID transactions but risks downtime or data loss during failover to a slave. A consensus protocol is required to mediate mastership.
- **Synchronous Master/Slave.** A master waits for changes to be mirrored to slaves before acknowledging them, allowing failover without data loss. Master and slave failures need timely detection by an external system.
- **Optimistic Replication.** Any member of a homogeneous replica group can accept mutations<sup>[23]</sup>, which are asynchronously propagated through the group. Availability and latency are excellent. However, the global mutation ordering is not known at commit time, so transactions are impossible.

<!-- language-pair -->

- **异步主从复制.** 主节点向至少一个从节点复制预写日志, 但无需等待从节点收到日志, 就可以确认日志已追加. 这种方案支持快速 ACID 事务, 但切换到从节点时可能停机或丢失数据, 还需要共识协议裁定谁来担任主节点.
- **同步主从复制.** 主节点等变更复制到从节点后才确认, 因此故障切换不会丢失数据, 但需要外部系统及时检测主从节点故障.
- **乐观复制.** 一组地位相同的副本中, 任意副本都可以接受变更<sup>[23]</sup>, 然后异步传播给其他副本. 这种方案可用性高, 延迟低, 但提交时还无法确定变更的全局顺序, 因而不能提供事务.

We avoided strategies which could lose data on failures, which are common in large-scale systems. We also discarded strategies that do not permit ACID transactions. Despite the operational advantages of eventually consistent systems, it is currently too difficult to give up the read-modify-write idiom in rapid application development.

大型系统经常发生故障, 因此我们排除了可能在故障时丢失数据, 或无法提供 ACID 事务的方案. 最终一致性系统虽然便于运维, 但开发者要快速构建应用, 仍离不开读-修改-写这类操作.

We also discarded options with a heavyweight master. Failover requires a series of high-latency stages often causing a user-visible outage, and there is still a huge amount of complexity. Why build a fault-tolerant system to arbitrate mastership and failover workflows if we could avoid distinguished masters altogether?

我们也排除了依赖重量级主节点的方案. 主节点故障后, 切换要经历多个耗时的阶段, 往往会造成用户可感知的中断, 实现起来也很复杂. 既然可以不设专用主节点, 又何必再构建一套容错系统来裁定主节点归属和故障切换流程?

### 2.1.2 采用 Paxos

We decided to use Paxos, a proven, optimal, fault-tolerant consensus algorithm with no requirement for a distinguished master<sup>[14, 27]</sup>. We replicate a write-ahead log over a group of symmetric peers. Any node can initiate reads and writes. Each log append blocks on acknowledgments from a majority of replicas, and replicas in the minority catch up as they are able - the algorithm's inherent fault tolerance eliminates the need for a distinguished "failed" state. A novel extension to Paxos, detailed in Section 4.4.1, allows local reads at any up-to-date replica. Another extension permits single-roundtrip writes.

我们决定使用 Paxos<sup>[14, 27]</sup>. 它是经过验证的最优容错共识算法, 无须指定专用主节点. 预写日志复制到一组对等副本; 任意节点均可发起读写. **追加日志必须等待多数副本确认**, 落后的少数副本随后追赶. 算法内在的容错能力使系统无须定义独立的 "故障" 状态. 第 4.4.1 节介绍的 Paxos 扩展允许在任意最新副本上本地读取; 另一项扩展让写入只需一次往返.

Even with fault tolerance from Paxos, there are limitations to using a single log. With replicas spread over a wide area, communication latencies limit overall throughput. Moreover, progress is impeded when no replica is current or a majority fail to acknowledge writes. In a traditional SQL database hosting thousands or millions of users, using a synchronously replicated log would risk interruptions of widespread impact<sup>[11]</sup>. So to improve availability and throughput we use multiple replicated logs, each governing its own partition of the data set.

不过, Paxos 的容错能力并不能消除单条日志的局限. 副本分散在广域网中, 通信延迟会限制总吞吐量. 若没有任何副本处于最新状态, 或多数副本未确认写入, 日志也难以继续推进. 对服务成千上万乃至数百万用户的传统 SQL 数据库而言, 同步复制单条日志还可能造成影响广泛的中断<sup>[11]</sup>. 因此, 我们让多条复制日志分别管理不同的数据分区, 提高可用性和吞吐量.

## 2.2 分区与局部性

To scale our replication scheme and maximize performance of the underlying datastore, we give applications fine-grained control over their data's partitioning and locality.

为了让复制方案能够扩展, 同时充分利用底层存储的性能, 我们让应用自行决定数据如何分区, 哪些数据应当相邻存放.

### 2.2.1 实体组

To scale throughput and localize outages, we partition our data into a collection of entity groups<sup>[24]</sup>, each independently and synchronously replicated over a wide area. The underlying data is stored in a scalable NoSQL datastore in each datacenter (see Figure 1).

为提高吞吐量并限制故障影响范围, 我们把数据划分为多个实体组<sup>[24]</sup>. 每个实体组都独立地跨广域网同步复制, 其数据则存于各数据中心的可扩展 NoSQL 数据存储中 (见图 1).

Entities within an entity group are mutated with single-phase ACID transactions (for which the commit record is replicated via Paxos). Operations across entity groups could rely on expensive two-phase commits, but typically leverage Megastore's efficient asynchronous messaging. A transaction in a sending entity group places one or more messages in a queue; transactions in receiving entity groups atomically consume those messages and apply ensuing mutations.

**实体组内的变更通过单阶段 ACID 事务完成, 提交记录由 Paxos 复制.** 跨实体组操作可以采用成本较高的两阶段提交, 但通常使用 Megastore 的高效异步消息机制: 发送方实体组的事务将一条或多条消息放入队列; 接收方实体组在事务中取出消息, 并完成相应变更.

Note that we use asynchronous messaging between logically distant entity groups, not physically distant replicas. All network traffic between datacenters is from replicated operations, which are synchronous and consistent.

异步消息只用于逻辑上分离的实体组之间, 并不意味着地理上分散的副本采用异步复制. 跨数据中心的数据复制仍是同步且一致的.

Indexes local to an entity group obey ACID semantics; those across entity groups have looser consistency. See Figure 2 for the various operations on and between entity groups.

实体组内的索引遵循 ACID 语义, 跨实体组索引的一致性保证则较弱. 图 2 展示了组内和组间的各种操作.

### 2.2.2 选择实体组边界

The entity group defines the a priori grouping of data for fast operations. Boundaries that are too fine-grained force excessive cross-group operations, but placing too much unrelated data in a single group serializes unrelated writes, which degrades throughput.

实体组的边界决定哪些数据能放在一起快速处理. 分组太细, 跨组操作就会增多; 把太多无关数据放进同一组, 又会让原本无关的写入依次执行, 降低吞吐量.

The following examples show ways applications can work within these constraints:

**以下示例说明应用如何选择实体组边界:**

- **Email.** Each email account forms a natural entity group. Operations within an account are transactional and consistent: a user who sends or labels a message is guaranteed to observe the change despite possible failover to another replica. External mail routers handle communication between accounts.
- **Blogs.** A blogging application would be modeled with multiple classes of entity groups. Each user has a profile, which is naturally its own entity group. However, blogs are collaborative and have no single permanent owner. We create a second class of entity groups to hold the posts and metadata for each blog. A third class keys off the unique name claimed by each blog. The application relies on asynchronous messaging when a single user operation affects both blogs and profiles. For a lower-traffic operation like creating a new blog and claiming its unique name, two-phase commit is more convenient and performs adequately.
- **Maps.** Geographic data has no natural granularity of any consistent or convenient size. A mapping application can create entity groups by dividing the globe into non-overlapping patches. For mutations that span patches, the application uses two-phase commit to make them atomic. Patches must be large enough that two-phase transactions are uncommon, but small enough that each patch requires only a small write throughput. Unlike the previous examples, the number of entity groups does not grow with increased usage, so enough patches must be created initially for sufficient aggregate throughput at later scale.

<!-- language-pair -->

- **邮件.** 每个邮箱账户自然构成一个实体组. 同一账户内的操作有事务和一致性保证: 即使服务切换到另一个副本, 用户发出的邮件或新加的标签仍然可见. 账户之间的通信则由外部邮件路由器处理.
- **博客.** 每位用户的个人资料可以单独构成一个实体组. 协作博客没有固定的唯一所有者, 因此每个博客的文章和元数据构成另一类实体组, 每个博客独占的名称则对应第三类实体组. 如果一次操作同时影响博客和用户资料, 应用使用异步消息; 创建博客并占用名称这类不常发生的操作, 则可以用两阶段提交.
- **地图.** 地理数据没有天然且大小合适的划分粒度, 应用可将地球划为互不重叠的区域, 每个区域一个实体组. 跨区域变更使用两阶段提交保证原子性. 区域要足够大, 让跨组事务较少发生; 又要足够小, 使单个区域的写入吞吐需求不高. 与前两个示例不同, 区域数量不会随使用量增长, 因此一开始就要划分足够多的区域, 以保证日后的总吞吐量.

Nearly all applications built on Megastore have found natural ways to draw entity group boundaries.

使用 Megastore 的应用几乎都能根据自身数据找到合适的实体组边界.

### 2.2.3 物理布局

We use Google's Bigtable<sup>[15]</sup> for scalable fault-tolerant storage within a single datacenter, allowing us to support arbitrary read and write throughput by spreading operations across multiple rows.

在每个数据中心内, 我们使用 Google Bigtable<sup>[15]</sup> 提供可扩展的容错存储. 应用将操作分散到多行, 就能按需扩展读写吞吐量.

We minimize latency and maximize throughput by letting applications control the placement of data: through the selection of Bigtable instances and specification of locality within an instance.

应用可以选择存放数据的 Bigtable 实例, 也可以指定实例内哪些数据要相邻存放, 以降低延迟, 提高吞吐量.

To minimize latency, applications try to keep data near users and replicas near each other. They assign each entity group to the region or continent from which it is accessed most. Within that region they assign a triplet or quintuplet of replicas to datacenters with isolated failure domains.

为降低延迟, 应用尽量让数据靠近用户, 同时让副本彼此靠近. 每个实体组放在访问它最多的地区或大洲; 在该区域内, 三个或五个副本分别放在故障域相互隔离的数据中心.

For low latency, cache efficiency, and throughput, the data for an entity group are held in contiguous ranges of Bigtable rows. Our schema language lets applications control the placement of hierarchical data, storing data that is accessed together in nearby rows or denormalized into the same row.

实体组的数据存放在连续的 Bigtable 行中, 有利于降低延迟, 提高缓存效率和吞吐量. 模式语言让应用决定层次化数据如何放置: 一起访问的数据可以存于相邻行, 也可以合并到同一行.

# 3. Megastore 功能概览

Megastore maps this architecture onto a feature set carefully chosen to encourage rapid development of scalable applications. This section motivates the tradeoffs and describes the developer-facing features that result.

基于上述架构, Megastore 有选择地提供一组功能, 帮助开发者快速构建可扩展应用. 本节说明这些取舍和相应的功能.

## 3.1 API 设计理念

ACID transactions simplify reasoning about correctness, but it is equally important to be able to reason about performance. Megastore emphasizes cost-transparent APIs with runtime costs that match application developers' intuitions.

ACID 事务让开发者更容易推断程序是否正确, 但也要让他们能判断程序的性能. 因此, Megastore 尽量让 API 的运行开销符合开发者的预期.

Normalized relational schemas rely on joins at query time to service user operations. This is not the right model for Megastore applications for several reasons:

规范化的关系模式通常要在查询时连接多张表, 才能完成一次用户操作. 对 Megastore 应用而言, 这种方式并不合适:

- High-volume interactive workloads benefit more from predictable performance than from an expressive query language.
- Reads dominate writes in our target applications, so it pays to move work from read time to write time.
- Storing and querying hierarchical data is straightforward in key-value stores like Bigtable.

<!-- language-pair -->

- 高吞吐的交互式工作负载更需要可预测的性能, 而非表达力很强的查询语言.
- 目标应用的读取多于写入, 因此值得把部分工作从读取阶段提前到写入阶段.
- 在 Bigtable 等键值存储中存储和查询层次化数据比较直接.

With this in mind, we designed a data model and schema language to offer fine-grained control over physical locality. Hierarchical layouts and declarative denormalization help eliminate the need for most joins. Queries specify scans or lookups against particular tables and indexes.

因此, 我们设计了数据模型和模式语言, 让应用细粒度地控制数据的物理布局. 分层存放数据, 并在模式中声明非规范化结构, 可以避免大多数连接操作. 查询则明确指定要扫描或查找的表和索引.

Joins, when required, are implemented in application code. We provide an implementation of the merge phase of the merge join algorithm, in which the user provides multiple queries that return primary keys for the same table in the same order; we then return the intersection of keys for all the provided queries.

确实需要连接时, 由应用代码负责组织. Megastore 提供归并连接中的归并步骤: 开发者提交多个查询, 让它们以相同顺序返回同一张表的主键, 系统再求主键集合的交集.

We also have applications that implement outer joins with parallel queries. This typically involves an index lookup followed by parallel index lookups using the results of the initial lookup. We have found that when the secondary index lookups are done in parallel and the number of results from the first lookup is reasonably small, this provides an effective stand-in for SQL-style joins.

有些应用还通过并行查询实现外连接: 先查一次索引, 再利用查得的结果并行查询其他索引. 如果首次查询返回的结果不多, 并行查找二级索引就能有效替代 SQL 式连接.

While schema changes require corresponding modifications to the query implementation code, this system guarantees that features are built with a clear understanding of their performance implications. For example, when users (who may not have a background in databases) find themselves writing something that resembles a nested-loop join algorithm, they quickly realize that it's better to add an index and follow the index-based join approach above.

修改模式时, 查询代码也要相应调整. 这样一来, 开发者添加功能时就不得不考虑性能代价. 例如, 开发者即使没有数据库背景, 写出类似嵌套循环连接的代码后, 也会很快发现添加索引, 再按上述方式连接更合适.

## 3.2 数据模型

Megastore defines a data model that lies between the abstract tuples of an RDBMS and the concrete row-column storage of NoSQL. As in an RDBMS, the data model is declared in a schema and is strongly typed. Each schema has a set of tables, each containing a set of entities, which in turn contain a set of properties. Properties are named and typed values. The types can be strings, various flavors of numbers, or Google's Protocol Buffers<sup>[9]</sup>. They can be required, optional, or repeated (allowing a list of values in a single property). All entities in a table have the same set of allowable properties. A sequence of properties is used to form the primary key of the entity, and the primary keys must be unique within the table. Figure 3 shows an example schema for a simple photo storage application.

Megastore 的数据模型介于关系数据库的抽象元组与 NoSQL 的具体行列存储之间. 与关系数据库一样, 它用模式声明数据结构, 并规定属性类型. 一个模式包含多张表, 每张表包含若干实体, 每个实体又包含若干属性. 属性都有名称和类型, 可以是字符串, 数值或 Google Protocol Buffers<sup>[9]</sup>. 属性可设为必填, 可选或重复; 重复属性可保存多个值. 同一张表中的实体遵循相同的属性定义. 若干属性按顺序组成主键, 主键在表内必须唯一. 图 3 给出了照片存储应用的模式示例.

Megastore tables are either entity group root tables or child tables. Each child table must declare a single distinguished foreign key referencing a root table, illustrated by the ENTITY GROUP KEY annotation in Figure 3. Thus each child entity references a particular entity in its root table (called the root entity). An entity group consists of a root entity along with all entities in child tables that reference it. A Megastore instance can have several root tables, resulting in different classes of entity groups.

Megastore 的表分为实体组根表和子表. 每张子表都必须声明一个指向根表的专用外键, 图 3 用 ENTITY GROUP KEY 标出这个外键. 子表中的每个实体都关联到根表中的一个实体, 称为根实体. 一个根实体及引用它的全部子实体, 共同构成一个实体组. 同一个 Megastore 实例可以有多张根表, 因而也可以有不同类型的实体组.

In the example schema of Figure 3, each user's photo collection is a separate entity group. The root entity is the User, and the Photos are child entities. Note the Photo.tag field is repeated, allowing multiple tags per Photo without the need for a sub-table.

在图 3 的示例模式中, 每个用户的照片集构成一个实体组: 用户是根实体, 照片是子实体. Photo.tag 是重复属性, 因此每张照片可以有多个标签, 无需另外建立子表.

### 3.2.1 通过键预连接

While traditional relational modeling recommends that all primary keys take surrogate values, Megastore keys are chosen to cluster entities that will be read together. Each entity is mapped into a single Bigtable row; the primary key values are concatenated to form the Bigtable row key, and each remaining property occupies its own Bigtable column.

传统关系建模常建议使用代理键; Megastore 则选择能让相关实体相邻存放的主键, 方便一起读取. 每个实体映射为一行 Bigtable 数据, 主键属性的值拼接成行键, 其余属性各占一列.

Note how the Photo and User tables in Figure 3 share a common user id key prefix. The IN TABLE User directive instructs Megastore to colocate these two tables into the same Bigtable, and the key ordering ensures that Photo entities are stored adjacent to the corresponding User. This mechanism can be applied recursively to speed queries along arbitrary join depths. Thus, users can force hierarchical layout by manipulating the key order.

图 3 中, Photo 和 User 表的键都以用户 ID 开头. IN TABLE User 指令让两张表的数据存于同一个 Bigtable, 再借助键排序, 把照片放在对应用户附近. 这种做法可以递归应用, 加快多层关联查询. 应用因此能够通过主键顺序决定数据的层次布局.

Schemas declare keys to be sorted ascending or descending, or to avert sorting altogether: the SCATTER attribute instructs Megastore to prepend a two-byte hash to each key. Encoding monotonically increasing keys this way prevents hotspots in large data sets that span Bigtable servers.

模式可以指定键按升序或降序排列, 也可以用 SCATTER 属性打散键的顺序: Megastore 会在每个键前加上两字节哈希值. 这样, 即使键本身单调递增, 数据也不会集中到少数 Bigtable 服务器上形成热点.

### 3.2.2 索引

Secondary indexes can be declared on any list of entity properties, as well as fields within protocol buffers. We distinguish between two high-level classes of indexes: local and global (see Figure 2). A local index is treated as separate indexes for each entity group. It is used to find data within an entity group. In Figure 3, PhotosByTime is an example of a local index. The index entries are stored in the entity group and are updated atomically and consistently with the primary entity data.

可以为任意实体属性组合及 Protocol Buffers 内部字段声明二级索引. 索引分为本地与全局两类 (见图 2). 本地索引在各实体组内独立存在, 用于查找组内数据. 图 3 的 PhotosByTime 就是本地索引. 索引条目保存在实体组内, 与主实体数据一起原子更新并保持一致.

A global index spans entity groups. It is used to find entities without knowing in advance the entity groups that contain them. The PhotosByTag index in Figure 3 is global and enables discovery of photos marked with a given tag, regardless of owner. Global index scans can read data owned by many entity groups but are not guaranteed to reflect all recent updates.

全局索引跨越实体组, 用于在不知道实体属于哪个组时查找它. 图 3 中的 PhotosByTag 就是全局索引: 即使不知道照片属于谁, 也能按标签查找. **全局索引扫描可以跨多个实体组读取数据, 但不保证包含所有最近的更新.**

Megastore offers additional indexing features:

Megastore 提供额外的索引功能:

#### 3.2.2.1 STORING 子句

Accessing entity data through indexes is normally a two-step process: first the index is read to find matching primary keys, then these keys are used to fetch entities. We provide a way to denormalize portions of entity data directly into index entries. By adding the STORING clause to an index, applications can store additional properties from the primary table for faster access at read time. For example, the PhotosByTag index stores the photo thumbnail URL for faster retrieval without the need for an additional lookup.

通过索引读取实体通常分两步: 先查索引取得主键, 再用主键读取实体. STORING 子句允许把主表中的部分属性直接存入索引条目, 省去第二次查找. 例如, PhotosByTag 索引保存照片缩略图的 URL, 查询缩略图时无需再读取主表.

#### 3.2.2.2 重复索引

Megastore provides the ability to index repeated properties and protocol buffer sub-fields. Repeated indexes are an efficient alternative to child tables. PhotosByTag is a repeated index: each unique entry in the tag property causes one index entry to be created on behalf of the Photo.

Megastore 还支持为重复属性和 Protocol Buffer 子字段建立索引. 对重复属性建立索引, 可以比使用子表更高效. 例如, PhotosByTag 会为标签属性中的每个不同值, 分别创建一条指向照片的索引记录.

#### 3.2.2.3 内联索引

Inline indexes provide a way to denormalize data from source entities into a related target entity: index entries from the source entities appear as a virtual repeated column in the target entry. An inline index can be created on any table that has a foreign key referencing another table by using the first primary key of the target entity as the first components of the index, and physically locating the data in the same Bigtable as the target.

内联索引把源实体中的部分数据复制到关联的目标实体中, 在目标实体上形成一个虚拟的重复列. 如果一张表有指向另一张表的外键, 就可以创建内联索引: 以目标实体主键的首个组成部分作为索引前缀, 并把索引数据与目标实体存放在同一个 Bigtable 中.

Inline indexes are useful for extracting slices of information from child entities and storing the data in the parent for fast access. Coupled with repeated indexes, they can also be used to implement many-to-many relationships more ef- ficiently than by maintaining a many-to-many link table.

内联索引可以把子实体中的部分信息放到父实体附近, 方便快速读取. 如果再结合重复索引, 还可以比维护多对多关联表更高效地表示多对多关系.

The PhotosByTime index could have been implemented as an inline index into the parent User table. This would make the data accessible as a normal index or as a virtual repeated property on User, with a time-ordered entry for each contained Photo.

PhotosByTime 也可以做成父表 User 的内联索引. 这样既能像普通索引一样访问, 也能把它当作 User 的虚拟重复属性读取; 每张照片各有一条按时间排序的记录.

The Bigtable column name is a concatenation of the Megastore table name and the property name, allowing entities from different Megastore tables to be mapped into the same Bigtable row without collision. Figure 4 shows how data from the example photo application might look in Bigtable.

Bigtable 的列名由 Megastore 表名与属性名拼接而成. 因此, 不同 Megastore 表中的实体可以存入同一 Bigtable 行, 列名也不会冲突. 图 4 展示照片应用的数据在 Bigtable 中的存放方式.

Within the Bigtable row for a root entity, we store the transaction and replication metadata for the entity group, including the transaction log. Storing all metadata in a single Bigtable row allows us to update it atomically through a single Bigtable transaction.

根实体所在的 Bigtable 行还保存实体组的事务与复制元数据, 包括事务日志. 将全部元数据放在同一行, 使系统能够通过一次 Bigtable 事务原子地更新它们.

Each index entry is represented as a single Bigtable row; the row key of the cell is constructed using the indexed property values concatenated with the primary key of the indexed entity. For example, the PhotosByTime index row keys would be the tuple (user id; time; primary key) for each photo. Indexing repeated fields produces one index entry per repeated element. For example, the primary key for a photo with three tags would appear in the PhotosByTag index thrice.

每个索引条目存为一行 Bigtable 数据, 行键由索引属性值与对应实体的主键拼接而成. 例如, PhotosByTime 的行键包含 (用户 ID; 时间; 照片主键). 重复属性的每个值都会生成一条索引记录; 一张有三个标签的照片, 其主键就会在 PhotosByTag 中出现三次.

## 3.3 事务与并发控制

Each Megastore entity group functions as a mini-database that provides serializable ACID semantics. A transaction writes its mutations into the entity group's write-ahead log, then the mutations are applied to the data.

每个 Megastore 实体组都像一个小型数据库, 支持可串行化的 ACID 事务. 事务先将变更记入该组的预写日志, 再更新实体数据.

Bigtable provides the ability to store multiple values in the same row/column pair with different timestamps. We use this feature to implement multiversion concurrency control (MVCC): when mutations within a transaction are applied, the values are written at the timestamp of their transaction. Readers use the timestamp of the last fully applied transaction to avoid seeing partial updates. Readers and writers don't block each other, and reads are isolated from writes for the duration of a transaction.

Bigtable 允许同一个单元格按不同时间戳保存多个值, 我们据此实现多版本并发控制 (MVCC). 应用事务中的变更时, 系统按事务时间戳写入新值; 读取时则使用最近一次已完整应用的事务时间戳, 避免看到只完成了一部分的更新. 读取和写入互不阻塞, 事务执行期间也不会读到尚未提交的变更.

Megastore provides current, snapshot, and inconsistent reads. Current and snapshot reads are always done within the scope of a single entity group. When starting a current read, the transaction system first ensures that all previously committed writes are applied; then the application reads at the timestamp of the latest committed transaction. For a snapshot read, the system picks up the timestamp of the last known fully applied transaction and reads from there, even if some committed transactions have not yet been applied. Megastore also provides inconsistent reads, which ignore the state of the log and read the latest values directly. This is useful for operations that have more aggressive latency requirements and can tolerate stale or partially applied data.

Megastore 支持当前读, 快照读和不一致读. 前两种读取都只针对单个实体组. 当前读开始时, 系统先确保此前提交的写入全部应用, 再以最近一次已提交事务的时间戳读取数据. 快照读则使用最后一个已知已完整应用的事务时间戳, 即使还有已提交但尚未应用的事务, 也不等待它们. 不一致读忽略日志状态, 直接读取最新值; 适用于更在意延迟, 且能容忍旧数据或部分更新结果的操作.

A write transaction always begins with a current read to determine the next available log position. The commit operation gathers mutations into a log entry, assigns it a timestamp higher than any previous one, and appends it to the log using Paxos. The protocol uses optimistic concurrency: though multiple writers might be attempting to write to the same log position, only one will win. The rest will notice the victorious write, abort, and retry their operations. Advisory locking is available to reduce the effects of contention. Batching writes through session affinity to a particular front-end server can avoid contention altogether.

写事务总是先执行当前读, 找到下一个可用的日志位置. 提交时, 系统将变更整理成日志条目, 为其分配一个大于此前所有时间戳的新时间戳, 再用 Paxos 将条目追加到日志. 这里采用乐观并发控制: 多个写入者可能争夺同一位置, 但只有一个能成功; 其余写入者发现已有事务胜出后, 必须中止并重试. 系统提供建议性锁来减轻竞争. 如果将同一会话的请求交给固定的前端服务器, 并批量处理写入, 甚至可以完全避免竞争.

The complete transaction lifecycle is as follows:

**一个事务依次经历以下阶段:**

1. **Read:** Obtain the timestamp and log position of the last committed transaction.
2. **Application logic:** Read from Bigtable and gather writes into a log entry.
3. **Commit:** Use Paxos to achieve consensus for appending that entry to the log.
4. **Apply:** Write mutations to the entities and indexes in Bigtable.
5. **Clean up:** Delete data that is no longer required. The write operation can return to the client at any point after Commit, though it makes a best-effort attempt to wait for the nearest replica to apply.

<!-- language-pair -->

1. **读取:** 取得最近一次已提交事务的时间戳和日志位置.
2. **执行业务逻辑:** 从 Bigtable 读取数据, 将待写入的变更收集成日志条目.
3. **提交:** 通过 Paxos 达成共识, 把日志条目追加到复制日志.
4. **应用:** 将变更写入 Bigtable 中的实体和索引.
5. **清理:** 删除不再需要的数据. 系统提交后即可向客户端返回成功, 但会尽力等附近的副本应用变更.

### 3.3.1 队列

Queues provide transactional messaging between entity groups. They can be used for cross-group operations, to batch multiple updates into a single transaction, or to defer work. A transaction on an entity group can atomically send or receive multiple messages in addition to updating its entities. Each message has a single sending and receiving entity group; if they differ, delivery is asynchronous. (See Figure 2.)

队列在实体组之间传递事务性消息, 既可用于跨组操作, 也可将多个更新合并到一个事务, 或把操作留待稍后处理. 一个实体组的事务除了更新实体, 还能原子地发送或接收多条消息. 每条消息都有发送方和接收方实体组; 如果不是同一组, 消息便异步送达 (见图 2).

Queues offer a way to perform operations that affect many entity groups. For example, consider a calendar application in which each calendar has a distinct entity group, and we want to send an invitation to a group of calendars. A single transaction can atomically send invitation queue messages to many distinct calendars. Each calendar receiving the message will process the invitation in its own transaction which updates the invitee's state and deletes the message.

队列也能处理涉及多个实体组的操作. 假设一个日历应用把每个日历划为独立实体组, 现在要向多个日历发送邀请. 应用只需执行一次事务, 就能原子地发出所有邀请消息. 收到消息后, 各日历再分别通过自己的事务处理邀请, 更新受邀者状态并删除消息.

There is a long history of message queues in full-featured RDBMSs. Our support is notable for its scale: declaring a queue automatically creates an inbox on each entity group, giving us millions of endpoints.

成熟的关系数据库早就支持消息队列. Megastore 的特点在于规模: 声明一个队列, 系统就会为每个实体组自动创建收件箱, 从而提供数百万个消息端点.

### 3.3.2 两阶段提交

Megastore supports two-phase commit for atomic updates across entity groups. Since these transactions have much higher latency and increase the risk of contention, we generally discourage applications from using the feature in favor of queues. Nevertheless, they can be useful in simplifying application code for unique secondary key enforcement.

Megastore 也支持用两阶段提交原子地更新多个实体组. 这类事务延迟更高, 发生竞争的可能性也更大, 因此我们通常建议应用优先使用队列. 但如果需要保证二级键唯一, 两阶段提交仍能简化应用代码.

## 3.4 其他功能

We have built a tight integration with Bigtable's full-text index in which updates and searches participate in Megastore's transactions and multiversion concurrency. A full-text index declared in a Megastore schema can index a table's text or other application-generated attributes.

Megastore 与 Bigtable 的全文索引紧密集成. 更新和搜索全文索引时, 也使用 Megastore 的事务及多版本并发控制机制. 开发者可在 Megastore 模式中声明全文索引, 为表中的文本或应用生成的其他属性建立索引.

Synchronous replication is sufficient defense against the most common corruptions and accidents, but backups can be invaluable in cases of programmer or operator error. Megastore's integrated backup system supports periodic full snapshots as well as incremental backup of transaction logs. The restore process can bring back an entity group's state to any point in time, optionally omitting selected log entries (as after accidental deletes). The backup system complies with legal and common sense principles for expiring deleted data.

同步复制可以防范最常见的数据损坏和意外事故, 但程序或运维人员操作失误时, 仍需要备份. Megastore 的备份系统既支持定期生成完整快照, 也支持增量备份事务日志. 恢复时, 可以将实体组还原到任意时间点; 如果某条日志记录了一次误删, 也可以在恢复时跳过它. 备份系统还会按照法律要求和合理的保留期限, 清理已删除的数据.

Applications have the option of encrypting data at rest, including the transaction logs. Encryption uses a distinct key per entity group. We avoid granting the same operators access to both the encryption keys and the encrypted data.

应用程序可以选择加密静态数据, 包括事务日志. 加密对每个实体组使用不同的密钥. 我们避免授予相同的操作员访问加密密钥和加密数据的权限.

# 4. 复制

This section details the heart of our synchronous replication scheme: a low-latency implementation of Paxos. We discuss operational details and present some measurements of our production service.

本节详述同步复制方案的核心: 如何用 Paxos 实现低延迟复制. 我们还讨论运维细节, 并给出生产环境中的测量结果.

## 4.1 概览

Megastore's replication system provides a single, consistent view of the data stored in its underlying replicas. Reads and writes can be initiated from any replica, and ACID semantics are preserved regardless of what replica a client starts from. Replication is done per entity group by synchronously replicating the group's transaction log to a quorum of replicas. Writes typically require one round of inter- datacenter communication, and healthy-case reads run locally. Current reads have the following guarantees:

Megastore 的复制系统让多个副本对外呈现一致的数据视图. 客户端可以从任意副本发起读写, 获得相同的 ACID 保证. 每个实体组独立复制, 其事务日志同步写入多数副本. 写入通常只需一次跨数据中心往返; 副本状态正常时, 读取可以在本地完成. **当前读提供以下保证:**

- A read always observes the last-acknowledged write.
- After a write has been observed, all future reads observe that write. (A write might be observed before it is acknowledged.)

<!-- language-pair -->

- 每次读取都能看到最近一次已确认的写入.
- 一旦某次读取看到了某次写入的结果, 之后的读取也都会看到它. 读取有可能先于写入确认看到结果.

## 4.2 Paxos 简述

The Paxos algorithm is a way to reach consensus among a group of replicas on a single value. It tolerates delayed or reordered messages and replicas that fail by stopping. A majority of replicas must be active and reachable for the algorithm to make progress - that is, it allows up to F faults with 2F + 1 replicas. Once a value is chosen by a majority, all future attempts to read or write the value will reach the same outcome.

Paxos 让一组副本对一个值达成共识. 即使消息延迟, 到达顺序改变, 或有副本停止运行, 算法仍能工作. 不过, 要继续推进, 多数副本必须正常运行并能相互通信; 2F + 1 个副本最多可容忍 F 个故障. 一旦多数副本选定某个值, 此后无论读取还是尝试写入, 都不会改变这个结果.

The ability to determine the outcome of a single value by itself is not of much use to a database. Databases typically use Paxos to replicate a transaction log, where a separate instance of Paxos is used for each position in the log. New values are written to the log at the position following the last chosen position.

只对一个值达成共识, 对数据库的帮助有限. 因此, 数据库通常用 Paxos 复制事务日志: 每个日志位置运行一个独立的 Paxos 实例, 新值写入最后一个已选定位置之后.

The original Paxos algorithm<sup>[27]</sup> is ill-suited for high-latency network links because it demands multiple rounds of communication. Writes require at least two inter-replica roundtrips before consensus is achieved: a round of prepares, which reserves the right for a subsequent round of accepts. Reads require at least one round of prepares to determine the last chosen value. Real world systems built on Paxos reduce the number of roundtrips required to make it a practical algorithm. We will first review how master-based systems use Paxos, and then explain how we make Paxos efficient.

原始 Paxos 算法<sup>[27]</sup> 需要多轮通信, 不适合高延迟链路. 一次写入至少要在副本之间往返两次: 先执行 Prepare, 获得随后执行 Accept 的权利, 才能达成共识. 读取也至少需要一轮 Prepare, 才能确定最后选定的值. 实际系统通常会减少通信往返, 使 Paxos 适合生产环境. 下文先回顾基于主节点的做法, 再介绍 Megastore 的优化.

## 4.3 基于主节点的方案

To minimize latency, many systems use a dedicated master to which all reads and writes are directed. The master participates in all writes, so its state is always up-to-date. It can serve reads of the current consensus state without any network communication. Writes are reduced to a single round of communication by piggybacking a prepare for the next write on each accept<sup>[14]</sup>. The master can batch writes together to improve throughput.

为降低延迟, 许多系统指定一个主节点, 所有读写都发往它. 主节点参与每次写入, 状态始终最新, 因而无需网络通信就能读取当前共识状态. 每次接受值时顺带准备下一次写入<sup>[14]</sup>, 可把写入减少到一轮通信. 主节点还能批量处理写入以提高吞吐量.

Reliance on a master limits flexibility for reading and writing. Transaction processing must be done near the master replica to avoid accumulating latency from sequential reads. Any potential master replica must have adequate resources for the system's full workload; slave replicas waste resources until the moment they become master. Master failover can require a complicated state machine, and a series of timers must elapse before service is restored. It is difficult to avoid user-visible outages.

主节点也限制了读写可以从哪里发起. 事务处理必须尽量靠近主节点, 否则多次连续读取会累积网络延迟. 每个可能接任主节点的副本, 都得预留足以承载全部负载的资源; 在真正接任之前, 从副本却无法充分利用这些资源. 主节点故障切换还可能涉及复杂的状态机, 并等待多个计时器到期, 很难避免用户可感知的中断.

## 4.4 Megastore 的方案

In this section we discuss the optimizations and innovations that make Paxos practical for our system.

本节介绍让 Paxos 适用于 Megastore 的改进.

### 4.4.1 快速读

We set an early requirement that current reads should usually execute on any replica without inter-replica RPCs. Since writes usually succeed on all replicas, it was realistic to allow local reads everywhere. These local reads give us better utilization, low latencies in all regions, fine-grained read failover, and a simpler programming experience.

我们从设计之初就要求: **当前读通常应能从任意副本完成, 无需副本间 RPC.** 大多数写入都会在所有副本上成功, 因此各地副本通常都能在本地提供读取. 这样既提高了资源利用率, 降低了各区域的读取延迟, 也方便在读取失败时切换副本, 并简化应用开发.

We designed a service called the Coordinator, with servers in each replica's datacenter. A coordinator server tracks a set of entity groups for which its replica has observed all Paxos writes. For entity groups in that set, the replica has sufficient state to serve local reads.

为此, 我们设计了 Coordinator 服务, 并在每个副本所在的数据中心部署服务器. 协调器记录哪些实体组在本地副本上已经包含全部 Paxos 写入; 这些实体组可以直接在本地读取.

It is the responsibility of the write algorithm to keep coordinator state conservative. If a write fails on a replica's Bigtable, it cannot be considered committed until the group's key has been evicted from that replica's coordinator.

写入算法必须确保协调器不会误把落后的副本标记为最新. 如果某副本写入 Bigtable 失败, 必须先从该副本的协调器中移除对应实体组的键, 才能把本次写入视为已提交.

Since coordinators are simple, they respond more reliably and quickly than Bigtable. Handling of rare failure cases or network partitions is described in Section 4.7.

协调器逻辑简单, 因而响应通常比 Bigtable 更快, 也更可靠. 第 4.7 节说明如何处理少见的故障和网络分区.

### 4.4.2 快速写

To achieve fast single-roundtrip writes, Megastore adapts the pre-preparing optimization used by master-based approaches. In a master-based system, each successful write includes an implied prepare message granting the master the right to issue accept messages for the next log position. If the write succeeds, the prepares are honored, and the next write skips directly to the accept phase. Megastore does not use dedicated masters, but instead uses leaders.

为实现 **只需一次往返的快速写入**, Megastore 借鉴了主节点方案中的预准备优化. 在主节点方案中, 每次成功写入都隐含一次 Prepare, 授予主节点在下一日志位置直接发送 Accept 消息的权利. 写入成功后, 下一次写入便可跳过 Prepare, 直接进入 Accept 阶段. Megastore 不设专用主节点, 而是为每个日志位置选出领导者.

We run an independent instance of the Paxos algorithm for each log position. The leader for each log position is a distinguished replica chosen alongside the preceding log position's consensus value. The leader arbitrates which value may use proposal number zero. The first writer to submit a value to the leader wins the right to ask all replicas to accept that value as proposal number zero. All other writers must fall back on two-phase Paxos.

每个日志位置都运行一个独立的 Paxos 实例. 前一位置达成共识时, 系统一并选出下一位置的领导者, 由它裁定哪个值可以使用编号为 0 的提案. 第一个向领导者提交值的写入者, 可以请求所有副本以 0 号提案接受该值; 其他写入者则必须退回两阶段 Paxos.

Since a writer must communicate with the leader before submitting the value to other replicas, we minimize writerleader latency. We designed our policy for selecting the next write's leader around the observation that most applications submit writes from the same region repeatedly. This leads to a simple but effective heuristic: use the closest replica.

写入者向其他副本提交值之前, 要先与领导者通信. 大多数应用会连续从同一地区发起写入, 因此我们选择距离写入者最近的副本担任下一位置的领导者, 尽量缩短这段通信的延迟.

### 4.4.3 副本类型

So far all replicas have been full replicas, meaning they contain all the entity and index data and are able to service current reads. We also support the notion of a witness replica. Witnesses vote in Paxos rounds and store the write-ahead log, but do not apply the log and do not store entity data or indexes, so they have lower storage costs. They are effectively tie breakers and are used when there are not enough full replicas to form a quorum. Because they do not have a coordinator, they do not force an additional roundtrip when they fail to acknowledge a write.

前文讨论的都是完整副本: 它们保存全部实体和索引数据, 能处理当前读. Megastore 还支持仲裁副本 (witness replica). 仲裁副本参与 Paxos 投票并保存预写日志, 但不应用日志, 也不保存实体与索引数据, 因而存储成本较低. 当完整副本不足以组成多数派时, 仲裁副本可以补足票数. 它没有协调器, 所以即使没有确认某次写入, 也不会因此增加一次通信往返.

Read-only replicas are the inverse of witnesses: they are non-voting replicas that contain full snapshots of the data. Reads at these replicas reflect a consistent view of some point in the recent past. For reads that can tolerate this staleness, read-only replicas help disseminate data over a wide geographic area without impacting write latency.

只读副本与仲裁副本相反: 它们不参与投票, 但保存完整的数据快照. 从只读副本读取, 得到的是近期某个时刻的一致数据视图. 如果应用能接受数据略有滞后, 就可以通过只读副本在更多地区提供读取, 而不会增加写入延迟.

## 4.6 数据结构与算法

This section details data structures and algorithms required to make the leap from consensus on a single value to a functioning replicated log.

本节介绍如何用数据结构与算法, 把针对单个值的共识扩展为可用的复制日志.

### 4.6.1 复制日志

Each replica stores mutations and metadata for the log entries known to the group. To ensure that a replica can participate in a write quorum even as it recovers from previous outages, we permit replicas to accept out-of-order proposals. We store log entries as independent cells in Bigtable.

每个副本都保存自己已知的日志条目, 以及相应的变更和元数据. 副本可以不按日志位置顺序接受提案, 因此即使还在从故障中恢复, 也能参与写入所需的多数派. 每个日志条目都单独存入 Bigtable 单元格.

We refer to a log replica as having "holes" when it contains an incomplete prefix of the log. Log position 101 was accepted by all replicas. Log position 102 found a bare quorum in A and C. Position 103 is noteworthy for having been accepted by A and C, leaving B with a hole at 103. A conflicting write attempt has occurred at position 104 on replica A and B preventing consensus.

如果日志副本缺少前缀中的某些位置, 我们称这些位置为 "空洞". 图 6 展示了同一 Megastore 实体组的几个日志副本. 位置 0-99 已完全清理; 位置 100 只清理了一部分, 因为各副本已获知其他副本不会再向自己索取这些位置的内容. 位置 101 被全部副本接受. 位置 102 仅被 A 和 C 接受, 刚好达到多数派. 位置 103 也被 A 和 C 接受, B 则在该位置留下空洞. 位置 104 上, A 和 B 发起了相互冲突的写入, 尚未达成共识.

### 4.6.2 读操作

In preparation for a current read (as well as before a write), at least one replica must be brought up to date: all mutations previously committed to the log must be copied to and applied on that replica. We call this process catchup.

执行当前读或开始写入前, 至少要让一个副本追上进度: 把此前已提交到日志的变更复制过去, 并应用到该副本的数据中. 我们称这个过程为追赶.

Omitting some deadline management, the algorithm for a current read (shown in Figure 7) is as follows:

![图 7: 本地副本 A 上的读取时间线](/assets/images/posts/megastore/figure-7.png)

</div>

<span class="paper-caption paper-lang-en" lang="en">Figure 7: Timeline for reads with local replica A</span>

1. **Query Local:** Query the local replica's coordinator to determine if the entity group is up-to-date locally.
2. **Find Position:** Determine the highest possibly-committed log position, and select a replica that has ap- plied through that log position. (a) (Local read) If step 1 indicates that the local replica is up-to-date, read the highest accepted log position and timestamp from the local replica. (b) (Majority read) If the local replica is not up-todate (or if step 1 or step 2a times out), read from a majority of replicas to find the maximum log position that any replica has seen, and pick a replica to read from. We select the most responsive or up-to-date replica, not always the local replica.
3. **Catchup:** As soon as a replica is selected, catch it up to the maximum known log position as follows: (a) For each log position in which the selected replica does not know the consensus value, read the value from another replica. For any log positions without a known-committed value available, invoke Paxos to propose a no-op write. Paxos will drive a majority of replicas to converge on a single value - either the no-op or a previously proposed write. (b) Sequentially apply the consensus value of all unapplied log positions to advance the replica's state to the distributed consensus state. In the event of failure, retry on another replica.
4. **Validate:** If the local replica was selected and was not previously up-to-date, send the coordinator a validate message asserting that the (entity group; replica) pair reflects all committed writes. Do not wait for a reply - if the request fails, the next read will retry.
5. **Query Data:** Read the selected replica using the timestamp of the selected log position. If the selected replica becomes unavailable, pick an alternate replica, perform catchup, and read from it instead. The results of a single large query may be assembled transparently from multiple replicas.

1. **检查本地:** 向本地副本的协调器查询, 判断该实体组的本地数据是否已追上最新写入.
2. **确定日志位置和读取副本:** 找出可能已提交的最高日志位置, 选择一个已应用到该位置的副本. (a) 若步骤 1 表明本地副本为最新, 就从它读取已接受的最高日志位置及时间戳. (b) 若本地副本并非最新, 或步骤 1, 步骤 2(a) 超时, 就查询多数副本, 找出它们已见过的最高日志位置. 随后选择响应最快或进度最靠前的副本读取, 不一定选本地副本.
3. **追赶:** 让选定副本追到已知的最高日志位置. 对该副本尚不知道共识结果的位置, 从其他副本查询; 如果还没有已知的提交值, 就通过 Paxos 发起一项不修改数据的写入提案. 多数副本最终会在这项提案与此前的写入提案之间选定一个值. 然后按日志顺序应用尚未处理的值. 若追赶失败, 改选另一副本重试.
4. **校验:** 如果选定的本地副本此前并非最新, 就向协调器发送校验消息, 告知它该实体组在此副本上已包含所有已提交写入. 无须等待回复; 如果消息未送达, 下次读取会重新校验.
5. **查询数据:** 按所选日志位置的时间戳, 从选定副本读取. 如果该副本不可用, 就改选一个副本, 先让它追上最新状态, 再执行读取. 大型查询的结果可以由多个副本共同提供, 对客户端呈现为一次查询.

Note that in practice 1 and 2a are executed in parallel.

实际执行时, 步骤 1 与步骤 2(a) 并行进行.

### 4.6.3 写操作

Having completed the read algorithm, Megastore observes the next unused log position, the timestamp of the last write, and the next leader replica. At commit time all pending changes to the state are packaged and proposed, with a timestamp and next leader nominee, as the consensus value for the next log position. If this value wins the distributed consensus, it is applied to the state at all full replicas; otherwise the entire transaction is aborted and must be retried from the beginning of the read phase.

完成读取阶段后, Megastore 就知道下一个空闲的日志位置, 最近一次写入的时间戳, 以及该位置的领导者副本. 提交时, 系统将待写入的变更, 新时间戳和下一任领导者候选副本打包成一个值, 提议将它写入该日志位置. 如果这个值赢得多数副本的共识, 系统就把变更应用到所有完整副本; 否则中止整个事务, 从读取阶段重新开始.

As described above, coordinators keep track of the entity groups that are up-to-date in their replica. If a write is not accepted on a replica, we must remove the entity group's key from that replica's coordinator. This process is called invalidation. Before a write is considered committed and ready to apply, all full replicas must have accepted or had their coordinator invalidated for that entity group.

如前所述, 协调器记录本地副本中哪些实体组的数据是最新的. 如果某副本没有接受一次写入, 就必须从它的协调器中移除对应实体组的键, 这个过程称为失效处理. 只有确认每个完整副本都已接受写入, 或已经完成相应的失效处理, 系统才能将这次写入视为已提交并开始应用变更.

The write algorithm (shown in Figure 8) is as follows:

1. **Accept Leader:** Ask the leader to accept the value as proposal number zero. If successful, skip to step 3.
2. **Prepare:** Run the Paxos Prepare phase at all replicas with a higher proposal number than any seen so far at this log position. Replace the value being written with the highest-numbered proposal discovered, if any.
3. **Accept:** Ask remaining replicas to accept the value. If this fails on a majority of replicas, return to step 2 after a randomized backoff.
4. **Invalidate:** Invalidate the coordinator at all full replicas that did not accept the value. Fault handling at this step is described in Section 4.7 below.
5. **Apply:** Apply the value's mutations at as many replicas as possible. If the chosen value differs from that originally proposed, return a conflict error.

<!-- language-pair -->

1. **领导者接受:** 请求领导者以编号 0 接受该提案值. 如果成功, 跳至步骤 3.
2. **准备:** 选择一个高于该日志位置所有现有提案的编号, 在全部副本上执行 Paxos 的 Prepare 阶段. 如果发现其他提案, 就改用其中编号最高的提案值.
3. **接受:** 请求其余副本接受该值. 如果未获得多数副本的同意, 就随机等待一段时间, 再返回步骤 2.
4. **失效处理:** 对所有未接受该值的完整副本, 将其协调器中的相应实体组标记为失效. 本步骤的故障处理见第 4.7 节.
5. **应用:** 在尽可能多的副本上应用该值对应的变更. 如果最终选定的值与最初提议的值不同, 返回冲突错误.

Step 1 implements the "fast writes" of Section 4.4.2. Writers using single-phase Paxos skip Prepare messages by sending an Accept command at proposal number zero. The next leader replica selected at log position n arbitrates the value used for proposal zero at n + 1. Since multiple proposers may submit values with proposal number zero, serializing at this replica ensures only one value corresponds with that proposal number for a particular log position.

第 1 步实现了第 4.4.2 节所述的 "快速写入". 使用单阶段 Paxos 时, 写入者直接发送编号为 0 的 Accept 请求, 跳过准备阶段. 在日志位置 n 选出的下一任领导者, 负责裁定位置 n + 1 上编号为 0 的提案值. 即使多个提议者都提交编号为 0 的提案, 领导者也会将它们串行处理, 保证该日志位置的这一提案编号只对应一个值.

In a traditional database system, the commit point (when the change is durable) is the same as the visibility point (when reads can see a change and when a writer can be notified of success). In our write algorithm, the commit point is after step 3 when the write has won the Paxos round, but the visibility point is after step 4. Only after all full replicas have accepted or had their coordinators invalidated can the write be acknowledged and the changes applied. Acknowledging before step 4 could violate our consistency guarantees: a current read at a replica whose invalidation was skipped might fail to observe the acknowledged write.

在传统数据库中, 变更持久化时, 读者就能看到变更, 写入者也能收到成功通知; 提交点与可见点重合. 但在这里, 写入赢得 Paxos 共识的第 3 步只是提交点, 第 4 步完成后才是可见点. 系统必须确认所有完整副本都接受了写入, 或其协调器中的记录已失效, 才能告知写入者成功并应用变更. 如果在第 4 步之前确认, 尚未失效的副本就可能漏掉这次已确认的写入, 破坏一致性保证.

## 4.7 协调器可用性

Coordinator processes run in each datacenter and keep state only about their local replica. In the write algorithm above, each full replica must either accept or have its coordinator invalidated, so it might appear that any single replica failure (Bigtable and coordinator) will cause unavailability.

每个数据中心都有协调器进程, 只记录本地副本的状态. 按照上述写入算法, 每个完整副本要么接受写入, 要么使协调器中的相应记录失效. 乍看之下, 只要一个副本的 Bigtable 和协调器同时故障, 整个系统就会不可用.

In practice this is not a common problem. The coordinator is a simple process with no external dependencies and no persistent storage, so it tends to be much more stable than a Bigtable server. Nevertheless, network and host failures can still make the coordinator unavailable.

实际运行中, 这种情况并不常见. 协调器进程很简单, 不依赖外部服务, 也不使用持久存储, 因此通常比 Bigtable 服务器更稳定. 不过, 网络或主机故障仍可能使它不可用.

### 4.7.1 故障检测

To address network partitions, coordinators use an out-of-band protocol to identify when other coordinators are up, healthy, and generally reachable.

为了应对网络分区, 协调器通过带外协议检查其他协调器是否仍在运行, 状态是否正常, 网络是否可达.

We use Google's Chubby lock service<sup>[13]</sup>: coordinators obtain specific Chubby locks in remote datacenters at startup. To process requests, a coordinator must hold a majority of its locks. If it ever loses a majority of its locks from a crash or network partition, it will revert its state to a conservative default, considering all entity groups in its purview to be out-of-date. Subsequent reads at the replica must query the log position from a majority of replicas until the locks are regained and its coordinator entries are revalidated.

我们使用 Google 的 Chubby 锁服务<sup>[13]</sup>. 协调器启动时, 会在远端数据中心获取指定的 Chubby 锁; 只有持有多数锁, 才能处理请求. 如果崩溃或网络分区导致它失去多数锁, 它就把自己负责的全部实体组标记为非最新. 此后, 该副本上的读取必须向多数副本查询日志位置, 直到协调器重新取得锁并校验记录.

Writers are insulated from coordinator failure by testing whether a coordinator has lost its locks: in that scenario, a writer knows that the coordinator will consider itself invalidated upon regaining them.

写入者会检查协调器是否失去锁, 因而不必一直等待故障协调器恢复: 如果锁已丢失, 写入者就知道该协调器重新取得锁后会把自身状态视为失效.

This algorithm risks a brief (tens of seconds) write outage when a datacenter containing live coordinators suddenly becomes unavailable - all writers must wait for the coordinator's Chubby locks to expire before writes can complete (much like waiting for a master failover to trigger). Unlike after a master failover, reads and writes can proceed smoothly while the coordinator's state is reconstructed. This brief and rare outage risk is more than justified by the steady state of fast local reads it allows.

如果运行协调器的数据中心突然不可用, 写入可能暂停数十秒: 所有写入者都得等该协调器的 Chubby 锁过期, 类似等待主节点故障切换. 但协调器重建状态期间, 读写仍可继续. 系统以这种短暂且少见的中断风险, 换取正常情况下快速本地读取的能力.

The coordinator liveness protocol is vulnerable to asymmetric network partitions. If a coordinator can maintain the leases on its Chubby locks, but has otherwise lost contact with proposers, then affected entity groups will experience a write outage. In this scenario an operator performs a manual step to disable the partially isolated coordinator. We have faced this condition only a handful of times.

非对称网络分区仍可能让存活检测失效: 协调器可以续租 Chubby 锁, 却无法与提议者通信, 导致相关实体组无法写入. 此时, 运维人员需要手动禁用这个与系统部分失联的协调器. 我们只遇到过少数几次这种情况.

### 4.7.2 校验竞态

In addition to availability issues, protocols for reading and writing to the coordinator must contend with a variety of race conditions. Invalidate messages are always safe, but validate messages must be handled with care. Races between validates for earlier writes and invalidates for later writes are protected in the coordinator by always sending the log position associated with the action. Higher numbered invalidates always trump lower numbered validates. There are also races associated with a crash between an invalidate by a writer at position n and a validate at some position m < n. We detect crashes using a unique epoch number for each incarnation of the coordinator: validates are only allowed to modify the coordinator state if the epoch remains unchanged since the most recent read of the coordinator.

协调器除了要保持可用, 还必须正确处理读写消息之间的竞态. Invalidate 消息总是安全的, Validate 消息则需要格外小心. 一次较早写入的 Validate, 可能与较晚写入的 Invalidate 交错到达. 因此, 每条消息都携带对应的日志位置; 较高位置的 Invalidate 总能覆盖较低位置的 Validate. 另一种竞态发生在位置 n 的写入者发送 Invalidate 后: 协调器可能在处理位置 m (m < n) 的 Validate 前后崩溃. 为识别这类重启, 系统给协调器的每次启动分配唯一的 epoch 编号. 只有从上次读取协调器到发送 Validate 期间, epoch 始终未变, 这条 Validate 才能修改协调器状态.

In summary, using coordinators to allow fast local reads from any datacenter is not free in terms of the impact to availability. But in practice most of the problems with running the coordinator are mitigated by the following factors:

总的来说, 协调器让各数据中心都能快速读取本地副本, 但也会影响可用性. **实际运行中, 以下因素缓解了大部分问题:**

- Coordinators are much simpler processes than Bigtable servers, have many fewer dependencies, and are thus naturally more available.
- Coordinators' simple, homogeneous workload makes them cheap and predictable to provision.
- Coordinators' light network traffic allows using a high network QoS with reliable connectivity.
- Operators can centrally disable coordinators for maintenance or unhealthy periods. This is automatic for certain monitoring signals.
- A quorum of Chubby locks detects most network partitions and node unavailability.

<!-- language-pair -->

- 协调器比 Bigtable 服务器简单, 依赖也少得多, 因而通常更稳定.
- 协调器处理的任务简单且一致, 部署成本低, 所需资源也容易预估.
- 协调器的网络流量很小, 因此可以采用高 QoS 的可靠网络连接.
- 运维人员可以在维护或运行异常时集中禁用协调器; 某些监控信号还能自动触发禁用.
- 多数派 Chubby 锁可以检测大多数网络分区和节点故障.

## 4.8 写入吞吐量

Our implementation of Paxos has interesting tradeoffs in system behavior. Application servers in multiple datacenters may initiate writes to the same entity group and log position simultaneously. All but one of them will fail and need to retry their transactions. The increased latency imposed by synchronous replication increases the likelihood of conflicts for a given per-entity-group commit rate.

Paxos 实现也有代价. 不同数据中心的应用服务器可能同时写入同一实体组的同一日志位置. 其中只有一个能成功, 其他事务必须重试. 同步复制拉长了写入时间, 因此在实体组提交速率不变时, 冲突更容易发生.

Limiting that rate to a few writes per second per entity group yields insignificant conflict rates. For apps whose entities are manipulated by a small number of users at a time, this limitation is generally not a concern. Most of our target customers scale write throughput by sharding entity groups more finely or by ensuring replicas are placed in the same region, decreasing both latency and conflict rate.

**如果将每个实体组的写入速率控制在每秒几次, 冲突便很少发生.** 对于同一时间只有少数用户修改实体的应用, 这一限制通常不成问题. 多数目标客户通过更细地划分实体组来提高写入吞吐量, 或将副本放在同一区域以缩短延迟, 减少冲突.

Applications with some server "stickiness" are well positioned to batch user operations into fewer Megastore transactions. Bulk processing of Megastore queue messages is a common batching technique, reducing the conflict rate and increasing aggregate throughput.

如果用户请求通常由固定的应用服务器处理, 应用就更容易将多次操作合并为少量 Megastore 事务. 批量处理 Megastore 队列消息也是常见做法, 可以减少冲突, 提高总吞吐量.

For groups that must regularly exceed a few writes per second, applications can use the fine-grained advisory locks dispensed by coordinator servers. Sequencing transactions back-to-back avoids the delays associated with retries and the reversion to two-phase Paxos when a conflict is detected.

如果某个实体组经常需要每秒写入数次以上, 应用可以使用协调器提供的细粒度建议性锁, 让事务一个接一个地执行. 这样既能避免冲突后的重试等待, 也能避免因冲突而退回两阶段 Paxos.

## 4.9 运维问题

When a particular full replica becomes unreliable or loses connectivity, Megastore's performance can degrade. We have a number of ways to respond to these failures, including: routing users away from the problematic replica, disabling its coordinators, or disabling it entirely. In practice we rely on a combination of techniques, each with its own tradeoffs.

某个完整副本不稳定或失联时, Megastore 的性能可能下降. 可以让用户流量避开问题副本, 禁用它的协调器, 或彻底禁用该副本. 实际操作中, 我们会根据情况结合使用这些方法.

The first and most important response to an outage is to disable Megastore clients at the affected replica by rerouting traffic to application servers near other replicas. These clients typically experience the same outage impacting the storage stack below them, and might be unreachable from the outside world.

发生故障时, 首要措施是将流量转发给其他副本附近的应用服务器, 停用故障副本处的 Megastore 客户端. 这些客户端通常也会受到下层存储故障影响, 甚至可能无法从外部访问.

Rerouting traffic alone is insufficient if unhealthy coordinator servers might continue to hold their Chubby locks. The next response is to disable the replica's coordinators, ensuring that the problem has a minimal impact on write latency. (Section 4.7 described this process in more detail.) Once writers are absolved of invalidating the replica's coordinators, an unhealthy replica's impact on write latency is limited. Only the initial "accept leader" step in the write algorithm depends on the replica, and we maintain a tight deadline before falling back on two-phase Paxos and nominating a healthier leader for the next write.

如果有故障的协调器仍持有 Chubby 锁, 仅仅转移用户流量还不够. 下一步是禁用这个副本的协调器, 尽量减轻故障对写入延迟的影响 (详见第 4.7 节). 这样一来, 写入者就不用等待对该协调器执行失效处理. 此时, 写入流程中只有最初的 "领导者接受" 步骤还依赖故障副本; 该步骤超时后, 系统会改用两阶段 Paxos, 并为下一次写入选出状态更好的领导者.

A more draconian and rarely used action is to disable the replica entirely: neither clients nor replication servers will attempt to communicate with it. While sequestering the replica can seem appealing, the primary impact is a hit to availability: one less replica is eligible to help writers form a quorum. The valid use case is when attempted operations might cause harm - e.g. when the underlying Bigtable is severely overloaded.

更严厉, 也更少采用的办法是彻底禁用该副本, 让客户端和复制服务器都不再与它通信. 这样做主要会降低可用性: 写入者少了一个可用于组成多数派的副本. 只有继续访问它可能造成损害时, 例如底层 Bigtable 已严重过载, 才适合这样做.

# 5. 实践经验

Development of the system was aided by a strong emphasis on testability. The code is instrumented with numerous (but cheap) assertions and logging, and has thorough unit test coverage. But the most effective bug-finding tool was our network simulator: the pseudo-random test framework. It is capable of exploring the space of all possible orderings and delays of communications between simulated nodes or threads, and deterministically reproducing the same behavior given the same seed. Bugs were exposed by finding a problematic sequence of events triggering an assertion failure (or incorrect result), often with enough log and trace information to diagnose the problem, which was then added to the suite of unit tests. While an exhaustive search of the scheduling state space is impossible, the pseudo-random simulation explores more than is practical by other means. Through running thousands of simulated hours of operation each night, the tests have found many surprising problems.

开发 Megastore 时, 我们格外重视可测试性. 代码中加入了大量开销较小的断言和日志, 单元测试也覆盖得很充分. 最有效的找错工具是网络模拟器, 也就是伪随机测试框架. 它会改变节点或线程之间的通信顺序和延迟, 并能用同一个随机种子稳定复现问题. 一旦某种事件顺序触发断言失败或错误结果, 日志和跟踪信息通常足以帮助定位原因; 对应的案例随后会加入单元测试. 虽然无法穷尽所有调度顺序, 伪随机模拟仍能探索其他方法难以覆盖的情况. 每晚运行数千小时的模拟后, 我们发现了许多意料之外的问题.

In real-world deployments we have observed the expected performance: our replication protocol optimizations indeed provide local reads most of the time, and writes with about the overhead of a single WAN roundtrip. Most applications have found the latency tolerable. Some applications are designed to hide write latency from users, and a few must choose entity group boundaries carefully to maximize their write throughput. This effort yields major operational advantages: Megastore's latency tail is significantly shorter than that of the underlying layers, and most applications can withstand planned and unplanned outages with little or no manual intervention.

实际部署的性能符合预期: **经过协议优化, 大多数读取在本地完成, 写入只增加约一次广域网往返的开销.** 大多数应用都能接受这样的延迟. 有些应用通过设计让用户感受不到写入等待; 少数应用则需要谨慎划定实体组边界, 以提高写入吞吐量. 这些设计也带来了运维上的好处: Megastore 的尾部延迟远短于底层服务, 多数应用几乎无须人工干预, 就能承受计划内和突发的中断.

Most applications use the Megastore schema language to model their data. Some have implemented their own entity-attribute-value model within the Megastore schema language, then used their own application logic to model their data (most notably, Google App Engine<sup>[8]</sup>). Some use a hybrid of the two approaches. Having the dynamic schema built on top of the static schema, rather than the other way around, allows most applications to enjoy the performance, usability, and integrity benefits of the static schema, while still giving the option of a dynamic schema to those who need it.

多数应用使用 Megastore 的模式语言组织数据. 一些应用在这个模式之上自行实现实体-属性-值模型, 再用应用逻辑组织数据, 其中最典型的是 Google App Engine<sup>[8]</sup>; 还有应用混合使用两种方式. 由于动态模式建立在静态模式之上, 多数应用仍能获得静态模式在性能, 易用性和数据完整性上的好处, 需要动态模式的应用也有选择余地.

The term "high availability" usually signifies the ability to mask faults to make a collection of systems more reliable than the individual systems. While fault tolerance is a highly desired goal, it comes with it its own pitfalls: it often hides persistent underlying problems. We have a saying in the group: "Fault tolerance is fault masking". Too often, the resilience of our system coupled with insufficient vigilance in tracking the underlying faults leads to unexpected problems: small transient errors on top of persistent uncorrected problems cause significantly larger problems.

"高可用性" 通常意味着系统能够屏蔽局部故障, 让整体比各个组成部分更可靠. 容错很重要, 但也可能掩盖长期存在的底层问题. 团队有句话: "容错就是故障屏蔽". 如果系统靠容错继续运行, 运维人员却没有追查底层故障, 后来一次短暂的小故障就可能与尚未修复的问题叠加, 造成严重后果.

Another issue is flow control. An algorithm that tolerates faulty participants can be heedless of slow ones. Ideally a collection of disparate machines would make progress only as fast as the least capable member. If slowness is interpreted as a fault, and tolerated, the fastest majority of machines will process requests at their own pace, reaching equilibrium only when slowed down by the load of the laggers struggling to catch up. We call this anomaly chain gang throttling, evoking the image of a group of escaping convicts making progress only as quickly as they can drag the stragglers.

另一个问题是流量控制. 一种算法即使能容忍节点故障, 也未必会照顾运行缓慢的节点. 理想情况下, 异构机器组成的集群应当按最慢成员能跟上的速度推进. 如果把慢节点也当作故障来容忍, 速度较快的多数节点就会按自己的节奏处理请求; 直到落后节点追赶时产生的负载拖慢整个系统, 才重新达到平衡. 我们称这种异常为 "锁链式节流" (chain gang throttling), 就像一队逃犯不得不拖着落后者一起前进.

A benefit of Megastore's write-ahead log has been the ease of integrating external systems. Any idempotent operation can be made a step in applying a log entry.

Megastore 的预写日志也方便集成外部系统. 任何幂等操作都可以加入日志条目的应用流程.

Achieving good performance for more complex queries requires attention to the physical data layout in Bigtable. When queries are slow, developers need to examine Bigtable traces to understand why their query performs below their expectations. Megastore does not enforce specific policies on block sizes, compression, table splitting, locality group, nor other tuning controls provided by Bigtable. Instead, we expose these controls, providing application developers with the ability (and burden) of optimizing performance.

复杂查询要获得良好性能, 必须关注数据在 Bigtable 中的物理布局. 查询比预期慢时, 开发者需要查看 Bigtable 的跟踪记录, 找出原因. Megastore 不强制规定块大小, 压缩, 表拆分或局部性组等调优策略, 而是把这些控制项交给应用开发者. 开发者可以自行优化, 也必须承担相应的工作.

# 6. 相关工作

Recently, there has been increasing interest in NoSQL data storage systems to meet the demand of large web applications. Representative work includes Bigtable<sup>[15]</sup>, Cassandra<sup>[6]</sup>, and Yahoo PNUTS<sup>[16]</sup>. In these systems, scalability is achieved by sacrificing one or more properties of traditional RDBMS systems, for example, transactions, schema support, query capability<sup>[12, 33]</sup>. These systems often reduce the scope of transactions to the granularity of single key access and thus place a significant hurdle to building applications<sup>[18, 32]</sup>. Some systems extend the scope of transactions to multiple rows within a single table, for example the Amazon SimpleDB<sup>[5]</sup> uses the concept of domain as the transactional unit. Yet such efforts are still limited because transactions cannot cross tables or scale arbitrarily. Moreover, most current scalable data storage systems lack the rich data model of an RDBMS, which increases the burden on developers. Combining the merits from both database and scalable data stores, Megastore provides transactional ACID guarantees within a entity group and provides a flexible data model with user-defined schema, database-style and full-text indexes, and queues.

近年来, 大型 Web 应用推动了 NoSQL 存储的发展, 代表性系统包括 Bigtable<sup>[15]</sup>, Cassandra<sup>[6]</sup> 和 Yahoo PNUTS<sup>[16]</sup>. 这些系统通常舍弃传统 RDBMS 的部分能力, 例如事务, 模式支持或查询功能<sup>[12, 33]</sup>, 以换取扩展性. 它们往往只支持单键事务, 增加了应用开发的难度<sup>[18, 32]</sup>. 有些系统将事务扩展到同一张表中的多行, 例如 Amazon SimpleDB<sup>[5]</sup> 以 domain 为事务单位, 但仍无法跨表, 扩展能力也有限. 多数可扩展存储系统还缺少关系数据库那样丰富的数据模型. Megastore 则在实体组内提供 ACID 事务, 并支持用户定义模式, 二级索引, 全文索引和队列等功能.

Data replication across geographically distributed datacenters is an indispensable means of improving availability in state-of-the-art storage systems. Most prevailing data storage systems use asynchronous replication schemes with a weaker consistency model. For example, Cassandra<sup>[6]</sup>, HBase<sup>[1]</sup>, CouchDB<sup>[7]</sup>, and Dynamo<sup>[19]</sup> use an eventual consistency model and PNUTS uses "timeline" consistency<sup>[16]</sup>. By comparison, synchronous replication guarantees strong transactional semantics over wide-area networks and improves the performance of current reads.

跨地域数据中心复制是现代存储系统提高可用性的重要手段. 许多系统采用异步复制, 一致性保证也相对较弱: Cassandra<sup>[6]</sup>, HBase<sup>[1]</sup>, CouchDB<sup>[7]</sup> 和 Dynamo<sup>[19]</sup> 提供最终一致性, PNUTS 提供 "时间线" 一致性<sup>[16]</sup>. 相比之下, 同步复制能在广域网上保证较强的事务语义, 也让读取最新数据更快.

Synchronous replication for traditional RDBMS systems presents a performance challenge and is difficult to scale<sup>[21]</sup>. Some proposed workarounds allow for strong consistency via asynchronous replication. One approach lets updates complete before their effects are replicated, passing the synchronization delay on to transactions that need to read the updated state<sup>[26]</sup>. Another approach routes writes to a single master while distributing read-only transactions among a set of replicas<sup>[29]</sup>. The updates are asynchronously propagated to the remaining replicas, and reads are either delayed or sent to replicas that have already been synchronized. A recent proposal for efficient synchronous replication introduces an ordering preprocessor that schedules incoming transactions deterministically, so that they can be independently applied at multiple replicas with identical results<sup>[31]</sup>. The synchronization burden is shifted to the preprocessor, which itself would have to be made scalable.

传统 RDBMS 很难在保证同步复制的同时兼顾性能和扩展性<sup>[21]</sup>. 有些方案尝试通过异步复制实现强一致性. 一种方案允许更新先完成, 再将结果复制出去; 需要读取新状态的事务则承担同步等待<sup>[26]</sup>. 另一种方案把写入集中到单个主节点, 将只读事务分配给其他副本<sup>[29]</sup>. 更新再异步传播, 读取要么等待, 要么转发给已经同步的副本. 还有一种同步复制方案使用排序预处理器, 按确定顺序安排事务, 使各副本能够独立执行并得到相同结果<sup>[31]</sup>. 不过, 同步的负担转移到了预处理器, 而预处理器本身也必须能够扩展.

Until recently, few have used Paxos to achieve synchronous replication. SCALARIS is one example that uses the Paxos commit protocol<sup>[22]</sup> to implement replication for a distributed hash table<sup>[30]</sup>. Keyspace<sup>[2]</sup> also uses Paxos to implement replication on a generic key-value store. However the scalability and performance of these systems is not publicly known. Megastore is perhaps the first large-scale storage systems to implement Paxos-based replication across datacenters while satisfying the scalability and performance requirements of scalable web applications in the cloud.

直到当时, 用 Paxos 实现同步复制的系统仍不多见. SCALARIS 使用 Paxos 提交协议<sup>[22]</sup> 复制分布式哈希表<sup>[30]</sup>; Keyspace<sup>[2]</sup> 则用 Paxos 复制通用键值存储. 这些系统的扩展性和性能尚无公开数据. Megastore 或许是第一个既用 Paxos 跨数据中心复制, 又能满足云端 Web 应用扩展性和性能要求的大型存储系统.

Conventional database systems provide mature and sophisticated data management features, but have difficulties in serving large-scale interactive services targeted by this paper<sup>[33]</sup>. Open source database systems such as MySQL<sup>[10]</sup> do not scale up to the levels we require<sup>[17]</sup>, while expensive commercial database systems like Oracle<sup>[4]</sup> significantly increase the total cost of ownership in large deployments in the cloud. Furthermore, neither of them offer fault-tolerant synchronous replication mechanism<sup>[3, 11]</sup>, which is a key piece to build interactive services in the cloud.

传统数据库具备成熟而丰富的数据管理能力, 却难以支撑本文关注的大规模交互式服务<sup>[33]</sup>. MySQL<sup>[10]</sup> 等开源数据库无法扩展到我们所需的规模<sup>[17]</sup>; Oracle<sup>[4]</sup> 等商业数据库在云端大规模部署时, 总拥有成本又会显著增加. 此外, 两类系统都缺少容错的同步复制机制<sup>[3, 11]</sup>, 而这正是构建云端交互式服务的关键.

# 7. 结论

In this paper we present Megastore, a scalable, highly available datastore designed to meet the storage requirements of interactive Internet services. We use Paxos for synchronous wide area replication, providing lightweight and fast failover of individual operations. The latency penalty of synchronous replication across widely distributed replicas is more than offset by the convenience of a single system image and the operational benefits of carrier-grade availability. We use Bigtable as our scalable datastore while adding richer primitives such as ACID transactions, indexes, and queues. Partitioning the database into entity group sub-databases provides familiar transactional features for most operations while allowing scalability of storage and throughput.

本文介绍了 Megastore, 一套面向交互式互联网服务, 可扩展且高可用的存储系统. 它用 Paxos 跨广域网同步复制数据, 让单次操作的故障切换既快, 开销又低. 远距离副本间的同步复制虽然增加了延迟, 但统一数据视图带来的便利, 以及电信级可用性带来的运维收益, 足以弥补这一代价. Megastore 以 Bigtable 为存储基础, 在其上增加 ACID 事务, 索引和队列等功能. 通过将数据库划分为实体组, 系统既能让多数操作继续使用事务, 又能扩展存储容量和吞吐量.

Megastore has over 100 applications in production, facing both internal and external users, and providing infrastructure for higher levels. The number and diversity of these applications is evidence of Megastore's ease of use, generality, and power. We hope that Megastore demonstrates the viability of a middle ground in feature set and replication consistency for today's scalable storage systems.

论文撰写时, Megastore 已支撑 100 多个生产应用, 服务内部和外部用户, 也为上层系统提供基础设施. 这些应用数量多, 类型各异, 说明 Megastore 易于使用, 用途广泛, 功能也足够强. 我们希望 Megastore 能说明: 可扩展存储系统可以在功能与复制一致性之间找到可行的平衡.
