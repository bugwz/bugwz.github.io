# 0. 摘要

Minimum Storage Regenerating (MSR) codes have strong potential for building efficient and reliable storage systems due to their excellent fault tolerance and low repair bandwidth. However, to meet MSR code constraints and optimize storage performance, systems often adopt large chunk sizes. This leads to significant I/O amplification during degraded reads, as entire chunks must be reconstructed to access a single object.

最小存储再生 (Minimum Storage Regenerating, MSR) 码兼具出色的容错能力和较低的修复带宽, 很适合用于构建高效, 可靠的存储系统. 然而, 为满足 MSR 码的约束并提高存储性能, 系统通常采用较大的块. 发生降级读时, 访问一个对象也要重建整个块, 因而造成严重的 I/O 放大.

In this paper, we propose DRBoost, an approach that boosts degraded read performance in MSR-coded storage clusters by reducing repair bandwidth and eliminating access fragmentation for healthy data. DRBoost introduces three key techniques: (1) a partial-chunk reconstruction algorithm that reduces repair bandwidth by leveraging two forms of data reuse; (2) a reconstruction-friendly coding layout that improves reuse efficiency and accommodates objects of diverse sizes; and (3) a fragmentation-free storage layout that avoids unnecessary request splitting. Extensive experiments under various conditions and workloads show that DRBoost reduces degraded read latency by one to two orders of magnitude, significantly improving system responsiveness.

本文提出 DRBoost, 通过减少修复所需的数据传输量, 消除对健康数据的碎片化访问, 提升 MSR 编码存储集群的降级读性能. DRBoost 包含三项关键技术: (1) 利用两种数据复用方式减少修复带宽的部分块重建算法; (2) 提高复用效率, 适应不同对象大小的重建友好编码布局; (3) 避免不必要的请求拆分的无碎片存储布局. 在多种环境和负载下开展的大量实验表明, DRBoost 将降级读延迟降低了一到两个数量级, 显著改善了系统响应速度.

# 1. 引言

Erasure coding is a widely used technique in object storage systems <sup>[1, 8, 38, 69]</sup>, significantly reducing storage overhead compared to conventional replication techniques while ensuring fault tolerance. However, degraded reads in erasure-coded storage become critical operations due to frequent unavailability events in storage systems. First, storage devices such as HDDs <sup>[43, 52]</sup> and SSDs <sup>[36, 53]</sup> are subject to permanent errors. Second, temporary unavailability accounts for over 90% of data center failures <sup>[15]</sup>. Third, planned offline events, such as system maintenance or software updates, are ongoing activities <sup>[7, 29]</sup>. Compared with replication, the reconstruction of erasure-coded data involves additional overhead caused by read amplification and decoding computation. Therefore, ensuring efficient degraded reads is critical for the performance of erasure-coded storage systems <sup>[25, 47, 51]</sup>, as excessive performance gaps between normal and degraded reads can result in long latency tails <sup>[17]</sup>, leading to poor Quality of Service <sup>[54]</sup>.

纠删码广泛用于对象存储系统 <sup>[1, 8, 38, 69]</sup>, 相比传统多副本技术, 能在保证容错能力的同时显著降低存储开销. 但存储系统频繁出现数据不可用事件, 因此降级读是纠删码存储中的关键操作. 首先, HDD <sup>[43, 52]</sup> 和 SSD <sup>[36, 53]</sup> 等设备都会发生永久性故障. 其次, 临时不可用占数据中心故障的 90% 以上 <sup>[15]</sup>. 此外, 系统维护, 软件更新等计划内下线也经常发生 <sup>[7, 29]</sup>. 相比副本读取, 纠删码数据的重建会带来读放大和解码计算开销. 因此, 高效的降级读对纠删码存储系统的性能至关重要 <sup>[25, 47, 51]</sup>: 正常读与降级读的性能差距过大, 会拉长尾延迟 <sup>[17]</sup>, 损害服务质量 <sup>[54]</sup>.

Although two primary coding schemes are commonly utilized in production storage systems, they come with certain inherent deficiencies. Reed-Solomon (RS) codes <sup>[49]</sup> have optimal fault-tolerant properties but suffer from high repair penalties, which become pronounced with the use of wide stripes <sup>[19, 24, 62]</sup>. Locally Recoverable Codes (LRCs) <sup>[20, 24, 27]</sup> improve repair efficiency by adding extra local parities but bring about reduced fault tolerance. Benefiting from advanced coding theory, Minimum Storage Regenerating (MSR) codes have been proposed <sup>[13, 18, 28, 41, 48, 57, 61]</sup>, offering optimal repair bandwidth (`i.e.`, the amount of data being transferred during a repair operation) among MDS codes, which ensures optimal fault tolerance under the same storage overhead. This property makes MSR codes a highly promising option for building efficient and reliable storage systems.

生产存储系统中常用的两类编码方案各有不足. Reed-Solomon (RS) 码 <sup>[49]</sup> 具备最优的容错能力, 但修复代价高, 采用宽条带时尤为明显 <sup>[19, 24, 62]</sup>. 局部可恢复码 (Locally Recoverable Codes, LRC) <sup>[20, 24, 27]</sup> 通过增加局部校验块提高修复效率, 代价是容错能力下降. 随着编码理论的发展, 研究者提出了 MSR 码 <sup>[13, 18, 28, 41, 48, 57, 61]</sup>. 它在最大距离可分 (MDS) 码中具有最优修复带宽, 即修复操作所需传输的数据量最少, 同时在相同存储开销下保持最优容错能力. 因此, MSR 码很有希望用于构建高效, 可靠的存储系统.

However, MSR codes' theoretical advantages are mitigated by a significant practical limitation: their large chunk size. This limitation arises from the vectorized structure of MSR codes, where each chunk comprises multiple sub-chunks encoded by well-designed linear transformations. First, as the stripe width increases while maintaining a constant parity number, the lower bound of the sub-chunk number is proven to rise exponentially <sup>[6, 58]</sup>, as shown in Figure 1a. Second, larger sub-chunks are crucial for accelerating recovery, as shown in Figure 1b, because small sub-chunks cause inefficiencies such as increased seeking time in HDDs and request splitting in SSDs <sup>[39, 42]</sup>. For example, switching the (20, 16) RS-coded HDD stripe used by Backblaze <sup>[26]</sup> to MSR codes will increase the recommended chunk size to at least 256MB.

然而, 一个现实限制削弱了 MSR 码的理论优势: 块太大. 这源于 MSR 码的向量结构, 每个块由多个子块组成, 子块之间通过精心设计的线性变换进行编码. 首先, 在校验块数量不变时, 随着条带变宽, 子块数量的理论下界会呈指数增长 <sup>[6, 58]</sup>, 如图 1a 所示. 其次, 如图 1b 所示, 较大的子块对加快恢复十分重要, 因为小子块会增加 HDD 寻道时间, 引发 SSD 请求拆分等低效行为 <sup>[39, 42]</sup>. 例如, 若将 Backblaze 使用的 (20, 16) RS 编码 HDD 条带 <sup>[26]</sup> 改用 MSR 码, 推荐块大小会增至至少 256 MB.

Accordingly, typical objects often cannot fill an entire chunk. For instance, over 90% of objects in Alibaba Cloud Object Storage <sup>[55, 56]</sup> are smaller than 10MB, both in terms of object count and access frequency. Similar distributions are observed in IBM Cloud Object Storage <sup>[14]</sup> and Facebook F4 BLOB Storage <sup>[38]</sup>. Unfortunately, current research on MSR-coded storage systems <sup>[34, 35, 56, 61]</sup> considers the chunk as the basic repair unit. Consequently, reconstructing an entire chunk for retrieving a single object in the degraded mode exacerbates the I/O amplification problem significantly.

这样一来, 常见对象往往填不满一个块. 例如, 在阿里云对象存储中 <sup>[55, 56]</sup>, 无论按对象数量还是访问次数统计, 大小不足 10 MB 的对象占比都超过 90%. IBM Cloud Object Storage <sup>[14]</sup> 和 Facebook F4 BLOB Storage <sup>[38]</sup> 也呈现类似分布. 遗憾的是, 现有 MSR 存储研究 <sup>[34, 35, 56, 61]</sup> 将块视为基本修复单位. 降级读只需取回一个对象, 却要重建整个块, 从而大幅加剧了 I/O 放大.

Traditional approaches often treat degraded reads as a specific form of failure recovery, overlooking the significance of partial-chunk reconstruction, particularly in the scenario of MSR codes. Some scalar-coded systems <sup>[20, 38]</sup> perform partial-chunk reconstruction by isolating required codewords and requesting necessary fragments corresponding to the target object's location. However, this partial-chunk reconstruction does not work in MSR-coded systems because the hop-and-couple method used in these systems <sup>[47, 61]</sup> causes objects spanning at least one full sub-chunk to be embedded across all codewords within the stripe. Geometric Partitioning <sup>[56]</sup> mitigates I/O amplification in MSR-coded storage by using chunk sizes in a geometric sequence, which has notable limitations: recovery efficiency is compromised due to the inability to fully utilize storage devices, and scalability is constrained by the increasing stripe width, which exacerbates the difficulty of a single object fully occupying an entire chunk.

传统方法通常将降级读视为故障恢复的一种特殊情况, 忽视了部分块重建的重要性, 在 MSR 场景下尤其如此. 一些标量编码系统 <sup>[20, 38]</sup> 会隔离所需码字, 根据目标对象的位置请求必要片段, 从而只重建块的一部分. 但这种方法不适用于 MSR 系统: 其中的 hop-and-couple 方法 <sup>[47, 61]</sup> 会让覆盖至少一个完整子块的对象分散到条带内的全部码字中. Geometric Partitioning <sup>[56]</sup> 采用按等比数列变化的块大小来缓解 MSR 存储的 I/O 放大, 但有明显局限: 无法充分利用存储设备, 因而损害恢复效率; 条带越宽, 单个对象越难占满整个块, 也限制了其扩展能力.

In this paper, we propose DRBoost, an approach to boosting the performance of Degraded Reads in MSR-coded storage clusters by reducing repair bandwidth and preventing fragmentation of healthy data access. Specifically, we optimize degraded reads by introducing the concept of data reuse, incorporating a reconstruction-friendly coding layout and a fragmentation-free storage layout.

本文提出 DRBoost, 通过减少修复带宽并避免健康数据访问的碎片化, 提升 MSR 编码存储集群的降级读性能. 具体而言, 我们引入数据复用, 并结合重建友好的编码布局和无碎片的存储布局, 对降级读进行优化.

First, the complexity of linear transformations and the unpredictability of failure states make it challenging to maintain low repair bandwidth when handling various types of degraded read requests. To address this, we introduce sub-stripes with independent fault tolerance, allowing any node's data loss within the sub-stripe to be reconstructed using only its remaining healthy data. Next, we further reduce repair bandwidth through two data reuse concepts: (a) multiple lost sub-chunks can be reconstructed by reusing the same set of helper sub-chunks within an appropriate sub-stripe, and (b) certain helper sub-chunks used for reconstruction can also serve as the healthy portions of the requested object. Finally, we propose a lightweight partial-chunk reconstruction algorithm by utilizing such data reuse.

首先, 线性变换复杂, 故障状态难以预知, 因而处理不同类型的降级读请求时, 很难始终保持较低的修复带宽. 为此, 我们引入具有独立容错能力的子条带: 其中任意节点的数据丢失后, 只需利用该子条带内剩余的健康数据即可重建. 在此基础上, 再用两种方式复用数据, 进一步降低修复带宽: (a) 选取合适的子条带, 让多个丢失子块共用同一组辅助子块进行重建; (b) 某些用于重建的辅助子块, 同时也是请求对象尚且健康的部分. 最后, 我们利用这些复用机会设计了轻量的部分块重建算法.

Second, the asymmetric access patterns during repairing different nodes pose challenges in designing a reconstruction-friendly coding layout that ensures high reuse efficiency under any failure event. By identifying the distinct roles each sub-chunk takes on within sub-stripes, we introduce the concept of the data-reuse-oriented basic layout unit, as both its healthy portions and the sub-stripe it belongs to can be reused during degraded reads. Moreover, when the sub-chunk size of an MSR code matches the chunk size of a scalar code, the basic layout unit size of the MSR code aligns with the stripe size of the scalar code, thereby alleviating the mismatch issue between object and chunk sizes. Additionally, basic layout units are allocated guided by a tiered layout structure, which incorporates two intermediate levels between the basic layout unit and the entire stripe data. This structure facilitates the layout of varying-sized objects while improving load balancing and enabling the reuse of more sub-chunks.

其次, 修复不同节点时的访问模式并不对称. 要设计一种无论哪个节点故障都能高效复用数据的编码布局, 并不容易. 我们识别出各子块在子条带中的不同角色, 据此提出面向数据复用的基本布局单元: 降级读时, 既可以复用单元内的健康部分, 也可以复用其所属子条带. 当 MSR 码的子块大小与标量码的块大小相同时, MSR 的基本布局单元大小与标量码的条带大小一致, 从而缓解对象与块的粒度不匹配问题. 此外, 我们通过分层布局结构分配基本布局单元, 在基本单元和整个条带之间增加两个中间层级. 这一结构既能适应不同大小的对象, 又能改善负载均衡, 复用更多子块.

Third, the fragmented access patterns resulting from the data-reuse-oriented layout lead to request splitting, which impairs the I/O performance. To mitigate this, we propose an inner-chunk fragmentation elimination strategy to ensure that an object's portion within a single chunk remains unfragmented by placing sub-chunks from a basic layout unit contiguously and maintaining an appropriate order between basic layout units. Moreover, we construct a deterministic mapping table shared across all stripes with the same (n, k) configuration, enabling this fragmentation-free storage layout to work in tandem with the reconstruction-friendly coding layout. Additionally, we directly store the storage layout in object metadata and optimize the I/O process of normal reads by bypassing the coding layout translation, with translation triggered only during degraded reads. Consequently, the access sequentiality during degraded reads is optimized while avoiding potential negative impacts on normal reads.

第三, 面向复用的布局会造成碎片化访问, 引发请求拆分并损害 I/O 性能. 为此, 我们提出块内碎片消除策略: 连续存放同一基本布局单元的子块, 并合理安排各单元的顺序, 保证一个对象在单个块内的部分连续存储. 我们还构建了确定性映射表, 让相同 (n, k) 配置的所有条带共享, 使无碎片存储布局能够与重建友好的编码布局配合使用. 对象元数据直接记录存储布局, 正常读绕过编码布局转换, 只在降级读时触发转换. 这样既能改善降级读的访问连续性, 又能避免给正常读带来潜在负担.

We implement the DRBoost prototype and integrate it into Ceph <sup>[1, 63]</sup>. Due to the inadequate support for MSR codes in current production storage systems, which is caused by their simplistic alignment strategy and lack of partial-chunk reconstruction semantics, we make top-down modifications to Ceph, providing directional guidance for improved utilization of MSR codes. We evaluate DRBoost across various conditions and workloads. Our experimental results demonstrate that DRBoost reduces both the amplification ratio and the latency of degraded reads by one to two orders of magnitude.

我们实现了 DRBoost 原型并将其接入 Ceph <sup>[1, 63]</sup>. 现有生产存储系统的对齐策略较为简单, 又缺少部分块重建语义, 对 MSR 码的支持不足. 因此, 我们自上而下修改了 Ceph, 为更好地使用 MSR 码提供了实现方向. 我们在多种环境和负载下评估 DRBoost, 实验表明它可将降级读的放大率和延迟降低一到两个数量级.

# 2. 背景与动机

This section provides an overview of MSR codes (§2.1), discusses the issue of large chunk sizes in MSR-coded storage (§2.2), and examines how they exacerbate I/O amplification during degraded reads (§2.3).

本节先介绍 MSR 码 (原文第 2.1 节), 再讨论 MSR 存储中的大块问题 (原文第 2.2 节), 最后分析这一问题如何加剧降级读的 I/O 放大 (原文第 2.3 节).

## 2.1 纠删码与最优恢复

Erasure Codes. Erasure codes divide data into k equally-sized chunks and encode them into m parity chunks, forming a stripe of n = k + m chunks. The Maximum Distance Separable (MDS) property indicates that any m lost chunks can be recovered using any k healthy chunks.

**纠删码.** 纠删码将数据分成 k 个等大的数据块, 编码生成 m 个校验块, 共同构成含 n = k + m 个块的条带. 最大距离可分 (MDS) 性质意味着: 任意 m 个块丢失后, 都可用任意 k 个健康块恢复.

Scalar Codes and Vector Codes. Each stripe typically comprises multiple codewords. A codeword represents the minimal collection of symbols, commonly a byte for GF(2<sup>8</sup>), that are encoded and decoded together <sup>[44]</sup>. Erasure codes can be classified into scalar and vector codes, depending on the number of symbols each node contributes to a codeword. In scalar codes, such as RS codes <sup>[49]</sup> and LRCs <sup>[20]</sup>, each node contributes a single symbol to a codeword. In contrast, vector codes allow each node to contribute multiple symbols to a codeword. These symbols are interleaved in practice so that symbols at the same position of different codewords are stored contiguously <sup>[47, 61]</sup>. As a result, each chunk of vector codes comprises several smaller symbol units, referred to as sub-chunks. For clarity, the number of sub-chunks within a chunk is defined as the sub-packetization level, denoted by α.

**标量码与向量码.** 一个条带通常包含多个码字. 码字是一组共同参与编码和解码的最小符号集合 <sup>[44]</sup>; 对 GF(2<sup>8</sup>) 而言, 一个符号通常是一个字节. 按每个节点向一个码字贡献的符号数量, 纠删码可分为标量码和向量码. RS 码 <sup>[49]</sup>, LRC <sup>[20]</sup> 等标量码中, 每个节点只向一个码字贡献一个符号. 向量码则允许每个节点贡献多个符号. 实际存储时会交错排列这些符号, 将不同码字中位置相同的符号连续存放 <sup>[47, 61]</sup>. 因而向量码的每个块由多个较小的符号单元组成, 称为子块. 为便于描述, 我们将块内子块数量称为子分包数 (sub-packetization level), 记为 α.

Optimal Recovery of MSR Codes. MSR codes are a class of vector MDS codes that have the smallest possible repair bandwidth. Compared to scalar MDS codes, which suffer significant repair penalties, MSR codes mitigate these costs by employing linear transformations between sub-chunks within vector codes <sup>[13]</sup>. To reconstruct a lost chunk, only a portion of sub-chunks from each helper chunk within the corresponding stripe is required. When all n - 1 healthy nodes participate in the recovery, each contributes only 1/m of its sub-chunks. Currently, Clay (Coupled-Layer) codes <sup>[61]</sup> represent the state-of-the-art MSR code design, so this paper adopts Clay codes as the example throughout the discussion.

**MSR 码的最优恢复.** MSR 码是一类修复带宽达到理论最小值的向量 MDS 码. 标量 MDS 码的修复代价较高, 而 MSR 码通过向量码内子块之间的线性变换降低这一代价 <sup>[13]</sup>. 重建一个丢失块, 只需读取对应条带中每个辅助块的一部分子块. 当全部 n - 1 个健康节点参与恢复时, 每个节点只需贡献其子块的 1/m. Clay (Coupled-Layer) 码 <sup>[61]</sup> 是当时先进的 MSR 设计, 因此本文始终以它为例展开讨论.

Clay Codes. A layer refers to the set of sub-chunks that occupy the same position across all chunks. Sub-chunks are grouped into pairs, where coupling denotes the pairwise operation that transforms uncoupled sub-chunks into coupled ones, and uncoupling denotes the inverse operation. At a high level, Clay codes are constructed by applying an arbitrary MDS code within each layer, combined with pairwise coupling and uncoupling operations across layers.

**Clay 码.** 各个块中处于相同位置的子块组成一层. 子块两两配对, 将未耦合子块变为耦合子块的成对操作称为耦合, 逆操作称为解耦. 总体而言, Clay 码在每层内应用任意一种 MDS 码, 再结合跨层的成对耦合与解耦操作构建而成.

To illustrate the mechanism, consider the (6, 4) Clay codes, where each chunk contains α = 8 sub-chunks. As shown in Figure 2a, when data chunk 3 is lost, it can be reconstructed using sub-chunks at positions {0, 1, 4, 5} from all remaining healthy chunks through three steps: (a) Uncoupling: perform pairwise uncoupling on the highlighted sub-chunks from D1, D2, P1, and P2 to restore their uncoupled form, (b) Decoding: apply scalar MDS decoding to the uncoupled sub-chunks in layers {0, 1, 4, 5} to reconstruct the corresponding uncoupled sub-chunks of D3 and D4, and (c) Recoupling: use the recovered uncoupled sub-chunks of D3 and D4, along with the coupled sub-chunks of D4, to reconstruct the lost sub-chunks in their coupled form. Notably, the helper sub-chunks are taken from the same positions across different chunks, forming what we refer to as the repair pattern, shown in Figure 2b. For example, the repair pattern for D3 specifies the sub-chunk positions required for its reconstruction, as shown in Figure 2a.

以每块包含 α = 8 个子块的 (6, 4) Clay 码为例. 如图 2a 所示, 数据块 3 丢失后, 可用其他全部健康块中位置为 {0, 1, 4, 5} 的子块, 分三步重建: (a) 解耦, 对 D1, D2, P1, P2 中突出标出的子块两两解耦, 还原为未耦合形式; (b) 解码, 对第 {0, 1, 4, 5} 层未耦合子块执行标量 MDS 解码, 重建 D3 和 D4 中相应的未耦合子块; (c) 重新耦合, 利用恢复出的 D3, D4 未耦合子块以及 D4 的耦合子块, 重建丢失子块的耦合形式. 不同辅助块提供的子块位置相同, 这些位置构成图 2b 所示的修复模式. 例如, D3 的修复模式给出了图 2a 中重建它所需的子块位置.

## 2.2 块大小过大

Despite the theoretical optimality of MSR codes in repair efficiency, their large chunk sizes remain a major drawback. The chunk size is determined by the product of the sub-packetization level and the sub-chunk size, both of which contribute to the increase in chunk size.

尽管 MSR 码在理论上具有最优修复效率, 块过大仍是其主要缺点. 块大小等于子分包数与子块大小的乘积, 两者都会推动块大小增加.

First, the sub-packetization level of MSR codes grows exponentially as the stripe width increases with a constant parity number. Recent consensus favors wider stripes with a higher ratio of data chunks to parity chunks to reduce storage overhead, as evidenced in studies based on scalar codes <sup>[19, 24, 62]</sup>. However, the sub-packetization level of Clay codes, given by α = m<sup>⌈n/m⌉</sup>, represents the lower bound for optimal-access MSR codes <sup>[6]</sup>. For instance, the (20, 16) Clay code has a sub-packetization level of α = 4<sup>5</sup> = 1024.

首先, 在校验块数量不变时, MSR 码的子分包数随条带宽度呈指数增长. 近年来, 为降低存储开销, 使用数据块与校验块比例更高的宽条带已成为普遍趋势, 多项标量码研究也体现了这一点 <sup>[19, 24, 62]</sup>. 然而, Clay 码的子分包数为 α = m<sup>⌈n/m⌉</sup>, 已达到最优访问 MSR 码的下界 <sup>[6]</sup>. 例如, (20, 16) Clay 码的子分包数为 α = 4<sup>5</sup> = 1024.

Second, a larger sub-chunk size is essential for maximizing storage device bandwidth. In worst cases, as illustrated by the two parity chunks in Figure 2b, requests become random I/O operations at the sub-chunk granularity. Figure 3 illustrates the bandwidth of random reads using fio <sup>[5]</sup>, which recommends I/O request sizes of at least several hundred KB for HDDs and tens of KB for SSDs. Therefore, using small sub-chunk sizes will slow down full-node recovery, as each lost node contains chunks from stripes with fragmented repair patterns in common stripe placement strategies <sup>[64, 68]</sup>, which employ declustering techniques <sup>[37]</sup>.

其次, 为充分发挥存储设备带宽, 子块也必须足够大. 最坏情况下, 如图 2b 的两个校验块所示, 请求会变成以子块为单位的随机 I/O. 图 3 展示了 fio <sup>[5]</sup> 测得的随机读带宽, 据此建议 HDD 请求至少达到数百 KB, SSD 请求至少达到数十 KB. 因此, 较小的子块会拖慢整节点恢复: 常见条带放置策略 <sup>[64, 68]</sup> 使用去聚集技术 <sup>[37]</sup>, 一个故障节点包含来自不同条带的块, 而其中一些条带的修复模式会产生碎片化访问.

## 2.3 降级读中的 I/O 放大

Applying MSR codes in distributed object storage systems presents significant challenges due to the granularity mismatch between object and chunk sizes. Object sizes typically range from several KB to hundreds of MB <sup>[14, 38, 56]</sup>, often preventing a single object from fully occupying an entire chunk. Existing MSR-coded systems <sup>[34, 35, 56, 61]</sup> only support full-chunk reconstruction, which substantially increases I/O amplification. As shown in Figure 4, the average degraded read latency can rise by up to two orders of magnitude compared to normal reads, significantly impacting service quality.

对象与块的粒度不匹配, 给 MSR 码在分布式对象存储中的应用带来很大挑战. 对象大小通常从数 KB 到数百 MB 不等 <sup>[14, 38, 56]</sup>, 单个对象往往填不满一个块. 现有 MSR 系统 <sup>[34, 35, 56, 61]</sup> 仅支持整块重建, 因而显著放大了 I/O. 如图 4 所示, 平均降级读延迟最高可比正常读高出两个数量级, 严重影响服务质量.

Three key properties of MSR codes, which distinguish them from traditional scalar codes, contribute to these challenges:

MSR 码区别于传统标量码的三个特征, 共同造成了上述困难.

Interleaved Codeword Layout. Reconstructing at the codeword granularity, common in many scalar-coded storage systems <sup>[20, 38]</sup>, often leads to full-chunk reconstruction for MSR codes. This occurs because the hop-and-couple technique <sup>[47, 61]</sup> groups bytes from the same position across codewords into sub-chunks, causing objects spanning at least one full sub-chunk to be distributed across all codewords within the stripe.

**交错的码字布局.** 许多标量编码存储系统按码字重建 <sup>[20, 38]</sup>, 但在 MSR 码中, 这样做往往仍需重建整个块. 原因是 hop-and-couple 技术 <sup>[47, 61]</sup> 将各码字相同位置的字节归入同一子块, 因而覆盖至少一个完整子块的对象会横跨条带内所有码字.

Asymmetric Repair Patterns. Splitting objects into slices and encoding them into stripes (self-encoding <sup>[45]</sup>) can enhance read parallelism and reduce repair bandwidth. However, it is challenging to design an object layout that consistently benefits all potential failed chunks, since each object occupies only a portion of the sub-chunks within the large chunk, and the positions of helper sub-chunks vary depending on which chunk is being repaired.

**不对称的修复模式.** 将对象切片后编码为条带, 即自编码 (self-encoding) <sup>[45]</sup>, 能提高读取并行度并减少修复带宽. 但要设计一种对任意块故障都有效的对象布局十分困难: 一个对象只占大块中的部分子块, 而辅助子块的位置又随待修复块而变化.

Fragmented Access Patterns. Fragmented I/O negatively impacts storage system performance <sup>[39, 42]</sup>. The need for sequential access conflicts with MSR code designs, where each chunk contains multiple sub-chunks, but only part of them are needed for recovery, leading to unavoidable fragmented access.

**碎片化的访问模式.** 碎片化 I/O 会损害存储系统性能 <sup>[39, 42]</sup>. 顺序访问的需求与 MSR 的设计存在矛盾: 每个块包含多个子块, 恢复只需其中一部分, 因而不可避免地产生碎片化访问.

Furthermore, current production storage systems are not well-suited to support optimizations for MSR codes. First, the chunk (`e.g.`, Ceph's stripe unit <sup>[11]</sup>, HDFS's striping cell <sup>[4]</sup>, DAOS's EC cell <sup>[21]</sup>) serves as the basic operation unit, which simplifies software development but hinders the incorporation of partial-chunk reconstruction. Second, aligning I/O requests to the stripe leads to I/O amplification, a long-standing issue in Ceph <sup>[12, 16]</sup> that worsens as the chunk size increases.

此外, 现有生产存储系统也不便于支持 MSR 优化. 首先, 系统以块作为基本操作单位, 如 Ceph 的 stripe unit <sup>[11]</sup>, HDFS 的 striping cell <sup>[4]</sup>, DAOS 的 EC cell <sup>[21]</sup>. 这简化了软件开发, 却阻碍了部分块重建的引入. 其次, 将 I/O 请求对齐到条带会造成 I/O 放大. 这是 Ceph 长期存在的问题 <sup>[12, 16]</sup>, 块越大, 问题越严重.

# 3. 方案概览

Design Rationale. As discussed in Section 2.3, the main challenge in applying MSR codes to existing storage systems stems from the disparity between the distinct characteristics of MSR codes and the design principles of traditional storage systems. The key idea of this work is to separate the object layout into two parts: the storage layout, which maps the object space to the storage space, and the coding layout, which maps the object space to the coding space. This separation allows the storage layout to meet the storage system's requirements while granting the coding layout the flexibility to integrate MSR-specific optimization techniques.

**设计思路.** 如原文第 2.3 节所述, 现有存储系统应用 MSR 码的主要困难, 在于 MSR 的特性与传统存储系统的设计原则不一致. 本文的核心思路是将对象布局分成两部分: 存储布局负责从对象空间映射到存储空间, 编码布局负责从对象空间映射到编码空间. 前者满足存储系统需求, 后者则可以灵活纳入针对 MSR 的优化.

Figure 5 provides an overview of the DRBoost approach and demonstrates the roles of the two layouts in I/O processing. The storage layout indicates the location information of objects within the stripe, which is used for reading and writing storage devices. The coding layout shows how objects are aggregated into data chunks within a stripe, which are then encoded to construct parity chunks. Performing EC operations on stripes organized using the coding layout is essentially equivalent to applying linear transformations to the generator matrix while keeping the storage layout unchanged. Therefore, by abstracting the coding layout, we introduce a novel approach to optimizing MSR coding from a storage system perspective.

图 5 概括了 DRBoost, 并展示两种布局在 I/O 处理中的作用. 存储布局记录对象在条带中的位置, 供存储设备读写使用. 编码布局描述对象如何聚合到条带的数据块中, 再经编码生成校验块. 对按编码布局组织的条带执行纠删码操作, 本质上等价于保持存储布局不变, 对生成矩阵施加线性变换. 因此, 将编码布局单独抽象出来, 提供了一条从存储系统角度优化 MSR 编码的新途径.

</div>

Within this framework, coding-related operations, such as degraded reads (the focus of this paper), writes, and recovery, are initially encoded or decoded under the coding layout and then transitioned into the storage layout for accessing storage devices. In contrast, for coding-irrelevant operations, particularly normal reads, the translation between object space and coding space (including reading the coding layout) can be bypassed, allowing the original operation process to remain unchanged.

在这一框架下, 降级读 (本文重点), 写入和恢复等涉及编码的操作, 先在编码布局下编码或解码, 再转换到存储布局以访问设备. 不涉及编码的操作, 尤其是正常读, 则可跳过对象空间到编码空间的转换, 也无需读取编码布局, 保持原有操作流程.

Design Overview. To mitigate the I/O amplification issue during degraded reads in MSR-coded storage systems, we propose a new approach called DRBoost to boost degraded read performance. Targeting the three properties outlined in Section 2.3, DRBoost employs three key techniques:

**设计概览.** 为缓解 MSR 存储系统降级读中的 I/O 放大, 我们提出 DRBoost. 针对原文第 2.3 节的三个特征, 它采用三项关键技术.

DRBoost mitigates the mismatch between object sizes and chunk sizes by introducing an algorithm that reconstructs only a portion of a chunk. This is accomplished through the concept of a sub-stripe offering independent fault tolerance and is further optimized by incorporating two data reuse mechanisms. (§4.1)

DRBoost 引入只重建块的一部分的算法, 缓解对象与块的大小不匹配. 算法以具有独立容错能力的子条带为基础, 再结合两种数据复用机制进一步优化. 详见原文第 4.1 节.

DRBoost introduces a reconstruction-friendly coding layout to enhance data reuse efficiency during partial-chunk reconstruction. It proposes a basic layout unit that adapts to two data reuse mechanisms at a fine granularity, along with a tiered layout structure within the stripe to accommodate objects of varying sizes. (§4.2)

DRBoost 引入重建友好的编码布局, 提高部分块重建时的数据复用效率. 它以细粒度的基本布局单元适配两种复用机制, 并在条带内构建分层布局, 适应不同大小的对象. 详见原文第 4.2 节.

DRBoost features a fragmentation-free storage layout that optimizes access sequentiality by rearranging sub-chunks, ensuring that the sub-chunks occupied by an object within a chunk are stored contiguously. (§4.3)

DRBoost 通过重新排列子块, 让对象在一个块内占用的子块连续存放, 形成无碎片存储布局, 改善访问连续性. 详见原文第 4.3 节.

# 4. DRBoost 设计

## 4.1 利用数据复用实现高效的部分块重建

Feasibility of Partial-chunk Reconstruction. Bandwidth savings achieved by MSR codes originate from the careful design of parity-check equations, which restricts helper sub-chunks to a specific subset during full-chunk recovery. Furthermore, since each parity-check equation involves a limited number of sub-chunks, it is feasible to selectively request helper sub-chunks to reconstruct partial sub-chunks within a chunk.

**部分块重建的可行性.** MSR 码通过精心设计校验方程, 将整块恢复所需的辅助子块限制在特定子集内, 从而节省带宽. 而每个校验方程只涉及有限个子块, 因此可以有选择地请求辅助子块, 只重建块内的部分子块.

Recall that the recovery process of Clay codes involves decoding MDS stripes and performing pairwise coupling/uncoupling operations across layers. Therefore, we focus on the set of coupled sub-chunks that can be transformed into a layer of uncoupled sub-chunks, which collectively form a stripe of scalar MDS codes. We refer to this set as a sub-stripe (Note 2) due to its ability to independently tolerate faults. Like a regular stripe, data loss within the sub-stripe at any node can be reconstructed using its remaining healthy data. However, the sub-stripe differs from the regular stripe in that the data amount each node provides is not always equal.

回顾 Clay 码的恢复过程, 它需要对 MDS 条带解码, 并跨层进行成对的耦合或解耦. 因此, 我们关注这样一组耦合子块: 它们可以转换成一层未耦合子块, 后者共同构成一个标量 MDS 条带. 这组子块具有独立容错能力, 我们将其称为子条带 (注 2). 与普通条带一样, 子条带中任意节点的数据丢失后, 都可利用其中剩余的健康数据重建. 不同之处在于, 子条带中各节点提供的数据量并不总是相同.

<div class="paper-shared">

Note 2: In this paper, the term sub-stripe refers exclusively to the fault-tolerant sub-chunk set generated from a layer of uncoupled sub-chunks.

注 2: 本文的子条带专指由一层未耦合子块生成, 具有容错能力的子块集合.

</div>

Sub-stripe 1 consists of the sub-chunks required to be transformed into the uncoupled MDS stripe of layer 1. If two sub-chunks on data chunk 3 get lost, the remaining healthy sub-chunks of sub-stripe 1 can independently reconstruct lost sub-chunks. This fault-tolerant property applies to each node.

以图 6 为例, 每个子块都标注了其所属子条带的编号. 子条带 1 包含转换为第 1 层未耦合 MDS 条带所需的子块. 如果数据块 3 丢失两个子块, 子条带 1 中剩余的健康子块即可独立完成重建. 这一容错性质对每个节点都成立.

Exploiting Data Reuse. Similar to scalar MDS codes, partial-chunk reconstruction can be achieved by using all sub-stripes from the layers containing the lost object and reconstructing each sub-stripe independently. However, repair bandwidth can be further reduced by exploiting data reuse in two ways.

**利用数据复用.** 与标量 MDS 码类似, 可以取出丢失对象所在各层对应的全部子条带, 分别重建, 从而实现部分块重建. 在此基础上, 还可通过两种数据复用方式进一步减少修复带宽.

First, when multiple lost sub-chunks need to be reconstructed, if some belong to the same sub-stripe, the healthy portion of that sub-stripe can be reused as helper data. This approach is referred to as sub-stripe reuse. For example, as shown in Figure 6, two highlighted sub-chunks on data chunk 3 can be reconstructed simultaneously through sub-stripe 1, where the other highlighted healthy sub-chunks from sub-stripe 1 are reused to assist in the reconstruction.

第一, 多个待重建子块若属于同一子条带, 就可以共用该子条带的健康部分作为辅助数据, 称为子条带复用. 例如图 6 中, 数据块 3 上两个突出标出的子块可通过子条带 1 一并重建; 子条带 1 中其他突出标出的健康子块, 就同时为这两个子块提供了重建所需的数据.

Second, the healthy portion of the requested object can potentially serve as helper data for reconstruction, a concept referred to as request reuse. For instance, as shown in Figure 6, if an object is stored across the six highlighted sub-chunks in data chunks 1 to 4, no additional helper data is needed from these nodes during a degraded read (`e.g.`, when data chunk 3 is lost), since these sub-chunks already contain the data portion of the sub-stripe 1, which can be used for reconstruction.

第二, 请求对象的健康部分也可能充当重建辅助数据, 称为请求复用. 例如图 6 中, 如果一个对象分布在数据块 1 至 4 的六个突出标出的子块上, 降级读时即使数据块 3 丢失, 也无需从这些数据节点额外读取辅助数据. 因为这六个子块已包含子条带 1 的数据部分, 可以直接用于重建.

Partial-chunk Reconstruction Algorithm. The selection of sub-stripes for degraded reads affects data reuse and, consequently, repair bandwidth. However, searching for the optimal sub-stripe set requires extensive calculations and comparisons, resulting in high computational complexity.

**部分块重建算法.** 降级读时选取哪些子条带, 会影响数据复用程度, 进而影响修复带宽. 但寻找最优子条带集合需要大量计算和比较, 复杂度很高.

To address this challenge, we prioritize sub-stripe reuse over request reuse for two reasons. First, sub-stripe reuse is generally more efficient, as it ensures the deterministic reuse of all healthy sub-chunks within a sub-stripe. In contrast, request reuse depends on the request location and typically reuses fewer sub-chunks. Second, sub-stripe reuse reduces computational overhead since this approach not only enables the reuse of intermediate uncoupled sub-chunks for decoding multiple lost sub-chunks, but also eliminates the need for extensive comparisons between the number of reused sub-chunks across different sub-stripe candidates.

为此, 我们优先考虑子条带复用, 再考虑请求复用, 原因有二. 首先, 子条带复用通常更高效, 能确定地复用子条带中全部健康子块; 请求复用则取决于请求位置, 通常可复用的子块较少. 其次, 子条带复用的计算开销更低: 解码多个丢失子块时, 既可复用中间的未耦合子块, 又不必大量比较不同候选子条带能复用多少子块.

Based on these considerations, we propose a lightweight partial-chunk reconstruction algorithm containing three steps:

基于这些考虑, 我们提出以下三步组成的轻量部分块重建算法.

Step 0: Identify sub-chunks occupied by the target object, including both healthy and lost ones. As shown in Figure 7, the target object consists of 12 blue sub-chunks, with three lost ones on data chunk 3 (marked with diagonal texture). Next, count the requested but lost sub-chunks in each sub-stripe. Following the sub-stripe structure in Figure 6, the example in Figure 7 shows that sub-stripe 1 has two lost sub-chunks being requested, while sub-stripes 0, 2, and 3 each have one.

**步骤 0:** 找出目标对象占用的全部子块, 包括健康子块和丢失子块. 图 7 中, 目标对象包含 12 个蓝色子块, 其中数据块 3 上的三个子块已丢失, 用斜线标出. 接着, 统计每个子条带中既被请求又已丢失的子块数. 按图 6 的子条带结构, 图 7 中子条带 1 有两个这样的子块, 子条带 0, 2, 3 则各有一个.

Step 1: Apply sub-stripe reuse to all the sub-stripes with multiple requested but lost sub-chunks. As shown in Figure 7, sub-stripe 1 is selected based on the requested-but-lost sub-chunk count from Step 0. Here, two yellow sub-chunks on data chunk 3 are reconstructed simultaneously by decoding sub-stripe 1 using the remaining yellow sub-chunks.

**步骤 1:** 对包含多个被请求且已丢失子块的子条带, 应用子条带复用. 图 7 中, 根据步骤 0 的计数选中子条带 1. 利用其余黄色子块对该子条带解码, 即可同时重建数据块 3 上的两个黄色子块.

Step 2: For the remaining requested but lost sub-chunks that cannot be reconstructed via sub-stripe reuse, select a sub-stripe to maximize request reuse. In Figure 7, both sub-stripes 0 and 2 can reconstruct the green sub-chunk on data chunk 3. However, sub-stripe 2 is preferred for its higher reuse degree, as it allows reusing 3 healthy sub-chunks within the current read request (dotted area), whereas sub-stripe 0 offers none.

**步骤 2:** 对剩余无法通过子条带复用重建的目标丢失子块, 选择能最大化请求复用的子条带. 图 7 中, 子条带 0 和 2 都能重建数据块 3 上的绿色子块. 但应优先选子条带 2, 因为它能复用本次读请求中的三个健康子块 (点状区域), 而子条带 0 无法复用任何一个.

## 4.2 重建友好的编码布局

Basic Layout Unit. While partial-chunk reconstruction reduces I/O amplification during degraded reads, its effectiveness is limited when applied to objects stored using traditional layout schemes native to scalar codes, such as stripe layout <sup>[40]</sup> or contiguous layout <sup>[38]</sup>. This limitation arises for two main reasons: (a) each sub-stripe contains multiple sub-chunks, but objects often span only part of them, preventing full utilization of sub-stripe reuse, and (b) the healthy portions of these objects cannot serve as helper data, thereby reducing the potential for request reuse. To address these challenges, it is essential to adopt a sub-stripe layout tailored for MSR codes, which aligns objects with sub-stripes that offer independent fault tolerance.

**基本布局单元.** 部分块重建能减轻降级读的 I/O 放大, 但若对象沿用标量码的传统条带布局 <sup>[40]</sup> 或连续布局 <sup>[38]</sup>, 效果仍然有限. 原因主要有两个: (a) 一个子条带包含多个子块, 对象却往往只覆盖其中一部分, 无法充分利用子条带复用; (b) 对象的健康部分不能充当辅助数据, 限制了请求复用. 因此, 需要针对 MSR 采用子条带布局, 将对象与具有独立容错能力的子条带对齐.

However, aligning the object layout with sub-stripes is both infeasible and inefficient due to the sub-chunk overlap between sub-stripes and the asymmetric distribution of sub-chunks across chunks. As shown in Figure 6, each sub-stripe comprises two types of nodes: nodes that contribute m sub-chunks and nodes that contribute only one sub-chunk. We refer to the former as major sub-chunks, as the nodes they belong to provide more repair bandwidth, while the latter are called minor sub-chunks. Besides, each sub-chunk plays a major role in one sub-stripe, and most of them, except those that are self-coupled, play a minor role in another sub-stripe.

然而, 子条带之间存在子块重叠, 子块在各块间的分布也不对称, 因而直接按子条带对齐对象既不可行, 也不高效. 如图 6 所示, 一个子条带的节点分成两类: 一类贡献 m 个子块, 另一类只贡献一个子块. 前者提供的修复带宽更多, 我们称其子块为主要子块 (major sub-chunks); 后者称为次要子块 (minor sub-chunks). 每个子块恰好在一个子条带中充当主要子块, 而除自耦合子块外, 大多数子块还在另一个子条带中充当次要子块.

Therefore, we introduce the concept of basic layout unit, which groups major sub-chunks while excluding minor sub-chunks within a single sub-stripe, as illustrated by sub-chunks labeled with the same number in the left sub-figure of Figure 8. Since each sub-chunk assumes exactly one major role within a sub-stripe and sub-stripes cover all data sub-chunks, all basic layout units can cover the entire stripe data without duplication or omission. Furthermore, objects aligned with basic layout units naturally conform to sub-stripe reuse, enabling multiple lost sub-chunks to be reconstructed together. This alignment also optimizes request reuse to some extent, as major sub-chunks, which provide higher repair bandwidth, can serve as helper data during sub-stripe reconstruction. Lastly, the granularity of basic layout units is sufficiently fine-grained as each unit contains k sub-chunks, with 1/m of all data nodes contributing m sub-chunks each. This granularity matches the stripe data size of scalar codes with the same chunk size as the MSR codes' sub-chunk size, thereby alleviating the I/O amplification caused by increasing sub-packetization.

因此, 我们提出基本布局单元: 将一个子条带中的主要子块归为一组, 排除次要子块. 图 8 左图中, 编号相同的子块构成一个单元. 每个子块恰好在一个子条带中担任主要角色, 而子条带覆盖所有数据子块, 因此基本布局单元能不重不漏地覆盖整个条带的数据. 对象按基本单元对齐后, 自然适合子条带复用, 可同时重建多个丢失子块. 它也能在一定程度上优化请求复用, 因为贡献更多修复带宽的主要子块可以直接用作子条带重建的辅助数据. 此外, 基本单元的粒度足够细: 每个单元只有 k 个子块, 来自全部数据节点中的 1/m, 每个参与节点贡献 m 个子块. 其数据量等于块大小与 MSR 子块大小相同的标量码的条带数据量, 从而缓解子分包数增加造成的 I/O 放大.

Tiered Layout Structure. The allocation of basic layout units affects the degraded read performance of real-world objects, which vary in size and require different numbers of units. For example, sequentially assigning the first two basic layout units in Figure 8 to an object is inefficient, as it utilizes only data nodes 1 and 3 while leaving the other two data nodes unused, thereby reducing read parallelism. The imbalance in sub-chunk distribution across data nodes arises because each basic layout unit contains 1/m of all data nodes, and the selected chunks are asymmetrically distributed. Furthermore, although basic layout units are designed to prioritize the reuse of major sub-chunks, there is potential for further reuse of minor sub-chunks, especially when objects span multiple layout units.

**分层布局结构.** 实际对象大小不同, 需要的基本布局单元数量也不同, 因而单元的分配顺序会影响降级读性能. 例如, 若依次将图 8 的前两个基本单元分给同一对象, 就只会用到数据节点 1 和 3, 另外两个节点闲置, 读取并行度降低. 这种不均衡源于每个基本单元只包含 1/m 的数据节点, 且选中的块并非对称分布. 另外, 虽然基本单元优先复用主要子块, 但次要子块仍有进一步复用的空间, 尤其是对象跨越多个单元时.

The size gap between the basic layout unit and the stripe data size limits the flexibility in accommodating objects of intermediate size. To address this issue, we introduce two intermediate layout units that enhance object layout efficiency and adaptability, as shown in the right sub-figure of Figure 8:

基本布局单元与整个条带的数据量相差较大, 限制了对中等大小对象的适应性. 为此, 我们引入两个中间层级, 提高对象布局的效率和灵活性, 如图 8 右图所示.

Balanced Layout Unit: A balanced layout unit consists of m basic layout units and ensures that each data node appears exactly once, distributing data evenly across nodes. Referring to the index notation of Clay codes <sup>[61]</sup>, a basic layout unit with index z = (z<sub>0</sub>, z<sub>1</sub>, . . . , z<sub>t-1</sub>), where t = ⌈n/m⌉, contains nodes whose indices belong to the set {(z<sub>y</sub>, y) | y = 0, 1, . . . , t - 2}. Thus, to prevent duplicate data nodes, the indices of basic layout units within a balanced layout unit must differ pairwise in the first t - 1 digits. For example, the basic layout unit with index z = 1 = (0, 0, 1) includes data chunks at positions (z<sub>0</sub>, 0) = (0, 0) and (z<sub>1</sub>, 1) = (0, 1), corresponding to data chunks D1 and D3, respectively. Moreover, the basic layout units with indices z = 1 = (0, 0, 1) and z = 7 = (1, 1, 1) differ in their first two digits, indicating that they cover distinct data chunks and can therefore be combined into a balanced layout unit.

**均衡布局单元.** 一个均衡布局单元由 m 个基本布局单元组成, 每个数据节点恰好出现一次, 数据均匀分布到各节点. 沿用 Clay 码 <sup>[61]</sup> 的索引表示, 基本单元索引为 z = (z<sub>0</sub>, z<sub>1</sub>,  . . . , z<sub>t-1</sub>), 其中 t = ⌈n/m⌉; 它包含的节点索引属于集合 {(z<sub>y</sub>, y): y = 0, 1,  . . . , t - 2}. 为避免数据节点重复, 同一均衡单元内各基本单元索引的前 t - 1 位必须逐位两两不同. 例如, z = 1 = (0, 0, 1) 包含位置 (z<sub>0</sub>, 0) = (0, 0) 和 (z<sub>1</sub>, 1) = (0, 1) 的数据块, 即 D1 与 D3. z = 1 = (0, 0, 1) 与 z = 7 = (1, 1, 1) 的前两位均不同, 覆盖的数据块互不重叠, 因而可组合成一个均衡布局单元.

Reuse-optimal Layout Unit: A reuse-optimal layout unit is a set of basic layout units where reconstruction does not require additional helper data from any data node during a node failure. Reconstructing basic layout units requires minor sub-chunks in the corresponding sub-stripe, and these minor sub-chunks can be reused when the indices of two sub-stripes differ by only one specific digit among the first t - 1 digits. Thus, all basic layout units sharing the same z<sub>t-1</sub> index collectively form a reuse-optimal layout unit. For example, the basic layout units with indices z = 1 = (0, 0, 1), z = 7 = (1, 1, 1), z = 3 = (0, 1, 1), and z = 5 = (1, 0, 1) cover all basic layout units whose last digit is 1. These basic layout units can collectively form a reuse-optimal layout unit and require no additional helper data from data chunks during data reconstruction.

**最优复用布局单元.** 这是一组基本布局单元, 在一个节点故障时, 重建这组单元无需从任何数据节点额外读取辅助数据. 基本单元重建需要对应子条带中的次要子块; 当两个子条带的索引仅在前 t - 1 位中的某一位不同, 这些次要子块就可复用. 因此, 最后一位 z<sub>t-1</sub> 相同的所有基本单元共同构成最优复用布局单元. 例如, z = 1 = (0, 0, 1), z = 7 = (1, 1, 1), z = 3 = (0, 1, 1), z = 5 = (1, 0, 1) 覆盖了末位为 1 的全部基本单元. 它们可组成一个最优复用单元, 重建时无需从数据块额外读取辅助数据.

Coding Layout for Varying-sized Objects. When aggregating multiple objects into a stripe, their coding layouts are determined during the sub-chunk allocation process, which builds upon the basic layout unit and the hierarchical structure illustrated in Figure 8. Specifically, Algorithm 1 formalizes the generation of the basic layout unit allocation sequence, which determines the final coding layout. As an example, consider the requested object shown in Figure 7. Suppose this object arrives after four sub-chunks (`i.e.`, one basic layout unit) have already been allocated; it is assigned to the basic layout units 6, 2, 4, according to the allocation sequence 0, 6, 2, 4, 1, 7, 3, 5 for (6, 4) Clay codes following Algorithm 1.

**适应不同对象大小的编码布局.** 将多个对象聚合到条带中时, 编码布局由子块分配过程决定, 该过程以图 8 的基本布局单元和分层结构为基础. 算法 1 给出了基本单元分配序列的生成过程, 这一序列决定最终编码布局. 以图 7 的请求对象为例, 假设它到达前已分配四个子块, 即一个基本单元; 根据算法 1, (6, 4) Clay 码的分配序列为 0, 6, 2, 4, 1, 7, 3, 5, 因而该对象获分基本单元 6, 2, 4.

<div class="paper-shared">

Algorithm 1: Layout allocation sequence generation

算法 1: 生成布局分配序列. 输入为编码参数, 输出为基本布局单元序列 S. z 和 α 的下标区间采用左闭右开表示; 此处 α 是算法中的索引向量.

```text
Input: Code parameters / 编码参数
Output: Sequence of basic layout units S / 基本布局单元序列 S
 1: S ← []
 2: // Iterate over Reuse-optimal Layout Units / 遍历最优复用布局单元
 3: for z_(t-1) = 0, ..., m - 1 do
 4:     // Iterate over Balanced Layout Units / 遍历均衡布局单元
 5:     for z_[1,t-1) = 0, ..., m^(t-2) - 1 do
 6:         z_[1,t) ← z_[1,t-1) × m + z_(t-1)
 7:         // Iterate over Basic Layout Units / 遍历基本布局单元
 8:         for z_0 = 0, ..., m - 1 do
 9:             α_[0,t) ← (0, z_1, ..., z_(t-1))
10:             // Digit-Wise Modulo Addition / 逐位模加
11:             for y = 0, ..., t - 2 do
12:                 α_y ← (α_y + z_0) mod m
13:             S.push_back(α_[0,t))
14: return S
```

</div>

For clarity, we categorize the coding layout of aggregated objects within a stripe into two cases. First, for objects that span multiple basic layout units, the allocation sequence ensures their reconstruction-friendly properties. Successive basic layout units assigned to the same object are likely to form balanced or reuse-optimal layout units, improving load balance and reuse efficiency. Second, for objects smaller than a basic layout unit or with misaligned portions, they are continuously aggregated within one or more basic layout units to avoid internal fragmentation. Additionally, small objects are preferentially placed in a single chunk rather than being striped, which helps mitigate the impact of tail latencies.

为便于说明, 条带中聚合对象的编码布局可分为两种情况. 对跨越多个基本单元的对象, 分配序列保证了布局有利于重建: 连续分给同一对象的基本单元容易构成均衡或最优复用单元, 改善负载均衡和复用效率. 对小于基本单元的对象, 或对象未对齐的部分, 则将其连续聚合到一个或多个基本单元中, 避免内部碎片. 此外, 小对象优先放在单个块中, 而不跨块条带化, 以减轻尾延迟的影响.

## 4.3 无碎片存储布局

Fragmentation Elimination. The coding layout previously discussed offers a theoretical pathway to significantly reduce the degraded read amplification ratio. However, it comprises fragmented sub-chunks within each data node, which results in performance degradation since request splitting affects the entire I/O process. Besides, maintenance costs increase due to the need for additional metadata for object locations, given the asymmetric sub-chunk distribution across data nodes.

**消除碎片.** 上述编码布局从理论上显著降低了降级读放大率, 但各数据节点内的子块分布零散, 请求拆分会影响整个 I/O 流程, 导致性能下降. 子块在数据节点间不对称分布, 还需要额外记录对象位置元数据, 增加维护成本.

To address this issue, we propose a fragmentation elimination method that generates storage layout schemes based on the position of basic layout units and their allocation sequence, as illustrated in Figure 9. First, sub-chunks within each data node are rearranged so that those belonging to the same basic layout unit are placed contiguously, allowing the basic layout unit to be viewed as a whole. Second, the basic layout units within each data node are adjusted to preserve their sequential order of allocation, as specified in Algorithm 1, ensuring that consecutively allocated basic layout units are placed adjacent to each other. Consequently, objects are continuously stored within each data node. For example, the requested object shown in Figure 7 is assigned to basic layout units 6, 2, 4, as described in Section 4.2. Its storage layout can be obtained from the right sub-figure of Figure 9, with its sub-chunks placed contiguously on the corresponding chunks.

为此, 我们根据基本单元的位置及分配序列生成存储布局, 消除碎片, 如图 9 所示. 首先, 重排每个数据节点内的子块, 让属于同一基本单元的子块连续存放, 使该单元可以作为整体处理. 然后, 按算法 1 的分配顺序调整节点内各基本单元的位置, 保证连续分配的单元也相邻存放. 这样, 对象在每个节点内都是连续的. 例如, 按原文第 4.2 节, 图 7 的请求对象获分基本单元 6, 2, 4. 图 9 右图给出了对应存储布局, 对象子块在各自所在的块中连续排列.

Coding-storage Mapping. The reconstruction-friendly coding layout aims to reduce the theoretical amplification ratio, while the fragmentation-free storage layout is designed to meet the requirements of storage systems. To enable the coexistence of these two layouts, we propose a coding-storage mapping method that allows objects to occupy fragmented sub-chunks in the coding address space while being stored continuously in the storage address space.

**编码与存储之间的映射.** 重建友好的编码布局旨在降低理论放大率, 无碎片存储布局则服务于存储系统的实际需求. 为让两者共存, 我们引入编码-存储映射, 允许对象在编码地址空间中占用分散的子块, 同时在存储地址空间中连续存放.

We employ mapping tables to facilitate bidirectional translation between the coding and storage address spaces, with the sub-chunk serving as the basic translation unit. A common concern is the risk of increased storage costs of mapping tables, as objects may vary in size and have different coding and storage layouts across all stripes. However, the mapping relationship is deterministic because layout sequences determine the sub-chunk order, making it independent of the sizes or order of aggregated objects. Consequently, mapping tables can be shared among all stripes with the same (n, k) configuration, minimizing additional storage overhead. For instance, all (20, 16) Clay stripes can share a single mapping table, whose size is 2 × SizeTableEntry × α × k = 2 × 4B × 1024 × 16 = 128KB, where each entry is stored as a 4-byte integer.

我们以子块为基本转换单位, 用映射表实现编码地址空间与存储地址空间之间的双向转换. 一个常见顾虑是映射表的存储成本: 对象大小不同, 各条带中的编码布局与存储布局也可能不同. 但布局序列决定了子块顺序, 因而映射关系是确定的, 与聚合对象的大小及先后顺序无关. 所有相同 (n, k) 配置的条带都可共享映射表, 将额外开销降至很低. 例如, 全部 (20, 16) Clay 条带可共用一个表, 大小为 2 × SizeTableEntry × α × k = 2 × 4 B × 1024 × 16 = 128 KB, 每个条目是一个 4 字节整数.

Metadata Management and I/O Workflow. The proposed mapping strategy enables the coexistence of coding and storage layouts. However, the way these two layouts are stored and when translations are triggered are crucial for both implementation convenience and performance efficiency.

**元数据管理与 I/O 流程.** 映射策略使编码布局与存储布局能够共存. 但两种布局如何保存, 何时触发转换, 对实现难度和运行效率都很关键.

In our metadata design, storage addresses, rather than coding addresses, are used to locate objects, which simplifies the metadata structure and ensures that normal read operations do not require additional address translation. Since aggregating objects into large stripes (`e.g.`, Facebook F4 <sup>[38]</sup>), regardless of the coding scheme, requires necessary metadata such as offset and length to locate objects, we argue that DRBoost's MSR-specific design does not introduce additional object metadata: its deterministic sub-chunk allocation enables locating both coding and storage layout units directly from an object's offset and length, even for small or misaligned objects.

我们的元数据用存储地址而非编码地址定位对象, 简化了结构, 也使正常读无需额外地址转换. 无论采用哪种编码方案, 将对象聚合为大条带的系统, 如 Facebook F4 <sup>[38]</sup>, 都需要偏移和长度等元数据来定位对象. 因此, DRBoost 针对 MSR 的设计不引入额外对象元数据: 确定性的子块分配方式使我们能直接根据对象偏移和长度, 定位编码与存储布局中的单元, 对小对象和未对齐对象也同样适用.

We isolate unnecessary translation from normal I/O operations to avoid modifications to the I/O stack and minimize adverse effects on system performance. For write operations, objects aligned with balanced layout units can be easily split into k equal-sized slices. However, for misaligned portions of objects, the data allocation across nodes must be computed based on the layout allocation sequence. During degraded reads, the process begins with translating the request's storage address into the corresponding coding address, followed by partial-chunk reconstruction. The expanded coding addresses, along with the necessary helper data for reconstruction, are then translated back into storage addresses to interact with storage devices.

我们让正常 I/O 绕过不必要的转换, 避免修改 I/O 栈, 尽量减少对系统性能的影响. 写入时, 按均衡布局单元对齐的对象可直接分成 k 个等大切片; 未对齐部分则需按布局分配序列计算各节点的数据量. 降级读先将请求的存储地址转换为编码地址, 再执行部分块重建. 随后, 将扩展后的编码地址, 包括重建所需辅助数据的地址, 转回存储地址, 供存储设备访问.

In summary, the reconstruction-friendly coding layout discussed in Section 4.2 is implemented as an extension of the coding module rather than as an additional abstraction layer, thereby minimizing its impact on the storage system.

因此, 原文第 4.2 节的重建友好编码布局是编码模块的扩展, 而非新增的抽象层, 从而尽量减少对存储系统的影响.

Discussion. In the DRBoost design, we employ deterministic strategies to minimize metadata overhead associated with address translation and to reduce potential computational costs. However, we recognize that adaptive methods, such as dynamically selecting objects for aggregation and customizing coding layouts for each stripe, have not been fully explored, and these approaches could offer additional performance benefits. We consider it a promising direction for future research.

**讨论.** DRBoost 采用确定性策略, 以减少地址转换的元数据开销和潜在计算成本. 不过, 动态选择待聚合对象, 为每个条带定制编码布局等自适应方法尚未充分探索, 可能带来进一步的性能收益, 是值得继续研究的方向.

# 5. 实现

We implement DRBoost as a prototype system in C++ (§5.1) and integrate it into Ceph with essential modifications (§5.2).

我们用 C++ 实现 DRBoost 原型 (原文第 5.1 节), 并对 Ceph 做必要修改, 将其接入其中 (原文第 5.2 节).

## 5.1 原型系统

To perform MSR coding operations, we use Intel's Intelligent Storage Acceleration Library (ISA-L) <sup>[22]</sup>. Some MSR-specific implementation details are as follows:

我们使用 Intel Intelligent Storage Acceleration Library (ISA-L) <sup>[22]</sup> 执行 MSR 编码操作. 以下介绍与 MSR 有关的实现细节.

Degraded Read Trigger. In traditional RS-coded storage systems, it is common to send k data chunk requests along with ∆ redundant chunk requests and decode the first k that arrive <sup>[30, 45]</sup>, thereby incorporating degraded read handling into the normal read process. However, this approach is unsuitable for MSR codes due to the asymmetric nature of helper data used to reconstruct different chunks, as well as the significant bandwidth overhead associated with the redundant large chunks. As a result, monitoring mechanisms, such as heartbeat packets, are essential in MSR-coded storage systems to determine whether a degraded read should be performed based on the status of the relevant data nodes.

**降级读触发.** 传统 RS 存储系统常同时发出 k 个数据块请求和 Δ 个冗余块请求, 对最先到达的 k 个块解码 <sup>[30, 45]</sup>, 将降级读处理融入正常读取流程. 但 MSR 重建不同块所需的辅助数据并不对称, 冗余大块还会产生大量带宽开销, 因而不适合这种方式. MSR 存储系统需要心跳等监控机制, 根据相关数据节点的状态决定是否执行降级读.

Two-Phase Write. Due to the large chunk and stripe sizes in MSR codes, creating or updating an object can result in significant I/O amplification if the parity chunks are updated accordingly. To mitigate this, we employ a two-phase write scheme when aggregating objects into the stripe, similar to the approach in previous work <sup>[23]</sup>. In this scheme, objects are first written to a replicated-type pool, with one replica directly placed in its intended location within the stripe of the erasurecoded pool. After all object data within a stripe is written and the corresponding parity chunks are then computed and stored on the disks, the objects are logically transitioned to the erasure-coded pool. Additionally, object updates are treated as new writes, and partially valid stripes are periodically recycled and merged when the system is idle.

**两阶段写入.** MSR 的块和条带较大, 创建或更新对象时若同步更新校验块, 会产生严重的 I/O 放大. 为此, 我们在将对象聚合到条带时采用两阶段写入, 类似已有工作 <sup>[23]</sup>. 先将对象写入副本池, 其中一个副本直接放到纠删码池条带中的预定位置. 等到条带全部对象数据写完, 并计算校验块写入磁盘后, 再在逻辑上将对象转入纠删码池. 对象更新视为新写入, 仅部分数据有效的条带则在系统空闲时定期回收并合并.

## 5.2 接入 Ceph

Ceph <sup>[1, 63]</sup> is a widely-used, open-source distributed object storage system that supports erasure coding for fault tolerance. The concept of the sub-chunk was first introduced when integrating Clay codes into Ceph <sup>[59]</sup>. However, several implementation details remain incompatible with MSR codes, posing challenges for MSR-specific optimizations: (a) Ceph aligns requests to the stripe by default, leading to I/O amplification when only a portion of the stripe is accessed <sup>[12]</sup>; (b) Ceph recovers full chunks by default, lacking support for partial-chunk reconstruction, which also results in I/O amplification; and (c) Ceph performs online encoding when writing objects by default, which aligns small objects with large MSR stripes, leading to significant I/O amplification.

Ceph <sup>[1, 63]</sup> 是广泛使用的开源分布式对象存储系统, 支持纠删码容错. 接入 Clay 码时, Ceph 首次引入了子块概念 <sup>[59]</sup>. 但若干实现细节仍不适配 MSR, 给专项优化带来困难: (a) Ceph 默认将请求对齐到条带, 即使只访问条带一部分也会产生 I/O 放大 <sup>[12]</sup>; (b) 默认恢复整块, 不支持部分块重建, 同样会放大 I/O; (c) 默认在对象写入时在线编码, 将小对象对齐到较大的 MSR 条带, 导致严重的 I/O 放大.

We provide a brief overview of the modifications made to Ceph and Rados <sup>[65]</sup> to enable the use of DRBoost.

下面简要介绍为支持 DRBoost, 我们对 Ceph 和 Rados <sup>[65]</sup> 所做的修改.

Librados API. Librados provides read and write functions that only support full-stripe operations. However, an object may occupy only part of the stripe and the coding layout may divide objects into multiple slices. To enable partial-stripe reads, we implement a new interface that supports batch reading of multiple slices within a stripe. For partial-stripe writes, we introduce an interface that allows appending data smaller than the chunk size in a single operation.

Librados API. Librados 的读写函数只支持整条带操作. 但对象可能只占条带的一部分, 编码布局又可能将其分为多个切片. 为支持部分条带读取, 我们实现了一个新接口, 可批量读取条带内多个切片. 对部分条带写入, 则提供可在单次操作中追加小于一个块的数据的接口.

EC Module. Ceph's EC modules use chunk indices as inputs to calculate helper data positions and perform decoding. However, partial-chunk reconstruction requires more detailed positional information. Currently, we implement the reconstruction logic within the prototype system, using Ceph solely for data access. Integrating the partial-chunk reconstruction algorithm into Ceph is a potential direction for future work.

**EC 模块.** Ceph 的 EC 模块以块索引为输入, 计算辅助数据位置并解码. 部分块重建却需要更细的位置描述. 目前, 重建逻辑实现在原型系统中, Ceph 仅用于数据访问. 将部分块重建算法纳入 Ceph 的 EC 模块, 仍是未来的工作方向.

EC Backend. Instead of aligning read requests with the stripe size, we first specify the exact target location using a slice map. These positions are then added to the ECSubRead structure <sup>[9]</sup> for inter-node communication. Next, we ensure that the data nodes correctly process the sub-read operations based on ECSubRead packets. Finally, we modify the callback function to properly assemble the slices into the target object.

**EC 后端.** 我们不再把读取请求对齐到整个条带, 而先用切片映射明确目标位置, 再将位置加入节点间通信使用的 ECSubRead 结构 <sup>[9]</sup>. 随后, 确保数据节点根据 ECSubRead 数据包正确处理子读操作. 最后, 修改回调函数, 将切片正确组装成目标对象.

## 6.3 各项技术的作用

In this section, we present the evaluation results of the individual techniques' contributions to the read performance.

本节分别评估各项技术对读取性能的贡献.

Degraded Reads. Figure 12a illustrates the impact of individual techniques on the latency of degraded reads. The partial-chunk reconstruction algorithm is essential in addressing the granularity mismatch issue between object and chunk sizes, particularly for small objects, and the speedup can reach up to ×72.3. This improvement arises because only lost sub-chunks containing the target object, rather than the entire chunk, are reconstructed. The coding layout provides significant speedups across objects of all sizes, with speedup factors ranging from ×2.95 to ×4.90. This is due to DRBoost's alignment of object layouts with sub-stripes and its awareness of request reuse, which reduces repair bandwidth. Notably, the coding layout's consistent positive impact across all object sizes demonstrates the effectiveness of the tiered structure of layout units, since the reuse degree improves progressively with the object size. Finally, although the storage layout is primarily designed for normal reads, it also offers some benefits for degraded reads. By storing objects continuously within each data node, the access to their healthy parts is optimized, resulting in speedups of up to ×1.28.

**降级读.** 图 12a 展示各项技术对降级读延迟的影响. 部分块重建算法解决了对象与块的粒度不匹配, 对小对象尤其重要, 最高加速 72.3 倍. 原因是它只重建包含目标对象的丢失子块, 无需重建整块. 编码布局对各种对象大小均有显著收益, 加速比为 2.95 至 4.90: 将对象与子条带对齐, 并考虑请求复用, 能减少修复带宽. 这种跨对象大小的一致收益, 也说明分层布局单元有效, 因为复用程度会随对象大小逐步提高. 最后, 存储布局虽然主要为正常读设计, 也能改善降级读. 对象在每个节点内连续存放, 优化了对健康部分的访问, 最高带来 1.28 倍加速.

Normal Reads. We also assess whether designs optimized for degraded reads negatively impact normal read performance, given that objects are split and rearranged to align with the coding layout. Figure 12b presents the results. As expected, partial reconstruction does not affect normal read performance because it does not modify the object layout. However, the coding layout does negatively impact the normal read performance. The latency increases by ×1.25 to ×1.38 due to the splitting of objects into multiple slices on each device, which causes fragmentation and leads to random I/O access delays. Nevertheless, the storage layout mitigates this issue by maintaining a fragmentation-free layout on each device and allowing normal read requests to bypass sub-chunk translation. Consequently, DRBoost enhances degraded read performance without compromising normal read performance.

**正常读.** 对象为适配编码布局而拆分和重排, 因此我们也检查这些降级读优化是否损害正常读, 结果见图 12b. 如预期, 部分块重建不改变对象布局, 不影响正常读. 但单独采用编码布局, 会让正常读延迟变为原来的 1.25 至 1.38 倍: 对象在每个设备上分成多个切片, 产生碎片和随机 I/O 延迟. 存储布局则消除了这一问题, 既保持设备内连续存放, 又让正常读绕过子块转换. 因此, DRBoost 能改善降级读, 同时保持正常读性能.

Impact of (n, k) Settings. We conduct a sensitivity study on the impact of the (n, k) settings on DRBoost by varying k and m. For example, a (104, 100) Clay code would require a 16 EB chunk size, which far exceeds the capacity of a typical storage device. Therefore, we fix m=4 and vary k from 8 to 24, as the (28, 24) configuration represents the largest possible setup supported by Ceph due to its U_INT_MAX/2 (2 GiB) stripe size limit, with the sub-chunk size set to 4KB.

(n, k) 配置的影响. 我们改变 k 和 m, 研究编码配置对 DRBoost 的影响. 现有 MSR 码在条带过宽时, 块大小会大到无法实际部署. 例如, (104, 100) Clay 码需要 16 EB 的块, 远超普通设备容量. 因此, 我们固定 m = 4, 将 k 从 8 增至 24, 子块设为 4 KB. Ceph 将条带大小限制为 U_INT_MAX/2, 即 2 GiB, 所以 (28, 24) 已是这一条件下支持的最大配置.

However, DRBoost shows a significantly smaller latency increase, leading to greater speedups compared to baseline. The greater speedups can be attributed to the increased sub-packetization level of MSR codes as k grows, which results in larger chunk sizes for a fixed sub-chunk size. Thus, for given workloads, the object sizes become relatively smaller compared to the chunk size, allowing DRBoost to reduce read amplification more effectively.

图 13 展示 k 对 Ceph 接入 DRBoost 前后降级读性能的影响. k 增大时, 重建所需辅助数据更多, 两者延迟均上升, 但 DRBoost 的增幅明显更小, 相对加速效果因而更大. 原因是 k 增大会提高 MSR 子分包数; 子块大小固定时, 块随之变大. 对给定负载而言, 对象相对块更小, DRBoost 就能更有效地减少读放大.

## 6.5 与标量码比较

While MSR codes typically minimize repair traffic during full-chunk recovery, their performance advantages can be limited by I/O amplification during degraded reads. Therefore, we evaluate whether DRBoost can preserve the performance advantages of MSR codes compared to RS codes and LRCs in degraded read scenarios. In these scalar codes <sup>[20, 33, 38, 67, 70]</sup>, objects are grouped into fixed-size chunks, which are then encoded into stripes. We conduct a comparison with scalar codes using the default (20, 16) setting and a chunk size of 16 MB. Specifically, the LRC scheme includes two local and two global parity chunks. To ensure fairness, we enable partial-chunk reconstruction at 4 KB granularity for both RS codes and LRCs in our Ceph-based environment.

MSR 码通常能最小化整块恢复的修复流量, 但降级读的 I/O 放大可能削弱这一优势. 因此, 我们评估 DRBoost 能否让 MSR 在降级读中保持相对 RS 和 LRC 的性能优势. 这些标量码 <sup>[20, 33, 38, 67, 70]</sup> 将对象聚合为固定大小的块, 再编码为条带. 比较采用默认 (20, 16) 配置和 16 MB 块. LRC 包含两个局部校验块和两个全局校验块. 为公平起见, 在基于 Ceph 的环境中, 我们为 RS 和 LRC 都实现了 4 KB 粒度的部分块重建.

As shown in Figure 16, DRBoost delivers performance comparable to that of LRCs when handling a large number of small 4 KB objects, as observed in the Alibaba traces. This results from DRBoost's current implementation, which reconstructs data at the sub-chunk granularity of 16 KB to simplify design. Consequently, reconstructing objects smaller than the sub-chunk size incurs I/O amplification. In other cases, DRBoost improves degraded read latency by a factor of ×1.62 to ×3.12 compared to RS codes, and by ×1.52 to ×1.80 compared to LRC codes.

如图 16 所示, 对 Ali 轨迹中大量 4 KB 小对象, DRBoost 与 LRC 性能接近. 原因是当前 DRBoost 为简化设计, 按 16 KB 子块粒度重建, 对小于子块的对象仍存在 I/O 放大. 其他情况下, DRBoost 的降级读加速比相对 RS 为 1.62 至 3.12, 相对 LRC 为 1.52 至 1.80.

## 6.6 元数据内存开销

DRBoost adopts a deterministic mapping strategy, where a single mapping table is shared across all stripes with the same coding scheme and (n, k) configuration. This design keeps the memory overhead very low. Specifically, the mapping table is implemented using two integer pointer arrays in C++, resulting in an exact memory overhead of 128 KB for the (20, 16) Clay codes evaluated.

DRBoost 使用确定性映射, 相同编码方案与 (n, k) 配置的所有条带共享一个表, 内存开销很低. 具体实现是在 C++ 中使用两个整数指针数组, 本文评估的 (20, 16) Clay 码恰好占用 128 KB.

Since the mapping table size is determined by the sub-packetization level and stripe width, even in large-scale clusters with tens of different coding schemes or configurations, the total memory overhead remains in the range of tens of MBs, which is a negligible cost compared to the tens or hundreds of GBs of RAM typically available on each server.

映射表大小由子分包数和条带宽度决定. 即使大规模集群包含数十种编码方案或配置, 总开销仍只是数十 MB. 相对于服务器通常拥有的数十乃至数百 GB 内存, 这点成本可以忽略.

# 7. 对其他 MSR 码的适用性

In this section, we discuss the applicability of DRBoost to general MSR codes.

本节讨论 DRBoost 对一般 MSR 码的适用性.

First, DRBoost's focus on MSR codes with a coupled-layer structure does not substantially limit its applicability. As proven in prior work <sup>[6]</sup>, any disk-read-optimal MSR code that achieves the lower bound on the sub-packetization level inherently has a coupled-layer structure. Therefore, DRBoost applies to a broad class of advanced MSR codes, including those proposed in <sup>[31, 32, 50, 60, 66]</sup>. As discussed in Section 2, a higher sub-packetization level leads to larger chunk sizes, which in turn exacerbate the I/O amplification problem. We argue that minimizing the sub-packetization level is an emerging trend in recent MSR code designs, making DRBoost well-suited for these emerging approaches.

首先, DRBoost 针对耦合层结构的设计, 并未明显限制其适用范围. 既有工作 <sup>[6]</sup> 已证明, 任何达到子分包数下界的磁盘读取最优 MSR 码, 都具有耦合层结构. 因而 DRBoost 适用于一大类先进 MSR 码, 包括文献 <sup>[31, 32, 50, 60, 66]</sup> 的方案. 如原文第 2 节所述, 更高的子分包数会增大块, 加重 I/O 放大. 我们认为, 尽量减少子分包数已成为近期 MSR 设计的趋势, DRBoost 因而也适合这些新方案.

Second, for MSR codes without a coupled-layer structure, the current design of DRBoost is not directly applicable. Nevertheless, high-level optimization principles of DRBoost, which include reconstructing at the granularity of sub-stripes, aligning the object layout with sub-stripes, and eliminating storage fragmentation, may still be beneficial. In such cases, the specific algorithms would require adaptation to accommodate the structural characteristics of the target MSR code.

其次, 当前 DRBoost 不能直接用于非耦合层结构的 MSR 码. 但其优化原则仍可能有价值: 按子条带重建, 将对象布局与子条带对齐, 消除存储碎片. 对这类编码, 需要根据目标码的具体结构调整算法.

# 8. 相关工作

Design of MSR Codes. Several designs of MSR codes have emerged since their inception <sup>[13]</sup>, but early schemes pose challenges when applied to storage systems. F-MSR codes <sup>[18]</sup> incur high decoding costs when accessing healthy data. Product-Matrix MSR codes <sup>[46, 48]</sup> are restricted to specific (n, k) parameters, making them unsuitable for storage-efficient scenarios with high data-to-parity ratios. Butterfly codes <sup>[41]</sup>, optimized for XOR operations, support only two parities and require a high sub-packetization level. Although ZigZag codes <sup>[57]</sup> and HashTag codes <sup>[28]</sup> support arbitrary (n, k) parameters, they have drawbacks: ZigZag codes are non-explicit and require programmatic searching for coefficients, while HashTag codes need a large field size and lack efficient software support. Currently, Clay codes <sup>[61]</sup> represent the state-of-the-art MSR design, offering flexible configuration, a low field size, and the optimal sub-packetization level. Therefore, we focus on applying our designs to Clay codes.

**MSR 码设计.** 自 MSR 码提出 <sup>[13]</sup> 以来, 已出现多种设计, 但早期方案应用到存储系统时各有困难. F-MSR <sup>[18]</sup> 访问健康数据也要付出较高解码成本. Product-Matrix MSR <sup>[46, 48]</sup> 只支持特定 (n, k), 不适合数据块与校验块比例较高, 追求存储效率的场景. 针对 XOR 优化的 Butterfly 码 <sup>[41]</sup> 仅支持两个校验块, 且需要较高子分包数. ZigZag <sup>[57]</sup> 和 HashTag <sup>[28]</sup> 虽支持任意 (n, k), 仍各有不足: ZigZag 不是显式构造, 需用程序搜索系数; HashTag 需要较大的有限域, 缺乏高效软件支持. Clay <sup>[61]</sup> 则兼具灵活配置, 较小有限域和最优子分包数, 因此本文主要将设计应用于 Clay 码.

System-level Optimizations of MSR Codes. While MSR codes offer theoretically optimal repair bandwidth, their implementation in distributed storage systems presents several system-level challenges. Therefore, several approaches have been developed to optimize full-chunk recovery. ParaRC <sup>[34]</sup> distributes and parallelizes the repair of sub-chunks across all healthy nodes, alleviating potential repair bottlenecks. G-Clay <sup>[35]</sup> reorganizes sub-chunk positions to enhance the overall continuity of sub-chunks, thereby improving disk read efficiency. Geometric Partitioning <sup>[56]</sup> targets large-object scenarios by maintaining multiple groups of stripes where sub-chunk sizes follow a geometric sequence and partitioning each object into a series of chunks, which reduces I/O amplification. In contrast, DRBoost is the first approach to propose and optimize the partial-chunk reconstruction, significantly improving the versatility and scalability of MSR codes.

**MSR 码的系统级优化.** MSR 码理论上具有最优修复带宽, 但在分布式存储中实现仍有系统层面的挑战, 因而出现了多种整块恢复优化. ParaRC <sup>[34]</sup> 将子块修复分散到全部健康节点并行执行, 缓解修复瓶颈. G-Clay <sup>[35]</sup> 重排子块位置, 改善整体连续性, 提高磁盘读取效率. Geometric Partitioning <sup>[56]</sup> 面向大对象, 维护多组子块大小按等比数列变化的条带, 将每个对象划分为一系列块, 从而减少 I/O 放大. DRBoost 则首次提出并优化部分块重建, 显著增强 MSR 码的通用性和扩展能力.

# 9. 结论

The mismatch between object and MSR codes' chunk sizes significantly exacerbates I/O amplification during degraded reads, as it necessitates reconstructing entire chunks to retrieve a single object. This paper proposes DRBoost, an approach to boosting the performance of degraded reads in MSR-coded storage clusters. DRBoost optimizes degraded reads by introducing the concept of data reuse, incorporating both a reconstruction-friendly coding layout and a fragmentation-free storage layout. Experimental results show that DRBoost effectively reduces the latency of degraded reads by one to two orders of magnitude.

对象大小与 MSR 块大小不匹配, 使降级读为了取回一个对象也要重建整个块, 严重加剧 I/O 放大. 本文提出 DRBoost, 提升 MSR 编码存储集群的降级读性能. 它引入数据复用, 结合重建友好的编码布局与无碎片存储布局优化降级读. 实验表明, DRBoost 能将降级读延迟降低一到两个数量级.
